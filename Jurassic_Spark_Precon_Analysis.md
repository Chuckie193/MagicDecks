# Jurassic Spark — Gruul Dragons & Dinosaurs Commander (From-Scratch Build)

Commander: Xenagos, God of Revels ({3}{R}{G}, Red/Green)

## Overview

- **Strategy**: There is no Gruul legend that pays off *both* Dragons and Dinosaurs — the two tribes share exactly one trait, which is that they are enormous. So the commander pays off **enormous**. Xenagos costs 5, is an indestructible God that survives the board wipes which would eat a Klauth or a Dragonlord Atarka, and at the beginning of combat doubles a creature's power and hands it haste. Ramp into a fatty, point Xenagos at it, and whichever opponent is furthest ahead takes 16–24 in the air. The deck's engine underneath that is an **enrage loop**: Marauding Raptor, Terror of the Peaks, Scourge of Valkas and Warstorm Surge all deal damage to *your own* creatures on demand, which turns Ripjaw Raptor into card draw, Wrathful Raptors into removal and Apex Altisaur into a machine gun.
- **Intended for**: **Multiplayer**, explicitly. Both Etalis scale with opponent count, Dragonlord Atarka / Bogardan Hellkite / Balefire Dragon / Apex Altisaur / Ryusei all hit the whole table, Spire Garden only enters untapped when you have two or more opponents, and Chandra's Ignition on a 12/12 Ghalta is frequently lethal to three players at once. Sixteen fliers means you are never stuck behind three ground boards.
- **Card pool**: **The entire Magic card pool** — built to request without reference to the collection. This is a shopping list, not a collection build. ~**$650** for the 83 non-basic cards at cheapest-printing NM; ~**$327** if you skip the five luxury slots (see *Cost Analysis*).
- **Base deck**: none. See *Precon Analysis* for whether buying and merging precons beats buying singles — the short answer is that it does, but only with the two decks releasing 2 October 2026.

## Lore

The reptiles never agreed on who inherited the world. The ones with wings took the sky and the ones with teeth took the ground, and for sixty million years that was a workable truce. Then something with hooves and a grin walked between them and pointed at a rival, and suggested — reasonably, warmly, with real enthusiasm — that the truce had gone on quite long enough.

*It only takes one god of revels to start an extinction event.*

## Decklist (100 cards)

### Commander (1)
- **Xenagos, God of Revels** — {3}{R}{G} 6/5 indestructible. **At the beginning of combat on your turn, another target creature you control gets +X/+X where X is its power, and gains haste.** Doubling power *and* granting haste means every fatty is a threat the turn it lands, and it does this every turn for free. He is only a creature while your devotion to red and green is 7+, which matters less than you would think — the trigger works regardless.

### Creatures (35)

**Ramp and cost reduction (8)**
- **Sakura-Tribe Elder** — Chump block, sacrifice in response, fetch a land. Never a dead card.
- **Drover of the Mighty** — {1}{G} mana dork that is a 3/3 whenever you control a Dinosaur.
- **Otepec Huntmaster** — Dinosaurs cost {1} less and gain haste. The haste matters: it makes a top-decked Ghalta immediately lethal.
- **Dragonspeaker Shaman** — Dragons cost {2} less. Turns Dragonlord Atarka into a 7-drop.
- **Topiary Stomper** — {2}{G} ramp that becomes a 4/4 Dinosaur once you hit seven lands.
- **Wayward Swordtooth** — Extra land drops every turn, and a 5/5 once you have city's blessing.
- **Goreclaw, Terror of Qal Sisma** — Creatures with power 4+ cost {2} less, and pump the team with trample when they attack. With 25 power-4 creatures this is the single best cost reducer in the deck.
- **Marauding Raptor** — Dinosaurs cost {2} less, and it deals 2 damage to each creature that enters. **That damage is a feature**: it triggers every enrage card in the deck. Be aware it will kill your own Drover and Otepec.

**Dragons (17)**
- **Terror of the Peaks** — Every creature that enters throws damage at a target. The best card in the deck and the main removal engine.
- **Scourge of Valkas** — Same effect on Dragons only, but it scales with how many Dragons you already have.
- **Thunderbreak Regent** — 4/4 flier for four that taxes 3 life every time an opponent targets your Dragons.
- **Glorybringer** — Exert to deal 4 to a creature an opponent controls. Haste, so it works the turn it lands.
- **Goldspan Dragon** — Treasures on attack and on being targeted, and each Treasure taps for two.
- **Savage Ventmaw** — Attack for six mana every combat. With Xenagos's haste it pays for itself immediately.
- **Hellkite Courser** — **Attacks → put Xenagos onto the battlefield tapped and attacking, for free.** Returns him to the command zone at end of combat, so you dodge commander tax entirely.
- **Klauth, Unrivaled Ancient** — 7/7 that adds red mana equal to the greatest power among your attackers at beginning of combat. Casts a second fatty mid-combat.
- **Old Gnawbone** — Treasure equal to combat damage dealt. One connection funds the rest of the game.
- **Ryusei, the Falling Star** — 5 damage split among all creatures **without flying** when it dies. Your Dragons survive; the pod's ground boards do not.
- **Lathliss, Dragon Queen** — A 5/5 Dragon every time a non-token Dragon enters. The primary rebuild engine after a wipe.
- **Bogardan Hellkite** — **Flash**, then 5 damage divided as you choose. Instant-speed multi-kill at end of an opponent's turn.
- **Balefire Dragon** — Combat damage to a player wipes that player's entire board. In a pod that is a one-sided wrath every turn it connects.
- **Utvara Hellkite** — Each attacking Dragon makes a 6/6 Dragon. Wins the game two turns after it lands if unanswered.
- **Hellkite Tyrant** — Steals every artifact from a player it damages, and **wins the game outright** if you control 20. A live alt-win in artifact-heavy pods.
- **Dragonlord Atarka** — 8/8 flying trample, and on entry deals 5 damage divided among any number of target creatures and planeswalkers **your opponents control**. Multi-opponent removal stapled to a finisher.
- **Atarka, World Render** — All attacking Dragons gain double strike. With Xenagos that is a quadrupled fatty.

**Dinosaurs (9)**
- **Ripjaw Raptor** — Enrage: draw a card whenever it is dealt damage. Feeds off Marauding Raptor.
- **Regisaur Alpha** — Haste anthem for the team plus a free 3/3 Dinosaur.
- **Trumpeting Carnosaur** — A 7/6 that also reads "discard this: destroy target creature with mana value 3 or less" — removal that is never a dead card.
- **Etali, Primal Storm** — **Attacks → exile the top card of each player's library, cast any of them free.** Scales directly with the number of opponents.
- **Etali, Primal Conqueror** — On entry, each player exiles three cards and you cast one free. Transforms into a Phyrexian finisher.
- **Apex Altisaur** — Nine mana, and on entry it fights every creature your opponents control. With enrage it keeps fighting. This ends pods.
- **Ghalta, Primal Hunger** — Costs {10}{G}{G} minus the total power of your creatures. Routinely a 12/12 for four or five mana.
- **Ghalta, Stampede Tyrant** — Attacks → drop any number of creatures with total power 12 or less from hand, tapped and attacking.
- **Wrathful Raptors** — Enrage payoff: whenever a Dinosaur you control is dealt damage, it deals that much to a creature an opponent controls.

**Both tribes (1)**
- **Bonehoard Dracosaur** — Legendary **Dinosaur Dragon**. Every upkeep exiles two cards, giving you a Treasure and a 3/1 flying Dragon token on top of free card advantage. The single most on-theme card in Magic for this deck.

### Enchantments (8)
- **Garruk's Uprising** — Trample for the team plus a card whenever a power-4 creature enters. Trample is what converts a doubled Ghalta into a dead opponent instead of a chump block.
- **Elemental Bond** — A card for every power-3 creature that enters.
- **Guardian Project** — A card for every non-token creature that enters. In a singleton deck this never misses.
- **Rhythm of the Wild** — Your creatures cannot be countered and get riot (haste or a +1/+1 counter). Redundancy for the haste plan.
- **Dragon Tempest** — Haste for fliers, and every Dragon that enters throws damage equal to your flier count.
- **Warstorm Surge** — Every creature that enters deals damage equal to its power to any target. Also an enrage enabler on your own board.
- **Sarkhan's Unsealing** — Whenever you cast a creature with power 4+, it deals 4 damage to a creature or planeswalker; power 7+ adds 4 to a player. Free value on essentially every spell in the deck.
- **Sneak Attack** — {R}: put a creature from hand onto the battlefield with haste, sacrifice at end of turn. Cheats the top end out at instant speed; with an ETB fatty (Dragonlord Atarka, Apex Altisaur, either Etali) the sacrifice barely costs you anything.

### Artifacts & Mana (4)
- **Sol Ring** — Fastest ramp in the format.
- **Arcane Signet** — Two-mana fixing on colour identity.
- **Quicksilver Amulet** — {4}: put a creature from hand onto the battlefield at instant speed. Dodges counterspells and sorcery-speed removal entirely.
- **The Great Henge** — Costs {8}{G}{G} minus the greatest power among your creatures, so with any fatty out it is a 2–4 mana artifact that makes mana, gains life, draws a card per creature and puts counters on everything.

### Instants (4)
- **Chaos Warp** — The only answer in Gruul to an opposing enchantment or a resolved commander at instant speed.
- **Beast Within** — Destroys **any** permanent. The 3/3 is a fair price.
- **Heroic Intervention** — Hexproof and indestructible for your whole board. The single best protection card in these colours.
- **Return of the Wildspeaker** — Draw cards equal to your biggest creature's power, at instant speed. Regularly draws 8+.

### Sorceries (10)
- **Nature's Lore** — Fetches a Forest **untapped**, and can grab Stomping Ground.
- **Three Visits** — Functional reprint of the above.
- **Cultivate** — Ramp and fixing that guarantees the next land drop.
- **Kodama's Reach** — Functional reprint of the above.
- **Green Sun's Zenith** — Tutors any green creature onto the battlefield. Finds Goreclaw early or Ghalta late.
- **Decimate** — Four-for-one, but it needs all four target types on the table. In a pod that condition is almost always met.
- **Hull Breach** — Destroys an artifact **and** an enchantment, or two of either. Sorcery speed, so plan it on your own turn.
- **Vandalblast** — Overloaded, destroys every artifact your opponents control and leaves yours alone.
- **Chandra's Ignition** — Target creature you control deals damage equal to its power to **each other creature and each opponent**. On a doubled Ghalta this is a one-sided wrath *and* lethal to the table.
- **Rishkar's Expertise** — Draw cards equal to your biggest creature's power, then cast a spell with mana value 5 or less free.

### Planeswalkers (1)
- **Xenagos, the Reveler** — +1 makes mana equal to your creature count or a 2/2 Satyr; ultimate is an emblem that doubles damage. Ramp and a body on a permanent that soaks attacks.

### Lands (37)
- **Command Tower**, **Stomping Ground**, **Cinder Glade**, **Rootbound Crag**, **Karplusan Forest**, **Copperline Gorge**, **Sheltered Thicket**, **Game Trail**, **Rockfall Vale**, **Mossfire Valley**, **Gruul Turf** — the dual base.
- **Spire Garden** — Enters untapped **only if you have two or more opponents**. A multiplayer-only dual, which is exactly this deck.
- **Wooded Foothills** — Fetches either half of the mana base and thins.
- **Path of Ancestry** — Scry on every Dragon and Dinosaur, which is most of the deck.
- **Haven of the Spirit Dragon** — Returns a Dragon from the graveyard to hand.
- **Kessig Wolf Run** — Trample and a pump for a huge creature. A second Xenagos effect on a land.
- **Rogue's Passage** — Makes a doubled fatty unblockable for the kill.
- **Yavimaya, Cradle of Growth** — Every land on the table taps for green, including your Mountains.
- **Boseiju, Who Endures** — Channel to destroy an artifact, enchantment or problem land from hand; otherwise a Forest.
- **Ancient Tomb** — Two colourless for one land. Powers out a turn-two rock.
- **9 Mountain**, **8 Forest**

## Key Synergies

- **Xenagos + trample + Rogue's Passage** — Doubling power does nothing against a 1/1 chump block. Garruk's Uprising, Goreclaw, Kessig Wolf Run and Rogue's Passage are the four cards that convert doubled power into dead opponents. Never durdle without one of them available.
- **Hellkite Courser → free Xenagos** — Courser attacks, Xenagos comes in tapped and attacking from the **command zone**, doubles something, and goes back to the zone at end of combat with **no commander tax**. This is the deck's best individual turn.
- **The enrage loop** — Marauding Raptor damages every creature that enters. That draws off Ripjaw Raptor, shoots off Wrathful Raptors, and re-triggers Apex Altisaur's fight. Terror of the Peaks and Warstorm Surge can aim at your own creatures deliberately to fire the same triggers.
- **Ghalta + Chandra's Ignition** — Ghalta costs almost nothing once the board is set, Xenagos doubles it to 24, and Ignition deals 24 to every other creature and every opponent.
- **Sneak Attack / Quicksilver Amulet + ETB fatties** — Dragonlord Atarka, Apex Altisaur and both Etalis all do their work on entry, so cheating them in and losing them at end of turn is nearly free.
- **Klauth + Savage Ventmaw** — Both make mana at beginning of combat / on attack, which casts the second and third threat in the same turn.

## Precon Analysis

### Existing precons — do not buy any of them

Priced against the cards from each that would actually go into this deck:

| Precon | Cards usable here | Those cards as singles | Sealed price | You overpay by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Draconic Rage (AFR, Gruul dragons) | 22 | $28.74 | ~$125 | **$96** |
| Veloci-Ramp-Tor (LCI, Naya dinos) | 15 | $52.06 | ~$135 | **$83** |
| Temur Roar (Tarkir: Dragonstorm) | 11 | $13.08 | ~$71 | **$58** |

All three are out of print, so the sealed price is now driven by two or three chase cards (Klauth, Xenagos, Akroma's Will) rather than by the deck's contents. Draconic Rage is the closest thing to a Gruul dragon precon that exists and it *still* only yields $29 of relevant cards for $125. **Buy the ten cards you want from all three as singles instead** — Klauth $10, Xenagos $10, Apex Altisaur $9, Chandra's Ignition $8, Wrathful Raptors $6 — roughly $50 total.

### Upcoming precons — these are the ones to buy

The **Foundations Commander decks** release **2 October 2026** alongside Reality Fracture, at $29.99 / **£25.95** each:

- **Reign of Dragons** — mono-red, led by **Lathliss, Dragon Queen**. Dragon typal with Treasure ramp.
- **Tramplesaurus Rex** — mono-green, led by **Ghalta, Primal Hunger**. Big Dinosaurs, power-matters.

Decklists are **not yet published** (the reveal was scheduled for 18 September 2026 and nothing has appeared; Scryfall has no card data for the set). All cards are confirmed reprints.

**This is the rare case where buying and merging precons genuinely works.** A mono-red deck and a mono-green deck merge into Gruul with **zero off-colour waste** — which is precisely why Draconic Rage's 22 usable cards and Veloci-Ramp-Tor's 15 look so poor by comparison. Both face commanders are already in this 99, and the shells almost certainly contain Garruk's Uprising, Goreclaw, Sol Ring, Arcane Signet and a spread of the cheaper Dragons and Dinosaurs above.

Caveat: these are explicitly **beginner products** at a lower power level than standard precons, so expect low singles value and no expensive reprints. Buy them as a cheap chassis, not for value. Check the decklists on release before committing.

Also checked so you do not have to: **Reality Fracture Commander**'s five decks are white / black / red / colourless (Akroma, Avacyn, Memnarch, Omnath, Niv-Mizzet). Nothing Gruul, nothing Dragon or Dinosaur. Ignore it.

¹ Ancient Tomb ($127), The Great Henge ($62), Old Gnawbone ($53), Boseiju ($50), Green Sun's Zenith ($31) — $323 of the total sits in five cards. Substitutes: Mountain, Colossal Majesty, Cavern-Hoard Dragon, Forest, Hunting Wilds (~$3 combined).
² also cutting both large Ghaltas, Etali Primal Conqueror, Terror of the Peaks, Wooded Foothills, Yavimaya, Balefire Dragon, Bonehoard Dracosaur, Utvara Hellkite, Guardian Project, Goldspan Dragon.

**The direct answer: precons are not a cheaper way to buy this deck's cards.** 52 of the 83 named cards cost $3 or less and total **$44 combined** — the cheap majority of the deck is nearly free as singles, so paying $30–135 per box to harvest it is bad value. Precons only win when the precon *is* most of your deck, which is true of the two Foundations decks and of nothing else currently available.

**Recommended path**: wait until 2 October, buy Reign of Dragons and Tramplesaurus Rex (~£52), merge them into one Gruul pile, cut the ~40 weakest cards, and add the expensive half of this list on top. That reaches the ~$327 build for about $210 all-in, and you will have seen the real decklists before spending anything.

## Short Mulligan and Play Notes

- **Keep** any hand with two lands and two ramp pieces, or three lands and one cost reducer. **Ship** hands with four-plus lands and no acceleration; the top end is uncastable on seven lands alone without a reducer.
- Goreclaw, Dragonspeaker Shaman and Otepec Huntmaster are the cards that make the deck function. Prioritise them over a raw mana rock.
- **Do not cast Xenagos into an open pod on turn five** unless you can use him immediately. He is indestructible but he still eats a Chaos Warp or a Beast Within, and he is more valuable on the turn you have a threat to double.
- Hold Heroic Intervention. This deck loses to exactly one thing — a board wipe on a developed board — and one mana of protection is the difference.
- In a four-player pod, aim the first doubled attack at whoever is furthest ahead, not whoever is weakest. You get more attacks; you do not get more first strikes.
- Marauding Raptor kills your own Drover of the Mighty and Otepec Huntmaster. Sequence them onto the battlefield *before* the Raptor.
