# Byrke, Long Ear of the Law — Selesnya +1/+1 Counters Commander

Commander: Byrke, Long Ear of the Law (4GW, Green/White)

## Overview
- **Strategy**: Byrke distributes +1/+1 counters on entering creatures and then doubles all counters on attacking creatures that already have them — turning a modest buff into an exponential arms race over just a few attack steps. V2 sharpens the deck for 1v1: eight high-CMC or P/T-modifier cards are swapped for instant-speed interaction, deathtouch deterrents, a proliferate engine (Surge Conductor), and card-draw infrastructure (Skullclamp). The deck floods the board with low-cost creatures, spreads counters through creatures and Sagas, then curves into Byrke to close the game with overwhelmingly large attackers.
- **Intended for**: Single opponent (1v1 duel)

---

## Lore

Byrke doesn't just enforce the law — she doubles it. Every ally enters loaded; every swing multiplies the verdict. In her one-on-one courtroom, crimes *accumulate*, and the sentence is always *lethal*.

## Decklist (100 cards)

### Commander (1)
- **Byrke, Long Ear of the Law** — The engine of the entire deck. Her ETB distributes +1/+1 counters on up to two creatures, and her attack trigger doubles all counters on every attacking creature that carries one — turning incremental growth into exponential threats in just a few combat steps.

### Creatures (29)
- **Llanowar Elves** — Turn-1 mana accelerant that allows Byrke to land on turn 5 instead of 6. Frees up subsequent turns to deploy threats.
- **Hinterland Sanctifier** — 1-mana 1/2 that triggers life gain whenever any creature enters; fuels Ajani's Pridemate and Twinblade Paladin every time you rebuild the board.
- **Gilded Goose** — Produces a Food token on ETB and taps for any colour once you sacrifice food. Flexible ramp and a body for Byrke's counter.
- **Ajani's Pridemate** — Gets +1/+1 whenever you gain life; with Hinterland Sanctifier, Dazzling Angel, and Prosperous Innkeeper all triggering on creature ETBs, it grows several counters per turn almost automatically.
- **Action News Crew** — 2-mana 2/2 with vigilance that can be discarded as a channel ability ([6], Discard: put a +1/+1 counter on each creature you control and draw a card) — a late-game mana sink that refreshes the hand and distributes counters for Byrke's doubling.
- **Burrowguard Mentor** — Power and toughness equal to the number of creatures you control, with trample. Scales immediately with the wide board this deck builds and becomes enormous after Byrke's doubling trigger.
- **Finneas, Ace Archer** — Vigilance and reach; puts a +1/+1 counter on each token and Rabbit you control whenever it attacks, then draws a card if your creatures' total power reaches 10 or more — consistent counter distribution and card draw in one.
- **Frog Butler** — 1/1 Frog Spirit with deathtouch; taps for one mana of any colour, and can gain reach for {2}. Replaces a weaker land-fetcher with a mana dork that doubles as a deathtouch combat threat — opponents cannot safely attack into it, and Byrke's counter (2/2 deathtouch) makes blocking it catastrophic.
- **Druid of the Cowl** — Reliable turn-2 mana dork that taps for Green, hitting Byrke one turn earlier and freeing mana for protection spells.
- **Helpful Hunter** — 1/1 that draws a card on ETB. Cheap, replaces itself, and gives Byrke a body to put a counter on.
- **Prosperous Innkeeper** — Creates a Treasure on ETB; gains 1 life each time another creature enters. Both effects accelerate into Byrke and grow the life-gain sub-theme simultaneously.
- **Bulwark Ox** — 2/2 for 1W with Saddle 1; whenever it attacks while saddled, puts a +1/+1 counter on any target creature (Byrke doubles it next swing). More importantly, its sacrifice ability gives every creature with any counter hexproof and indestructible until end of turn — a one-card answer to board wipes and targeted removal that protects the entire counter-loaded army.
- **Wildwood Scourge** — 0/0 Hydra that enters with X counters and gains a +1/+1 counter whenever any other non-Hydra creature you control gets one. Byrke's doubling trigger puts counters on the Scourge too, building a separate enormous threat.
- **Arasta of the Endless Web** — 3/5 enchantment creature with reach; punishes opponents who cast instants or sorceries by creating 1/2 Green Spider tokens with reach — excellent blocker that creates blockers, discouraging burn and interaction.
- **Tireless Provisioner** — Landfall triggers create Food or Treasure tokens, providing life gain or mana fixing every time you play a land, synergising with the life-gain sub-theme.
- **Thornweald Archer** — 2/1 Elf Archer with reach and deathtouch. A defensive wall that kills any creature that attacks into it, including large ground threats. After Byrke places a counter (3/2 deathtouch), one doubling makes it a 5/4 deathtouch with reach that attacks or blocks for free.
- **Twinblade Paladin** — Gets +1/+1 whenever you gain life, and gains double strike once you're at 25 life or more. The life-gain engine can grow this creature significantly, and double strike + Byrke's counter-doubling creates lethal damage quickly.
- **Dazzling Angel** — 2/3 flyer that gains you 1 life whenever another creature you control enters; stacks with every ETB in the deck to feed Ajani's Pridemate and Twinblade Paladin.
- **Patrolling Peacemaker** — Artifact creature that enters with two +1/+1 counters on it (Byrke doubles both on the next attack); whenever an opponent commits a crime — targeting anything you control, countering your spells, targeting cards in your graveyard — you proliferate. In 1v1 an opponent using any targeted removal or interaction triggers this, steadily adding a counter to every permanent that already has one.
- **Felidar Savior** — 2/3 Lifelink; ETB puts +1/+1 counters on up to two target creatures. One of the cleanest ways to pre-load counters so Byrke's attack trigger can double them.
- **Solemn Simulacrum** — Fetches a basic land on ETB and draws a card when it dies; bridges the gap to Byrke while providing card advantage. Byrke can place a counter on this to start stacking.
- **Toski, Bearer of Secrets** — 1/1 Squirrel, indestructible, can't be countered. Draws a card whenever any creature you control deals combat damage to a player — the card advantage engine that keeps the hand full in a long game.
- **Pileated Provisioner** — 3/4 Bird with flying; ETB puts a +1/+1 counter on a non-flying creature you control, combining with Byrke's ability to pre-load the largest ground threat.
- **Serra Redeemer** — 4/3 Angel with flying; whenever *another* creature you control with power 2 or less enters the battlefield, it immediately gets two +1/+1 counters. This triggers on nearly every creature in the deck (all the 1/1 tokens, every mana dork, every cheap ETB creature). Those counters persist and Byrke doubles them the next attack step — Serra Redeemer is the single best counter-loader in the 99.
- **Jazal Goldmane** — 4/4 legendary Cat with first strike; activated ([3WW]: attacking creatures get +X/+X where X is the number of attacking creatures) turns a wide alpha strike into a lethal wave, especially after Byrke has distributed counters.
- **Tajuru Pathwarden** — 5/4 Elf Warrior with vigilance and trample; a sturdy ground presence that attacks and blocks in the same turn. Trample ensures Byrke's doubled counters translate to damage through tough blockers.
- **End-Raze Forerunners** — 7/7 Boar with vigilance, trample, and haste; ETB grants all other creatures +2/+2, vigilance, and trample until EOT. A game-ending top-end threat.
- **Prideful Parent** — 2/2 Cat with vigilance; ETB creates a 1/1 White Cat token. Provides two bodies for Byrke to distribute counters to, and the token can carry its own counters.
- **Dwynen's Elite** — 2/2 Elf; ETB creates a 1/1 Green Elf Warrior token if you control another Elf (Llanowar Elves or Druid of the Cowl). Two bodies for two counters from Byrke.

### Artifacts & Mana (10)
- **Sol Ring** — The quintessential Commander ramp piece; enables turn-2 three-drops or a turn-4 Byrke.
- **Arcane Signet** — Taps for Green or White; ensures colour consistency on every early turn.
- **Fellwar Stone** — Taps for any colour your opponent produces, effectively any colour in 1v1. Smooth two-mana ramp.
- **The Ozolith** — When a creature with counters leaves the battlefield, its counters move here; at the start of combat, you can move all stored counters to a single creature. Protects the counter investment from removal and creates a monstrous attacker out of nothing.
- **Skullclamp** — Equipped creature gets -1/+0; whenever the equipped creature dies, draw two cards. The best equipment-based card draw in the format. Equip to any 1/1 token (Rabbits from Hop to It or Carrot Cake, Cats from Prideful Parent, Elf Warriors from Dwynen's Elite) — the token draws 2 cards when it dies in combat, and pairs naturally with Hammer of Nazahn's auto-attach trigger.
- **Surge Conductor** — 3/2 Artifact Creature — Robot for {3}; whenever another nontoken artifact you control enters, proliferate (add a counter of each kind already on any number of permanents). Every mana rock cast (Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Fellwar Stone), every Equipment played, every Carrot Cake ETB — each triggers a free proliferate, stacking counters on every counter-bearing permanent simultaneously. Particularly devastating alongside Karn's Bastion and Patrolling Peacemaker's own proliferate.
- **Carrot Cake** — 2-mana Artifact — Food; when this enters AND when you sacrifice it, creates a 1/1 White Rabbit token and scry 1. Sacrificing it also gains 3 life. Four effects in two triggers: two Rabbit tokens for Finneas' counter-loading attack trigger, two scry 1s for library filtering, 3 life for the life-gain sub-theme, and its ETB triggers Surge Conductor's proliferate since it is itself a nontoken artifact entering.
- **Air Shoes (Swiftfoot Boots)** — Equip 1; grants hexproof and haste. Protect Byrke from targeted removal and give her haste to immediately double counters the turn she enters.
- **Power Sneakers (Lightning Greaves)** — Equip 0; grants haste and shroud. Free equip makes this the fastest protection for Byrke, though shroud means you can't target her with your own spells afterward.
- **Piko Piko Hammer (Hammer of Nazahn)** — 4W legendary Equipment; equipped creature gets +2/+1 and indestructible; whenever it or another Equipment enters, you may attach that Equipment for free. Enables a one-card Equipment toolbox by auto-attaching everything you search out with Open the Armory — including Skullclamp, which Hammer then attaches for free the turn it enters.

### Enchantments (8)
- **Resourceful Defense** — When a permanent with counters leaves the battlefield, move those counters elsewhere; [4W] to move counters between permanents as a sorcery. Works with The Ozolith to redirect counter investments and rescue them from removal.
- **Wolfwillow Haven** — Aura on a land that taps for G; inexpensive Green mana fixing and ramp that helps cast the two-Green finishers.
- **New Horizons** — Enchants a land to tap for G and W; also puts a +1/+1 counter on a creature when it enters, giving Byrke's doubling something to multiply immediately.
- **Beastmaster Ascension** — Each creature that attacks adds a quest counter; at 7 counters, all creatures you control get +5/+5. In a deck that attacks every turn with multiple creatures, this can reach threshold in 2–3 turns and instantly closes the game combined with Byrke's doubling.
- **Hunter's Talent** — Enchantment Class; Level 1: target creature you control fights another. Level 2 ([1G]): attacking creatures get +1/+0 and trample each attack (note: direct P/T modification, not a counter — Byrke cannot double it, but trample is the key payoff, ensuring Byrke's doubled counters convert to player damage through any blocker). Level 3 ([3G]): draw a card at each end step if you control a creature with power 4 or more. Provides removal, permanent trample delivery, and card draw across three upgrade thresholds.
- **Fall of Gil-galad** — {1G} Enchantment Saga; Chapter I: scry 2 (filter the library); Chapter II: put two +1/+1 counters on target creature you control (real counters — Byrke doubles them next attack); Chapter III: until end of turn, target creature gains "when this creature dies, draw two cards," then it fights up to one other target creature. One card provides library filtering, counter-loading for Byrke's doubling, removal by fighting, and draw on the creature's death.
- **Pacifism** — Taps and prevents attacks/blocks for 1W; cheap white spot removal that is particularly effective in 1v1 since the opponent cannot redirect the locked creature's value.
- **Prayer of Binding** — 3W enchantment with flash; exiles a nonland permanent until this enchantment leaves and gains 2 life. Flash timing makes this a surprise removal and life gain engine in the same card.

### Instants (9)
- **Swords to Plowshares** — Exile a creature for W; the cleanest and most mana-efficient removal spell in White. No opponent creature survives this.
- **Snakeskin Veil** — G instant; puts a permanent +1/+1 counter on target creature AND grants hexproof until EOT. The counter stays after the turn ends, meaning Byrke can double it in the next attack step. Use it reactively to save a counter-loaded creature from targeted removal while simultaneously adding to its counter total.
- **Adamant Will** — W instant; target creature gets +2/+2 and indestructible until EOT. Blocks lethal combat math or protects a key creature from board wipes.
- **Repel Calamity** — 1W instant; destroys a creature with power or toughness 4 or greater. Highly relevant in 1v1 where large commanders often attack alone.
- **Generous Gift** — 2W instant; destroys any permanent (opponent gets a 3/3 Elephant). The most flexible removal in the deck, hitting enchantments, artifacts, planeswalkers, and lands.
- **Rabbit Response** — 2WW instant; all creatures you control get +2/+1 until EOT; if you control a Rabbit, scry 2 afterward. A combat trick that can swing damage races and refines the top of your library.
- **Bite Down** — 1G instant; target creature you control deals damage equal to its power to a target creature or planeswalker an opponent controls. Scales with Byrke's counter-doubling — a massive creature fights almost anything.
- **Elspeth's Smite** — {W} instant; deals 3 damage to target attacking or blocking creature; if that creature would die this turn, exile it instead. Instant-speed removal for only 1 mana — handles most attackers and commanders, with exile to stop recursion. Works at instant speed unlike the sorcery it replaced.
- **Reprieve** — {1W} instant; return target spell to its owner's hand, then draw a card. Hard tempo play against targeted removal aimed at Byrke or counter-loaded threats, replacing itself with a draw while resetting the opponent's spell.

### Sorceries (7)
- **Open the Armory** — 1W sorcery; tutors any Aura or Equipment. Primarily fetches Hammer of Nazahn, Swiftfoot Boots, or Skullclamp depending on what the situation demands.
- **Hop to It** — 2W sorcery; creates three 1/1 White Rabbit tokens. Three bodies for Byrke to distribute counters across, and Finneas specifically buffs Rabbits and tokens when attacking. Each also feeds Skullclamp for card draw.
- **Biogenic Upgrade** — 4GG sorcery; distribute 3 +1/+1 counters among one to three creatures, then double counters on each target. Can load three creatures before Byrke attacks and doubles them again in combat.
- **Felling Blow** — 2G sorcery; put a +1/+1 counter on a target creature you control, then that creature fights a creature an opponent controls. Removes a problem blocker while pre-loading a counter for Byrke's doubling trigger.
- **Fumigate** — 3WW sorcery; destroys all creatures and gains 1 life per creature destroyed. The reset button for when the board gets out of hand; the life gain often puts you above Twinblade Paladin's double-strike threshold.
- **Wear Down** — 1G sorcery with Gift; destroys a target artifact or enchantment (Gift: opponent draws, you destroy two). Flexible non-creature removal that can clear blockers or problem enchantments.
- **Rootcast Apprenticeship** — 4-mana Green sorcery; choose three effects (repeatable): put two +1/+1 counters on target creature, copy a token you control, give an opponent a 1/1 Squirrel, or make an opponent sacrifice a nontoken artifact. Choosing "two counters" three times loads a single creature with six permanent +1/+1 counters — Byrke then doubles those to twelve on the next attack step.

### Lands (36)
- **Command Tower** — Taps for Green or White with no downside. Best dual land in Commander.
- **Path of Ancestry** — Taps for Green or White; ETB scrying when it produces mana for a spell that shares a creature type with your commander (Rabbit, Soldier).
- **Oran-Rief, the Vastwood** — Taps for Green; taps to put +1/+1 on each green creature that entered this turn. Distributes free counters for Byrke to double on the next attack.
- **Lupinflower Village** — Taps for colorless or for GW (ETB tapped). The deck's only GW dual land outside of Command Tower; critical for hitting both colours on turn 2.
- **Blossoming Sands** — Enters tapped, taps for G or W, gains 1 life on ETB. Life gain feeds Twinblade Paladin and Ajani's Pridemate.
- **Evolving Wilds** — Fetches any basic land tapped; thins the deck and ensures colour access.
- **Fabled Passage** — Fetches a basic land and untaps it if you control four or more lands; better than Evolving Wilds in the mid-to-late game.
- **Karn's Bastion** — Taps for colorless; can tap and pay [4] to Proliferate, adding a counter to every permanent that already has one. Multiplies Byrke's doubling between attacks; also benefits from Surge Conductor's proliferate chains.
- **Exotic Orchard** — Taps for any colour your opponent can produce; effectively GW in 1v1.
- **Secluded Steppe** — Cycling for W if drawn in surplus; effectively converts to a card when behind.
- **Uncharted Haven** — ETB tapped; choose a basic land type, taps for that colour permanently. Name Forest or Plains to fill in whichever colour is shorter.
- **Buried Ruin** — Taps for colourless; sacrifice it to return any artifact from your graveyard to hand. Recovers The Ozolith, Skullclamp, or a destroyed Equipment without spending a card slot on recursion.
- **Reliquary Tower** — No maximum hand size and taps for colourless. With Toski and Skullclamp drawing cards aggressively, hand size becomes a real ceiling — this removes it.
- **Study Hall** — Enters tapped; taps for G or W, and you can untap it by discarding a card. Provides mana flexibility and a discard outlet in rare situations.
- **Terramorphic Expanse** — Fetch any basic land tapped; effectively a second Evolving Wilds for more consistent colour fixing in the early turns.
- **Plains (x10)** — White basics for Swords to Plowshares, White creatures, and equipment costs.
- **Forest (x11)** — Green basics for Llanowar Elves, Green spells, and ramp costs.

## Key Synergies
- **Serra Redeemer + Small Creature ETBs + Byrke**: Every time any creature with power 2 or less enters (Llanowar Elves, Hinterland Sanctifier, all 1/1 tokens from Hop to It / Carrot Cake / Prideful Parent / Dwynen's Elite), Serra Redeemer puts two +1/+1 counters on it immediately. A 1/1 entering with Serra Redeemer in play becomes a 3/3 before Byrke swings — then Byrke doubles it to 6/6 on the attack. A board refill with three cheap ETBs can generate 10+ counters in one turn.
- **Surge Conductor + Artifact ETBs**: Surge Conductor proliferates whenever any nontoken artifact enters. The deck runs 10 artifacts (Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Fellwar Stone, The Ozolith, Skullclamp, Carrot Cake, Piko Piko Hammer, Air Shoes, Power Sneakers, Surge Conductor itself). Every artifact deployed after Surge Conductor fires adds a counter to every counter-bearing permanent simultaneously — Patrolling Peacemaker, Wildwood Scourge, and any Byrke-loaded creatures all grow without a spell being cast.
- **Skullclamp + 1/1 Tokens (Hop to It / Carrot Cake / Prideful Parent / Dwynen's Elite / Finneas)**: Equip Skullclamp to a 1/1 token for {1}; the token becomes 0/1 and draws 2 cards when it dies in combat or is sacrificed. With multiple token-generators, this turns empty board states into full hands in a single turn cycle. Tokens that Finneas loads with a +1/+1 counter become 1/1 → 0/1 under Skullclamp; if The Ozolith is in play, the counter transfers before Skullclamp draws 2.
- **Carrot Cake + Finneas + Byrke**: Carrot Cake ETBs create a Rabbit token and scry 1; sacrificing Carrot Cake creates another Rabbit and scry 1 plus 3 life. Each Rabbit triggers Finneas's counter-loading attack, and Byrke doubles those counters next swing. Carrot Cake also triggers Surge Conductor's proliferate on entry. One artifact generates two tokens, two scry 1s, 3 life, and a proliferate for 2 mana.
- **Fall of Gil-galad → Byrke**: Chapter I scries 2 (filter); Chapter II loads a creature with two real +1/+1 counters → Byrke doubles to 4 on attack; Chapter III gives that creature "draw 2 when it dies" and then it fights a threat. One Saga provides scry, two counters for Byrke's engine, removal, and draw in three turns of value.
- **Byrke + Felidar Savior + Finneas**: Felidar Savior pre-loads two creatures with +1/+1 counters; Byrke doubles them the following attack step. Finneas adds more counters on tokens and Rabbits during the same attack. Together they can create creatures with 12–20+ counters by mid-game.
- **Byrke + The Ozolith + Resourceful Defense**: When a counter-loaded creature dies, The Ozolith absorbs its counters. At the start of combat, move them onto any creature — Byrke then doubles them in the same attack phase, turning removal into an advantage.
- **Rootcast Apprenticeship → Byrke**: Cast Rootcast Apprenticeship choosing "put two +1/+1 counters" three times on the same creature. It enters combat with six counters; Byrke doubles that to twelve in the same attack step. Combined with Biogenic Upgrade (which distributes and then doubles its own counters), two sorceries can create a 20+ power attacker from almost nothing.
- **Bulwark Ox sacrifice + Board Wipe**: When an opponent taps out for a board wipe, sacrifice Bulwark Ox in response. Every creature you control that has any counter gains hexproof and indestructible until end of turn — the board wipe hits nothing. Since Byrke's ETB loads counters on two creatures, at minimum the most important bodies survive every sweeper.

## Short Mulligan and Play Notes

**Keep hands that have**: 2 lands + a mana rock or dork, OR 3 lands + a low-cost creature with draw (Helpful Hunter) or a token-generator. Scry aggressively.

**Early game (Turns 1–3)**:
- Turn 1: Sol Ring, Llanowar Elves, Skullclamp (hold for tokens), Wolfwillow Haven.
- Turn 2: Arcane Signet, Fellwar Stone, Frog Butler (deathtouch mana dork — great deterrent), Druid of the Cowl, or Carrot Cake (two Rabbits across two triggers + scry, triggers Surge Conductor).
- Turn 3: Surge Conductor if you have a second artifact coming soon. Establish life-gain bodies (Hinterland Sanctifier). Equip Lightning Greaves if Byrke is coming next turn.

**Mid game (Turns 4–6)**:
- Deploy Byrke as early as turn 5 with two mana accelerants. Immediately equip Air Shoes (Swiftfoot Boots) or Power Sneakers (Lightning Greaves).
- Use Byrke's ETB to counter Felidar Savior and another creature, then attack to double — this creates 4/4+ attackers from 2/2 bodies in a single turn.
- Build toward Finneas for passive counter-spreading, and Toski for sustained card draw.
- Hold 1W or 2 open for Reprieve/Elspeth's Smite to protect Byrke on the stack or answer a large attacker at instant speed.

**Late game (Turns 7+)**:
- Beastmaster Ascension at 7 quest counters + Jazal Goldmane's activated ability is a frequent win condition.
- End-Raze Forerunners as a haste-enabled surprise attack gives the whole team trample and vigilance.
- Rootcast Apprenticeship + Byrke's doubling can create a 12-counter attacker out of nothing.

**Key interactions to remember**:
- Lightning Greaves grants shroud — move it off Byrke before targeting her with your own spells.
- The Ozolith absorbs counters passively; move them at the start of combat before Byrke attacks to maximize doubling.
- Hammer of Nazahn auto-attaches Equipment; play Skullclamp after Hammer is out to attach it for free.
- Carrot Cake's sacrifice trigger happens when you pay 2+tap+sacrifice — it creates the Rabbit and scry 1 then, plus the life gain.
- Surge Conductor's proliferate is "choose any number of permanents/players"; don't forget to add counters to Karn's Bastion, Ozolith, or Patrolling Peacemaker's existing counters.

## Why These Choices

- **V2 philosophy — cut P/T modifiers, add interaction**: Every card removed in V2 was either a direct P/T modifier Byrke cannot double (Joraga Invocation), a win-more piece that does nothing when behind (Colossification), or a sorcery-speed answer that's too slow for 1v1 (Rabid Bite, Dewdrop Cure). Every card added is either instant-speed (Elspeth's Smite, Reprieve) or generates compounding value (Surge Conductor, Skullclamp, Fall of Gil-galad).
- **Deathtouch as deterrence**: Thornweald Archer and Frog Butler both have deathtouch. In 1v1, this is a near-hard lock on ground attacks — opponents cannot profitably attack into a deathtouch creature. Once Byrke doubles their counters, they become large deathtouch threats that dominate combat.
- **Skullclamp as the draw engine**: The deck generates 1/1 tokens from six sources (Hop to It, Carrot Cake, Prideful Parent, Dwynen's Elite, Arasta — if opponent casts spells, Rootcast Apprenticeship). Skullclamp converts each one into 2 cards for {1} equip. This solves the previous draft's card draw ceiling in the mid-to-late game.
- **Surge Conductor as persistent proliferate**: With 10 artifacts in the deck, Surge Conductor can proliferate 3–5 times per game naturally without dedicating any spell slots. It adds counters to everything at once — particularly valuable for Patrolling Peacemaker's counter stack, Wildwood Scourge, and Ajani's Pridemate.
- **Low average CMC for a 6-mana commander**: Byrke herself costs 6, so the 99 skews aggressively toward 1–3 CMC cards (46 of 63 non-land cards at CMC ≤ 3). This ensures the board is established before Byrke arrives and she's never the whole plan.

## Analysis

**Strengths**:
- Exponential threat scaling: Byrke's doubling means one attack step can multiply a 4/4 into a 16/16+ over two turns.
- Instant-speed interaction: Elspeth's Smite, Reprieve, Snakeskin Veil, Repel Calamity, and Prayer of Binding give the deck meaningful responses without passing priority disadvantageously.
- Passive proliferate engine: Surge Conductor fires on every artifact ETB with no additional mana investment, growing all counter-bearing permanents automatically.
- Deathtouch board control: Two deathtouch creatures (Thornweald Archer, Frog Butler) plus Bite Down and Fall of Gil-galad's fight chapter make attacking into this board expensive.

**Weaknesses / Missing Staples**:
- **Counterspell protection**: No blue in the colour identity means Byrke is vulnerable to targeted exile — rely entirely on Air Shoes, Power Sneakers, Snakeskin Veil, Adamant Will, and Reprieve.
- **Enchantment/artifact removal is limited**: Only Wear Down, Generous Gift, and Prayer of Binding handle non-creature permanents. Austere Command or Return to Dust would strengthen this line.
- **Top-end is slow**: End-Raze Forerunners at 8 CMC is still a stretch without ramp. Best as a haymaker when ahead, not a comeback tool.

**Changes from previous draft (V1 → V2)**:

## Suggested Deck Names

- **Double Jeopardy** — Byrke attacks and every counter-carrying creature gets doubled; miss two blocks and the game is over. Captures the all-in escalation and the legal pun on her title.
- **Counter Prosecution** — GW counters strategy meets the courtroom theme; reads as both "prosecuting with counters" and "countering the prosecution." Rolls off the tongue in two words.
- **Crime & Counterment** — plays on *Crime & Punishment*; Patrolling Peacemaker proliferates on opponent crimes; every crime feeds the counter stack and earns a *Counterment*.
