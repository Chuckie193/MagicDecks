# Byrke, Long Ear of the Law — Selesnya +1/+1 Counters Commander

Commander: Byrke, Long Ear of the Law (4GW, Green/White)

## Overview
- **Strategy**: Byrke distributes +1/+1 counters on entering creatures and then doubles all counters on attacking creatures that already have them — turning a modest buff into an exponential arms race over just two or three attack steps. The deck floods the board with low-cost creatures to trigger life-gain engines and counter-distribution effects, then curves into Byrke and threatening finishers to close out the game with overwhelmingly large attackers.
- **Intended for**: Single opponent (1v1 duel)

---

## Lore

Byrke, the Long Ear of the Law, doesn't just enforce justice — she *amplifies* it. Her squad enters every duel twice as ready, growing more fearsome with every swing. In her court, the verdict is always: *double or nothing.*

## Decklist (100 cards)

### Commander (1)
- **Byrke, Long Ear of the Law** — The engine of the entire deck. Her ETB distributes +1/+1 counters on up to two creatures, and her attack trigger doubles all counters on every attacking creature that carries one — turning incremental growth into exponential threats in just a few combat steps.

- **Burrowguard Mentor** — Power and toughness equal to the number of creatures you control, with trample. Scales immediately with the wide board this deck builds and becomes enormous after Byrke's doubling trigger.
- **Finneas, Ace Archer** — Vigilance and reach; puts a +1/+1 counter on each token and Rabbit you control whenever it attacks, then draws a card if your creatures' total power reaches 10 or more — consistent counter distribution and card draw in one.

More importantly, its sacrifice ability gives every creature with any counter hexproof and indestructible until end of turn — a one-card answer to board wipes and targeted removal that the deck previously had no equivalent of.

In 1v1 an opponent using any targeted removal or interaction triggers this, steadily adding a counter to every permanent that already has one.

Those counters persist and Byrke doubles them the next attack step — Serra Redeemer is the single best counter-loader in the 99.

### Artifacts & Mana (7)
- **Sol Ring** — The quintessential Commander ramp piece; enables turn-2 three-drops or a turn-4 Byrke.
- **Arcane Signet** — Taps for Green or White; ensures colour consistency on every early turn.
- **Fellwar Stone** — Taps for any colour your opponent produces, effectively any colour in 1v1. Smooth two-mana ramp.
- **The Ozolith** — When a creature with counters leaves the battlefield, its counters move here; at the start of combat, you can move all stored counters to a single creature. Protects the counter investment from removal and creates a monstrous attacker out of nothing.
- **Air Shoes (Swiftfoot Boots)** — Equip 1; grants hexproof and haste. Protect Byrke from targeted removal and give her haste to immediately double counters the turn she enters.
- **Power Sneakers (Lightning Greaves)** — Equip 0; grants haste and shroud. Free equip makes this the fastest protection for Byrke, though shroud means you can't target her with your own spells afterward.
- **Piko Piko Hammer (Hammer of Nazahn)** — 4W legendary Equipment; equipped creature gets +2/+1 and indestructible; whenever it or another Equipment enters, you may attach that Equipment for free. Enables a one-card Equipment toolbox by auto-attaching everything you search out with Open the Armory.

### Enchantments (8)
- **Resourceful Defense** — When a permanent with counters leaves the battlefield, move those counters elsewhere; [4W] to move counters between permanents as a sorcery. Works with The Ozolith to redirect counter investments and rescue them from removal.
- **Wolfwillow Haven** — Aura on a land that taps for G; inexpensive Green mana fixing and ramp that helps cast the two-Green finishers.
- **New Horizons** — Enchants a land to tap for G and W; also puts a +1/+1 counter on a creature when it enters, giving Byrke's doubling something to multiply immediately.
- **Beastmaster Ascension** — Each creature that attacks adds a quest counter; at 7 counters, all creatures you control get +5/+5. In a deck that attacks every turn with multiple creatures, this can reach threshold in 2–3 turns and instantly closes the game combined with Byrke's doubling.
- **Hunter's Talent** — Enchantment Class; Level 1: target creature you control fights another. Level 2 ([1G]): attacking creatures get +1/+0 and trample each attack (note: direct P/T modification, not a counter — Byrke cannot double it, but trample is the key payoff, ensuring Byrke's doubled counters convert to player damage through any blocker). Level 3 ([3G]): draw a card at each end step if you control a creature with power 4 or more. Provides removal, permanent trample delivery, and card draw across three upgrade thresholds.
- **Colossification** — Aura for 5GG; enchanted creature gets +20/+20 (and taps when it enters). Disguises a medium-sized creature into a one-shot threat; use Byrke's doubling before playing this for maximum absurdity.
- **Pacifism** — Taps and prevents attacks/blocks for 1W; cheap white spot removal that is particularly effective in 1v1 since the opponent cannot redirect the locked creature's value.
- **Prayer of Binding** — 3W enchantment with flash; exiles a nonland permanent until this enchantment leaves and gains 2 life. Flash timing makes this a surprise removal and life gain engine in the same card.

### Instants (7)
- **Swords to Plowshares** — Exile a creature for W; the cleanest and most mana-efficient removal spell in White. No opponent creature survives this.
- **Snakeskin Veil** — G instant; puts a permanent +1/+1 counter on target creature AND grants hexproof until EOT. The counter stays after the turn ends, meaning Byrke can double it in the next attack step. Use it reactively to save a counter-loaded creature from targeted removal while simultaneously adding to its counter total.
- **Adamant Will** — W instant; target creature gets +2/+2 and indestructible until EOT. Blocks lethal combat math or protects a key creature from board wipes.
- **Repel Calamity** — 1W instant; destroys a creature with power or toughness 4 or greater. Highly relevant in 1v1 where large commanders often attack alone.
- **Generous Gift** — 2W instant; destroys any permanent (opponent gets a 3/3 Elephant). The most flexible removal in the deck, hitting enchantments, artifacts, planeswalkers, and lands.
- **Rabbit Response** — 2WW instant; all creatures you control get +2/+1 until EOT; if you control a Rabbit, scry 2 afterward. A combat trick that can swing damage races and refines the top of your library.
- **Bite Down** — 1G instant; target creature you control deals damage equal to its power to a target creature or planeswalker an opponent controls. Scales with Byrke's counter-doubling — a massive creature fights almost anything.

## Key Synergies
- **Serra Redeemer + Small Creature ETBs + Byrke**: Every time any creature with power 2 or less enters (Llanowar Elves, Hinterland Sanctifier, tokens from Hop to It, Prideful Parent, Dwynen's Elite, etc.), Serra Redeemer puts two +1/+1 counters on it immediately. A 1/1 entering with Serra Redeemer in play becomes a 3/3 before Byrke even swings — then Byrke doubles it to 6/6 on the attack. A full board of cheap ETBs can generate 10+ counters for Byrke to multiply in one turn.
- **Byrke + Felidar Savior + Finneas**: Felidar Savior pre-loads two creatures with +1/+1 counters; Byrke doubles them the following attack step. Finneas adds more counters on tokens and Rabbits during the same attack. Together they can create creatures with 12–20+ counters by mid-game.
- **Byrke + The Ozolith + Resourceful Defense**: When a counter-loaded creature dies, The Ozolith absorbs its counters. At the start of combat, move them onto any creature — Byrke then doubles them in the same attack phase, turning removal into an advantage.
- **Wildwood Scourge + Any Counter Distribution**: Each time any non-Hydra creature gets a +1/+1 counter, Wildwood Scourge gets one too. Byrke's doubling trigger in combat doubles both the targeted creature AND the Scourge simultaneously, building two massive threats at once.
- **Beastmaster Ascension + Wide Board**: Attacking with even 3–4 creatures every turn fills quest counters in two attacks. At 7 counters all creatures get +5/+5 — stacked with Byrke's doubling and Jazal Goldmane's activated ability, this can kill from a board state that looked harmless a turn earlier.
- **Life-gain Engine (Hinterland Sanctifier + Dazzling Angel + Prosperous Innkeeper + Bishop's Soldier) + Ajani's Pridemate / Twinblade Paladin**: Every creature ETB triggers Hinterland Sanctifier and Dazzling Angel for 1 life each. Ajani's Pridemate grows by 1 per life-gain trigger; Twinblade Paladin unlocks double strike once you reach 25 life. A full board refill from Dewdrop Cure can grow Pridemate by 4–6 counters in a single turn.
- **Rootcast Apprenticeship → Byrke**: Cast Rootcast Apprenticeship choosing "put two +1/+1 counters" three times on the same creature. It enters combat with six counters; Byrke doubles that to twelve in the same attack step. Combined with Biogenic Upgrade (which distributes and then doubles its own counters), two sorceries can create a 20+ power attacker from almost nothing.
- **Bulwark Ox sacrifice + Board Wipe**: When an opponent taps out for a board wipe, sacrifice Bulwark Ox in response. Every creature you control that has any counter gains hexproof and indestructible until end of turn — the board wipe hits nothing. Since Byrke's ETB loads counters on two creatures, at minimum the most important bodies survive every sweeper.
- **Open the Armory → Hammer of Nazahn → Auto-Equip**: Open the Armory tutors Hammer for 1W; Hammer then attaches itself and every Equipment you subsequently play for free. Colossification via Open the Armory bypasses the one-turn tap penalty if you attach it to a creature that hasn't attacked yet.

## Short Mulligan and Play Notes

**Keep hands that have**: 2 lands + a mana rock or dork, OR 3 lands + a low-cost creature with draw (Helpful Hunter, Inspiring Overseer). Scry aggressively.

**Early game (Turns 1–3)**:
- Turn 1: Sol Ring, Llanowar Elves, or Wolfwillow Haven.
- Turn 2: Arcane Signet, Fellwar Stone, Druid of the Cowl, or a life-gain creature (Hinterland Sanctifier, Bishop's Soldier).
- Turn 3: Establish a life-gain engine or an ETB-draw body (Inspiring Overseer, Helpful Hunter). Equip Lightning Greaves if Byrke is coming next turn.

**Mid game (Turns 4–6)**:
- Deploy Byrke as early as turn 5 with two mana accelerants. Immediately equip Swiftfoot Boots or Lightning Greaves.
- Use Byrke's ETB to counter Felidar Savior and another creature, then attack to double — this creates 4/4+ attackers out of 2/2 bodies in a single turn.
- Build toward Finneas for passive counter-spreading, and Toski for sustained card draw.

**Late game (Turns 7+)**:
- Beastmaster Ascension at 7 quest counters + Jazal Goldmane's activated ability is a frequent win condition.
- End-Raze Forerunners as a haste-enabled surprise attack gives the whole team trample and vigilance.
- Joraga Invocation forces unfavourable blocks into your counter-loaded army.

**Key interactions to remember**:
- Lightning Greaves grants shroud — move it off Byrke before targeting her with your own spells.
- The Ozolith absorbs counters passively; move them at the start of combat before Byrke attacks to maximize doubling.
- Hammer of Nazahn auto-attaches Equipment; play Lightning Greaves after Hammer is equipped to attach it for free.
- Rabbit Response is a combat trick for 2WW — hold mana open to close out a life race.

## Why These Choices

- **Life-gain sub-theme as a secondary engine**: Without Blue card draw, White and Green rely heavily on ETB effects. The life-gain suite (Hinterland Sanctifier, Dazzling Angel, Prosperous Innkeeper, Bishop's Soldier) pulls double duty — it fuels Ajani's Pridemate and keeps Twinblade Paladin's double-strike threshold active while incidentally filling out the curve.
- **Low average CMC for a 6-mana commander**: Byrke herself costs 6, so the 99 skews aggressively toward 1–3 CMC cards (43 of 63 non-land cards). This ensures the board is established before Byrke arrives and she's never the whole plan.
- **Equipment package as Byrke protection**: Lightning Greaves equips for 0; Swiftfoot Boots for 1. Hammer of Nazahn chains everything. Open the Armory fetches whichever is needed. Losing Byrke to targeted removal twice in a row is crippling; protecting her is the priority.
- **Counter resilience via The Ozolith + Resourceful Defense**: Both cards prevent removal from destroying counter investment. Together they make Byrke's strategy resilient against the removal that is most common in 1v1.
- **Trample delivery**: Several finishers (Tajuru Pathwarden, End-Raze Forerunners, Joraga Invocation) provide trample to ensure Byrke's doubled counters convert to player damage through blockers.

## Analysis

**Strengths**:
- Exponential threat scaling: Byrke's doubling means one attack step can multiply a 4/4 into a 16/16+ over two turns.
- Wide variety of win conditions: combat math, Beastmaster Ascension, Jazal pump, End-Raze Forerunners finisher.
- Resilient to removal via The Ozolith and Resourceful Defense saving counter investments.
- Consistent life gain prevents being raced in 1v1 aggro matchups.

**Weaknesses / Missing Staples**:
- **Counterspell protection**: No blue in the colour identity means Byrke is vulnerable to targeted exile (Path to Exile, Swords to Plowshares) — rely entirely on Lightning Greaves, Swiftfoot Boots, Snakeskin Veil, and Adamant Will.
- **Enchantment/artifact removal is limited**: Only Wear Down, Generous Gift, and Prayer of Binding handle non-creature permanents. Cards like Austere Command or Return to Dust would strengthen this line.
- **Top-end is slow**: End-Raze Forerunners at 8 CMC and Colossification at 7 CMC are difficult to cast without ramp. Drawing them when behind feels bad; they're best as haymakers when the board is already large.
- **No mass bounce or return**: No way to blank a superior board other than Fumigate; strong token decks or go-wide strategies that rebuild fast could be problematic.

**Changes from previous draft**:
- **Bulwark Ox in, Warren Elder out**: Warren Elder's [3W] activation gave all creatures +1/+1 *until end of turn* — a direct P/T modification with no interaction with Byrke's counter-doubling. Bulwark Ox costs the same mana, places real counters on attack while saddled, and its sacrifice ability gives all counter-bearing creatures hexproof and indestructible — directly answering the previous draft's vulnerability to board wipes.
- **Rootcast Apprenticeship in, Longstalk Brawl out**: Longstalk Brawl's counter placement was conditional on giving the opponent a 1/1 Fish, making it an expensive way to load a single counter. Rootcast Apprenticeship's choose-three structure can place six permanent counters on a single creature (three times "put two counters"), priming a massive doubling trigger from Byrke on the next swing.

## Suggested Deck Names

- **Double Jeopardy** — Byrke attacks and every counter-carrying creature gets doubled; miss two blocks and the game is over. The name captures the all-in escalation and the legal pun on her title.
- **Counter Prosecution** — GW counters strategy meets the courtroom theme; reads as both "prosecuting with counters" and "countering the prosecution." Rolls off the tongue in two words.
- **Hop to Judgment** — Combines the BLB flavour of rabbit hops with the finality of the verdict; "Hop to It" is a card in the deck and a command to hop to the judgment.
- **The Long Arm of the Law** — The classic idiom echoes Byrke's "Long Ear" title and promises that no creature escapes justice once the attack step doubles her army into lethal range.
