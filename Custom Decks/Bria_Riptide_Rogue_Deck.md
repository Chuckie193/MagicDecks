# Bria, Riptide Rogue — Izzet Spellslinger Commander

Commander: Bria, Riptide Rogue (2UR, Blue/Red)

## Overview
- **Strategy:** Cast cheap noncreature spells in rapid succession to pump all your creatures via prowess and make one unblockable each spell. Layer spell-payoff creatures (Veyran, Harmonic Prodigy, Archmage Emeritus) to multiply triggers, generate mana and tokens, then swing with evasive, oversized threats. Win through combat damage after a big spell turn or via cascade/copy effects for a lethal alpha strike.

## Decklist (100 cards)

### Commander (1)
- **Bria, Riptide Rogue** — 3/3 for 2UR; gives all your creatures prowess and makes one creature unblockable each time you cast a noncreature spell. Every instant or sorcery pulls double duty: triggering prowess bumps AND providing unblockable insurance.

### Creatures (19)

**Spell Payoffs & Synergy Engines**
- **Archmage Emeritus** — Magecraft: draw a card whenever you cast or copy an instant/sorcery. The premier draw engine for this deck; draws you into more spells constantly.
- **Veyran, Voice of Duality** — Magecraft: +1/+1 until end of turn. More importantly: if casting/copying an instant/sorcery causes a triggered ability to trigger, it triggers an additional time. Doubles Archmage Emeritus draws, Coruscation Mage pings, Storm-Kiln Artist treasures, and more.
- **Harmonic Prodigy** — 1/3 for 1R with prowess. If a triggered ability of another Shaman or Wizard you control triggers, it triggers an additional time. Stacks with Veyran to triple-trigger Archmage Emeritus, Storm-Kiln Artist, and Coruscation Mage.
- **Coruscation Mage** — 2/2 for 1R; whenever you cast a noncreature spell, deals 1 damage to each opponent. With Veyran + Harmonic Prodigy active, each spell can ping for 4 damage to each opponent.
- **Storm-Kiln Artist** — 2/2 for 3R; magecraft: create a Treasure token whenever you cast or copy an instant/sorcery. Doubles as a big body (gets +1/+0 per artifact). Every spell generates mana for more spells.
- **Chrome Host Seedshark** — 2/4 flying for 2U; whenever you cast a noncreature spell, incubate X where X is that spell's mana value. Converts spells into Phyrexian artifact creatures.
- **Stormcatch Mentor** — 1/1 haste prowess for UR; instant and sorcery spells you cast cost 1 less. The Strixhaven staple: combines Sapphire Medallion + Ruby Medallion into one prowess body that attacks immediately.
- **Rootha, Mastering the Moment** — 3/4 for 2UR; at the beginning of combat on your turn, if you cast an instant or sorcery this turn, create an X/X flying haste Elemental token where X is the highest mana value among spells cast this turn. Cast Dig Through Time (CMC 8) and attack with an 8/8 flier!
- **Faerie Mastermind** — 2/1 flash flying for 1U; whenever an opponent draws their second card each turn, you draw a card. Stealthy free draw against blue players; also a flash creature to trigger Bria's abilities on opponents' turns.

**Big Threats & Token Generators**
- **Manaform Hellkite** — 4/4 flying for 2RR; whenever you cast a noncreature spell, create an X/X red Dragon Illusion token with flying and haste, where X is the mana spent on that spell. A Dig Through Time generates an 8/8 flying haste dragon for one turn.
- **Galazeth Prismari** — 3/4 flying for 2UR; creates a Treasure on ETB and lets all artifacts tap to add mana specifically for instant/sorcery spells. Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, and all your rocks become bonus mana for spells.
- **Rionya, Fire Dancer** — 3/4 for 3RR; at the beginning of combat, create X tokens that are copies of another creature you control (with haste), where X = 1 plus instants/sorceries cast this turn. Cast 2 spells, swing with 3 copies of your best creature.
- **Plargg and Nassari** — 5/4 for 3RR; at each upkeep, each player exiles until they exile a nonland card. An opponent picks one of those cards. You may cast up to two of the remaining exiled cards for free. Effectively cast 2 free spells per turn cycle.
- **Brudiclad, Telchor Engineer** — 4/4 for 4UR; creature tokens you control have haste. At combat, create a 2/1 Myr token, then optionally copy it for all your other tokens. Turns an army of incubators, elementals, and Myr into copies of your best token.
- **Goldspan Dragon** — 4/4 flying haste for 3RR; creates a Treasure when it attacks or is targeted; Treasure tokens produce double mana. Accelerates into expensive spells and ensures combat pressure every turn it's in play.
- **Redoubled Stormsinger** — 3/3 first strike for 2R; whenever it attacks, for each creature token that entered this turn, create a tapped attacking copy of it. Cast noncreature spells to create Manaform Hellkite dragon tokens, then Redoubled Stormsinger doubles them as attackers.
- **Tolarian Terror** — 5/5 ward 2 for 6U, but costs 1 less per instant/sorcery in your graveyard. Late game this commonly costs 1U or even U. A near-free finisher.
- **Brazen Borrower** — 3/1 flash flying for 2UUU; the Petty Theft adventure (1U: return any nonland permanent to hand) is the key play — a cheap instant-speed trigger + interaction. The creature closes games in the air.
- **Solemn Simulacrum** — 2/2 for 4; searches a basic land on ETB, draws a card on death. Smooths mana and replaces itself.

### Instants (17)

**Cantrips & Cheap Triggers (1 mana)**
- **Opt** — U instant; scry 1, draw 1. The cheapest prowess/Bria trigger; chains into more spells.
- **Fleeting Distraction** — U instant; target creature gets -1/-0 until end of turn, draw a card. Another 1-mana cantrip that replaces itself and fires Bria's trigger.
- **Swan Song** — U instant; counter any enchantment, instant, or sorcery. Creates a 2/2 Bird for the opponent but stops the most dangerous spell types for 1 mana.

**Interaction (2–3 mana)**
- **Into the Roil** — 1U instant; return any nonland permanent to hand. Kicker (1U): draw a card. Flexible bounce; kicked version is a draw trigger too.
- **Arcane Denial** — 1U instant; counter any spell (opponent draws 2, you draw 3 next upkeep). Trade-positive counterspell that keeps the deck moving.
- **Reality Shift** — 1U instant; exile target creature, owner manifests the top card of their library. Clean creature removal that avoids death triggers; the manifest usually does nothing.
- **Quick Study** — 2U instant; draw 2 cards. Simple, efficient instant-speed refuel.
- **Thirst for Knowledge** — 2U instant; draw 3, discard an artifact card or discard 2. In the late game, discard a land from an oversized hand; artifacts are rarely discarded with only 9 in the deck.
- **Resculpt** — 2U instant; exile target artifact or creature; its controller creates a 4/4 Shapeshifter. Answers threats cleanly without generating graveyard fodder.
- **Chaos Warp** — 2R instant; shuffle any permanent into its owner's library; they reveal the top card and put it into play if it's a permanent. Universal but random answer for problem permanents.
- **Prismari Command** — 1UR instant; choose two of four modes: 2 damage to any target / draw 2 discard 2 / create a Treasure / destroy an artifact. Incredibly flexible at 3 mana.
- **Big Score** — 3R instant; discard a card, draw 2, create 2 Treasure tokens. Pays for itself in mana and filters the hand.
- **Unexpected Windfall** — 3R instant; discard a card, draw 2, create 2 Treasure tokens. Identical upside to Big Score; running both ensures reliable Treasure generation.

**High-Impact Payoffs**
- **Magma Opus** — 4UR instant; deal 4 damage divided among any targets, tap two permanents, create a 4/4 blue/red Elemental token, draw 2 cards. Six mana buys you everything: removal, disruption, a body, and gas. High CMC means Manaform Hellkite creates a massive Dragon token when cast.
- **Dig Through Time** — 6UU instant (delve); look at top 7, keep 2. With a graveyard full of spent cantrips and spells, this typically costs UU–1UU. The best late-game selection in the deck.
- **Treasure Cruise** — 7U instant (delve); draw 3. Usually 1U or less with a fueled graveyard. Refuels the hand after a big spell turn.
- **Cancel** — 1UU instant; counter any spell. Reliable backup counterspell; less elegant than Swan Song but works universally.

### Sorceries (16)

**Card Selection & Draw**
- **Expressive Iteration** — UR sorcery; look at top 3, one to hand, one exile (play this turn), one to bottom. Nets two cards of selection for 2 mana; best card-selection in Izzet.
- **Deep Analysis** — 3U sorcery (flashback 1U + 3 life); draw 2 twice over two casts. The flashback fills the graveyard for Tolarian Terror and delve spells.
- **Fabricate** — 2U sorcery; tutor for any artifact and put it in hand. Finds Sol Ring, Cloud Key, Greaves, Everflowing Chalice, or Cursed Mirror situationally.
- **Replication Technique** — 2U sorcery; create a token copy of target artifact or creature you control. Copy Archmage Emeritus for double draw, or copy Brudiclad's Myr to reshape your token army.

**Removal & Wipes**
- **Blasphemous Act** — 8R sorcery (costs 1 less per creature on the battlefield); deals 13 damage to each creature. In a typical multiplayer game with 10+ creatures, costs 1R or 2R.
- **Chain Reaction** — 2RR sorcery; deals X damage to each creature where X = number of creatures on the battlefield. Similar to Blasphemous Act; sometimes hits harder in creature-light games.

**Token Generators & Combat Tricks**
- **Twinflame** — 1R sorcery (strive: costs 2R more per additional target); choose any number of your creatures and create hasted token copies of each, exiled at end step. Copy Bria or a key creature for a final swing.
- **Rite of Replication** — 2UU sorcery (kicker 5); create 1 token copy of a creature, or kicked: 5 copies. Kicked copies of Archmage Emeritus nets 5 draw triggers per spell cast that turn.
- **Surge to Victory** — 4RR sorcery; exile a target instant/sorcery from your graveyard: your creatures get +X/+0 until end of turn (X = CMC of that spell), and whenever a creature deals combat damage this turn, copy the exiled card for free. Exile Magma Opus or Rite of Replication, buff your team by +6 or +5, then copy the big spell through combat.

**Mana Engines**
- **Rousing Refrain** — 2R sorcery; add Red for each card in target opponent's hand; this mana persists past phase end. Exile itself with 3 time counters. Each upkeep until counters run out, this re-triggers from exile, generating massive free mana.
- **Mana Geyser** — 3RR sorcery; add Red for each tapped land your opponents control. In a 4-player game on turn 5, opponents often have 15–20 tapped lands; this generates enormous Red mana for a big spell turn.

**Cascade Engines**
- **Volcanic Torrent** — 4R sorcery with cascade; deals X damage to each creature and planeswalker opponents control where X = number of spells you've cast this turn. The cascade finds something costing 4 or less for free; the damage scales with your spell chain.
- **Throes of Chaos** — 3R sorcery with cascade and retrace; cascade finds something for free. Retrace: discard a land to cast this from the graveyard repeatedly. Late game, discard excess lands to re-trigger cascade and Bria's prowess chain.

**Late-Game Finishers**
- **Creative Technique** — 4R sorcery; shuffle library, reveal until a nonland card, cast it for free. High-variance but can randomly win games on the spot.
- **Dance with Calamity** — 7R sorcery; exile cards from the top of your library as many times as you choose, and if total mana value is 13 or less, cast all of them free. Explosive finisher when ahead.
- **Furygale Flocking** — 8RR sorcery (costs 1 less per instant/sorcery card in your graveyard); create two 3/3 flying attacking tokens per opponent with haste. With 20+ instants/sorceries in the graveyard, this costs 0RR and floods the board with flying attacks.

## Key Synergies

- **Veyran + Harmonic Prodigy + Archmage Emeritus**: Every instant or sorcery draws 4 cards instead of 1. Archmage Emeritus triggers once; Veyran doubles to 2; Harmonic Prodigy (Wizard trigger) doubles to 4.
- **Galazeth Prismari + Storm-Kiln Artist**: Galazeth lets all artifacts tap for spell mana. Storm-Kiln creates a Treasure per spell. After casting 2–3 spells, your entire artifact base is producing mana exclusively for more instants and sorceries — a self-sustaining loop.
- **Manaform Hellkite + Redoubled Stormsinger + Brudiclad**: Cast a big spell (e.g., Magma Opus at CMC 6) to create a 6/6 Dragon token. Redoubled Stormsinger doubles it as a tapped attacker. If Brudiclad is in play, convert all your other tokens to 6/6 Dragons before combat.
- **Rootha, Mastering the Moment + Delve Spells**: Cast Treasure Cruise or Dig Through Time (CMC 8 before delve) to create an 8/8 flying haste Elemental token at the start of combat. With Bria active, one of your creatures is also unblockable.
- **Throes of Chaos + Retrace + Bria**: Discard excess lands in the late game to recast Throes of Chaos from the graveyard. Each cast cascades into a free spell, triggers prowess on all creatures, and makes one creature unblockable. Chain multiple activations for lethal combat damage.
- **Surge to Victory + Dig Through Time**: Exile Dig Through Time from your graveyard; your team gets +8/+0 until end of turn. Each creature that connects copies Dig for free. A single open attack lane through Bria's ability can win the game.

## Mulligan and Play Notes

**Opening hand targets:** 2–3 lands, 1 mana rock (Sol Ring, Arcane Signet), and at least 1 of Stormcatch Mentor / Opt / Expressive Iteration. Mulligan any hand without mana or without meaningful turn-2 action.

**Early game (turns 1–4):**
- T1: Sol Ring or Island into Swiftfoot Boots / Everflowing Chalice.
- T2: Drop a 2-mana rock. Keep mana open for reactive spells if needed.
- T3: Cast Bria, Riptide Rogue. Then cast any 1-mana spell immediately to give a creature prowess +1/+1 and make it unblockable this turn.
- T4: Chain 2 instants on the same turn. Bria's trigger provides 2 unblockable attacks; even a 2-damage hit sets up future lethal math.

**Mid game (turns 5–8):**
- Layer spell-payoff creatures: Archmage Emeritus → Storm-Kiln Artist → Veyran is the ideal curve.
- Once Galazeth Prismari is active, every artifact tap funds a free spell, which funds another free spell — look for 3-spell turns.
- Use Coruscation Mage + Veyran to whittle down opponents' life totals passively while swinging.
- Hold Cancel or Swan Song for critical moments; flash in Brazen Borrower // Petty Theft to clear blockers on opponents' end steps.

**Late game:**
- Win with combat: cast Surge to Victory, exile Magma Opus or Dig Through Time from graveyard, buff team by 6–8, swing with Bria-protected unblockable creature and copy the spell on connect.
- Win with Furygale Flocking (often 0–2 mana when graveyard is stocked) for a surprise flood of flying attackers.
- Plargg and Nassari generates free casts every upkeep; once you reach this stage, opponents must deal with it immediately or concede card advantage.
- Retrace Throes of Chaos repeatedly with excess land-heavy hands to cascade into free 3-mana spells each iteration.

## Analysis

**Strengths:**
- Extremely high synergy density: almost every card in the deck improves every other card.
- Combat evasion is built into the commander — no need to rely on flying or trample for the kill swing.
- Multiple independent win conditions: combat damage, Surge to Victory copying, and Furygale Flocking flood.
- Resilient to removal: Bria coming back from the command zone still reactivates the whole engine.

**Weaknesses / Missing Staples:**
- **Graveyard hate:** No dedicated graveyard hate. A Bojuka Bog or Soul-Guide Lantern in the deck would protect Mystic Sanctuary recursion and delve spells.
- **Stax / Rule of Law:** Enchantments that lock to one spell per turn (Rule of Law, Arcane Laboratory) shut the entire strategy down. The deck has no enchantment removal.
- **Artifact removal:** Only Prismari Command can destroy artifacts and only Resculpt can exile them. Consider swapping a sorcery for Abrade if facing heavy artifact decks.
- **No tutor for key creatures:** The deck can't consistently find Archmage Emeritus or Veyran. Mystical Tutor or Spellseeker would dramatically increase consistency.
