# Amy Rose — Equipment Voltron Commander

Commander: Amy Rose ({2}{R}{W}, Red/White)

## Overview
- **Strategy**: Amy Rose arms herself for free every time she attacks, then hands her whole power total to a second attacker as a +X/+0 pump. The deck stacks cheap, high-impact Equipment on her so that one attack becomes two lethal-sized threats, and backs it with double strike, trample and evasion so the damage actually lands. Wins by commander damage, by a single donated-power haymaker, or by grinding a pod down with repeated multi-attacker swings.
- **Intended for**: Multiplayer pod
- **Card pool**: Full collection (every owned card in `moxfield_latest.csv`, including cards currently sitting in assembled decks)

## Lore

Amy swings the biggest hammer on the hill, and whatever she grabs mid-charge, she keeps. Her crew learns fast: run behind her and her strength becomes theirs. *Love hurts. Her hammer hurts considerably more.*

## Key Synergies
- **Amy Rose + Dáin Ironfoot**: Amy attaches an Equipment to herself on attack; Dáin's attack trigger then gives every *equipped* attacking creature double strike. Amy hits twice at her boosted power, and the creature she pumped hits twice at its boosted power. This is the deck's fastest kill.
- **Amy Rose + Knuckles the Echidna**: Knuckles already has double strike and trample. Hand him Amy's power total and the donated +X/+0 gets counted twice and tramples over chump blockers — a five-mana board state that regularly deals 20+ to one opponent.
- **Piko Piko Hammer (Hammer of Nazahn) + Blasphemous Act / Fumigate**: The Hammer makes the equipped creature indestructible, so you can fire off a one-sided sweeper while Amy walks away untouched, then swing into an empty board.
- **Super State + any opponent count**: Amy becomes a 9/9 flying, first-striking trampler, and her combat damage to one opponent is copied to *each other opponent*. In a four-player pod that is 27 damage spread across the table from one attack.
- **Aurelia, the Warleader + Amy Rose**: Aurelia's extra combat gives Amy a second attack trigger — a second free Equipment attach and a second +X/+0 donation, on top of a fully untapped team.
- **Frontier Warmonger + donated power**: Menace on every attacker means the giant +X/+0 creature needs two blockers to stop, which a pod defending three threats at once rarely has.

## Short Mulligan and Play Notes
- **Early game (turns 1–3)**: Keep hands with two lands and a rock or a cheap Equipment. Deploy Sol Ring / Arcane Signet and a two-mana Equipment before Amy. Thorin Oakenshield and Mabel are both excellent turn-two and turn-three plays that leave a body behind.
- **Mid game (turns 4–8)**: Cast Amy only when you can attack the same turn (she has haste) and you already control at least one Equipment — an unequipped Amy is a 3/3 that eats removal for nothing. Pick the opponent with the weakest board, attach the biggest Equipment, and donate her power to whichever of your creatures has double strike, trample or evasion. Hold Boros Charm or Make a Stand as protection rather than spending it as reach.
- **Late game (turns 9+)**: Super State, Aurelia or Terror-sized attacks close it out. With the Hammer on the board, fire Blasphemous Act freely — you keep an indestructible threat while the pod starts over.
- **Key interactions**: Amy's attach trigger targets the *Equipment*, not her, so equip costs never matter and she can arm herself even while hexproof. The +X/+0 is measured when the ability resolves, so attach the Equipment first (same trigger, in order) and the pump already counts it. The pumped creature must also be *attacking* — declare attackers with a second threat, always.

## Why These Choices (Summary)
- Amy is a payoff commander, not an engine: the deck is built so that she is never attacking empty-handed. Thirteen Equipment, two Equipment tutors (Open the Armory, Hoarding Dragon), three Equipment-token makers and four "attach for free" effects mean an Equipment is available on essentially every attack step.
- Recipients were chosen for *multiplication*, not raw size. Double strike (Knuckles, Iron Hills Blacksmith, Dáin, Fireshrieker, Boros Charm) doubles the donated power, and trample/menace/flying make sure it is not blanked by a single chump blocker.
- Ramp is deliberately cheap and artifact-based so it also turns on Spire of Industry, and so the curve peaks at three — this is an attack deck that wants Amy down on four and pressure on five.
- Multiplayer concessions: three sweepers (Blasphemous Act, Fumigate, Explosive Getaway) paired with two ways to survive them (Piko Piko Hammer's indestructible, Make a Stand / Boros Charm), Frontier Warmonger for evasion against wide boards, and Super State as a finisher that scales with the number of opponents.
- Notable omissions: no Lightning Greaves (Power Sneakers) — its shroud fights Piko Piko Hammer's "attach to target creature you control" trigger. No Gratuitous Violence despite the huge payoff — {2}{R}{R}{R} is too demanding for a 17-nonbasic mana base. No Winota, Joiner of Forces — the deck runs too few Humans to power her.

## Analysis

**Strengths**:
- Very high damage ceiling for the mana invested — a single Amy attack with one Equipment and a double-striking partner routinely represents 20+ damage from a four-mana commander.
- Redundancy: thirteen Equipment plus two tutors means the core plan is extremely hard to disrupt by killing any one card.
- Low curve (peaks at three) with 36 lands and eight ramp pieces means the deck deploys and attacks ahead of slower pod decks.
- Piko Piko Hammer gives the deck something most aggro decks lack — the ability to fire off its own board wipes profitably.

**Weaknesses/Missing Staples**:
- **Card draw**: eight to nine sources (Skullclamp, Mazemind Tome, The Arkenstone, Inspiring Overseer, Ashe, Big Score, Seize the Spoils, Thrór's Map, Solemn Simulacrum) is on the low end for a multiplayer deck. Boros is the format's worst colour pair for refuelling, and the collection has no Boros-legal draw engine stronger than these.
- **Aura/Equipment removal**: the deck folds hard to a single well-timed Disenchant on a stacked Amy if she is not carrying the Hammer. Only Air Shoes and Thorin Oakenshield's ward provide protection.
- **Graveyard recursion**: Buried Ruin and Heirloom Blade are the only ways to rebuy Equipment. A Bojuka Bog–style exile effect or a targeted artifact wipe sets the deck back badly.
- **Interaction on other players' turns**: eleven instants is decent, but there are no counterspells (mono-colour restriction) and the deck cannot stop a combo kill it did not see coming.
- **Vulnerability to pillowfort**: Ghostly Prison / Propaganda effects tax every attack, and the deck has only Chaos Warp, Generous Gift and Banishing Light as answers.

## Next Steps (Optional Suggestions)
- Test whether Blasphemous Act earns its slot. If the pod is creature-light, swap it for **Gratuitous Violence** (doubles all your creature damage) or **Terror of Mount Velus** (team-wide double strike on ETB) as a second haymaker.
- If games run long, consider cutting **Burnished Hart** for **Coveted Jewel** or **Hedron Archive** to convert the mana surplus into cards.
- If the pod is fast and interactive, **Lightning Greaves (Power Sneakers)** is still worth a slot over **Adventuring Gear**-tier Equipment for the free haste and protection, accepting the Hammer anti-synergy.
- Cards worth acquiring: Sunforger (a Boros staple that turns Amy's free attach into an instant-speed tutor), Bruenor Battlehammer, and Puresteel Paladin — all three convert this shell from good to genuinely fast.
- **Colossus Hammer** deserves a mention: +10/+10 with equip {8} is exactly the card Amy was designed to cheat, since her trigger attaches it for free.

## Suggested Deck Names

- **Hammer Time** — Punchy two-word option. Amy's Piko Piko Hammer is the deck's best card and its flavour centre, and "you can't touch this" is exactly what an indestructible equipped commander says.
- **Crush Damage** — Double meaning: Amy's famously unrequited crush, and the crushing damage she hands to whoever is standing next to her.
- **Swing Set** — A pun on both halves of the deck: a *set* of Equipment, and the fact that every single turn is spent *swinging*.
- **Armed and Adorable** — Captures the tonal collision at the heart of a pink hedgehog with a giant mallet and a rack of legendary weapons.
