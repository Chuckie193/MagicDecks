# Bria, Riptide Rogue — Izzet Spellslinger Commander *(1v1 Edition)*

Commander: Bria, Riptide Rogue (2UR, Blue/Red)

## Overview
- **Strategy:** Cast cheap noncreature spells in rapid succession to pump all your creatures via prowess and make one unblockable each spell. Layer spell-payoff creatures (Veyran, Harmonic Prodigy, Archmage Emeritus) to multiply triggers, generate mana and tokens, then swing with evasive, oversized threats. Win through combat damage after a big spell turn or via cascade/copy effects for a lethal alpha strike.
- **Intended for:** Single opponent (1v1 / duel)

---

## Lore

Bria doesn't steal treasure, she steals *moments*; slipping through riptide currents no one else dares follow. The tide doesn't deal in mercy and neither does she. *Sea* you on the other side...

## Changes from Draft → v1.1 (1v1 Adaptation + Land Count Increase)

Nine cards were cut from the Draft: seven multiplayer-centric cards replaced with efficient, targeted alternatives, and two additional cuts to make room for two extra basic lands (36 → 38 total).

### Cut

| Card | Type | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| Coruscation Mage | Creature | "Deals 1 damage to **each opponent**" — only pings 1 target in 1v1; negligible drain compared to the 3–4 pings per spell it provides in a pod |
| Plargg and Nassari | Creature | Political group effect where the single opponent controls which exile you miss; expensive at 5 CMC for an effect that rewards having more opponents |
| Arcane Denial | Instant | Counterspell that gives the opponent 2 cards; the card-advantage swap is acceptable in multiplayer (where one opponent gains cards) but is a net loss in 1v1 |
| Chain Reaction | Sorcery | Damage scales with total creatures on the battlefield — far less reliable in 1v1 where the board is typically smaller |
| Blasphemous Act | Sorcery | Board wipe less valuable against a single opponent; cost reduction from creatures is much less reliable in 1v1 |
| Mana Geyser | Sorcery | "Add Red for each tapped land your opponents control" — opponent has ~4–6 tapped lands in 1v1 vs 15–20 in a pod; not worth a slot |
| Furygale Flocking | Sorcery | Creates tokens "per opponent" — only 2 tokens in 1v1 vs 6 in a pod; even free this is a poor rate for 1v1 |
| Faerie Mastermind | Creature | Conditional draw — only triggers when opponent draws their second card each turn; unreliable in 1v1; cut to make room for +1 land |
| Rousing Refrain | Sorcery | Suspend 3 too slow for aggressive 1v1; red mana payoff takes 3 turns to arrive; cut to make room for +1 land |

### Added

| Card | Type | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| Abrade | Instant | 2-mana instant that deals 3 to a creature **or** destroys an artifact — precise, efficient removal against 1 opponent's key threats |
| Soul-Guide Lantern | Artifact | 1-mana graveyard hate (named weakness in the original analysis); in 1v1 the sacrifice mode exiles the single opponent's entire graveyard |
| Rootha, Mercurial Artist | Creature | [2] + return to hand: copy target instant or sorcery — doubles Aether Gale, Surge to Victory, or Dig Through Time for brutal 1v1 blowouts |
| Mirrorwing Dragon | Creature | 4/5 flying that deters targeted removal in 1v1 (opponent must copy it for all their creatures); a dominant aerial threat that is extremely hard to answer cleanly |
| Prismari Pianist | Creature | Creates a 1/1 Elemental token per spell (3 tokens for CMC 5+) — floods the board every spell chain, providing attackers that Bria can make unblockable |
| Aether Gale | Sorcery | Returns up to 6 of the opponent's nonland permanents to hand for 5 mana (3UU) — a devastating one-sided reset ideally suited to 1v1 |
| Pull from Tomorrow | Instant | XUU instant: draw X, discard 1 — the ideal mana sink when Galazeth Prismari and artifact mana are active; draws 5–7 cards in a single turn |
| Island (×8 total) | Land | +1 Island raises land count to 38 and improves Mystic Sanctuary reliability |
| Mountain (×8 total) | Land | +1 Mountain smooths mid-game RR requirements |

## Decklist (100 cards)

### Commander (1)
- **Bria, Riptide Rogue** — 3/3 for 2UR; gives all your creatures prowess and makes one creature unblockable each time you cast a noncreature spell. Every instant or sorcery pulls double duty: triggering prowess bumps AND providing unblockable insurance.

---

### Creatures (19)

**Spell Payoffs & Synergy Engines**
- **Archmage Emeritus** — Magecraft: draw a card whenever you cast or copy an instant/sorcery. The premier draw engine for this deck; draws you into more spells constantly.
- **Veyran, Voice of Duality** — Magecraft: +1/+1 until end of turn. If casting/copying an instant/sorcery causes a triggered ability to trigger, it triggers an additional time. Doubles Archmage Emeritus draws, Storm-Kiln Artist treasures, and more.
- **Harmonic Prodigy** — 1/3 for 1R with prowess. If a triggered ability of another Shaman or Wizard you control triggers, it triggers an additional time. Stacks with Veyran to triple-trigger Archmage Emeritus and Storm-Kiln Artist.
- **Storm-Kiln Artist** — 2/2 for 3R; magecraft: create a Treasure token whenever you cast or copy an instant/sorcery. Doubles as a big body (gets +1/+0 per artifact). Every spell generates mana for more spells.
- **Chrome Host Seedshark** — 2/4 flying for 2U; whenever you cast a noncreature spell, incubate X where X is that spell's mana value. Converts spells into Phyrexian artifact creatures.
- **Stormcatch Mentor** — 1/1 haste prowess for UR; instant and sorcery spells you cast cost 1 less. Combines Sapphire Medallion + Ruby Medallion into one prowess body.
- **Rootha, Mastering the Moment** — 3/4 for 2UR; at the beginning of combat on your turn, if you cast an instant or sorcery this turn, create an X/X flying haste Elemental token where X is the highest mana value among spells cast this turn.
- **Rootha, Mercurial Artist** *(NEW)* — 1/4 Orc Shaman for 1UR; [2], return Rootha to hand: copy target instant or sorcery you control. Pay [2] to double Aether Gale, Surge to Victory, or Dig Through Time, then recast Rootha next turn.
**Threats & Token Generators**
- **Manaform Hellkite** — 4/4 flying for 2RR; whenever you cast a noncreature spell, create an X/X red Dragon Illusion token with flying and haste, where X is the mana spent on that spell.
- **Galazeth Prismari** — 3/4 flying for 2UR; creates a Treasure on ETB and lets all artifacts tap to add mana specifically for instant/sorcery spells. Every rock becomes bonus mana for spells.
- **Rionya, Fire Dancer** — 3/4 for 3RR; at the beginning of combat, create X tokens that are copies of another creature you control (with haste), where X = 1 plus instants/sorceries cast this turn.
- **Prismari Pianist** *(NEW)* — 2/1 Djinn Bard for 1RR; whenever you cast any instant or sorcery, create a 1/1 blue/red Elemental token (or three tokens if the spell's mana value is 5 or greater). Every spell chain grows the board; Bria can make any of them unblockable.
- **Brudiclad, Telchor Engineer** — 4/4 for 4UR; creature tokens you control have haste. At combat, create a 2/1 Myr token, then optionally copy it for all your other tokens.
- **Goldspan Dragon** — 4/4 flying haste for 3RR; creates a Treasure when it attacks or is targeted; Treasure tokens produce double mana. Ensures combat pressure every turn it's in play.
- **Mirrorwing Dragon** *(NEW)* — 4/5 flying for 3RR; whenever a player casts an instant or sorcery that targets only Mirrorwing Dragon, that player copies it for each other creature they control that the spell could target. In 1v1, opponents cannot safely aim removal at Mirrorwing without it reflecting onto all their other creatures — effectively a 4/5 flier that demands an artifact removal or board wipe to answer cleanly.
- **Redoubled Stormsinger** — 3/3 first strike for 2R; whenever it attacks, for each creature token that entered this turn, create a tapped attacking copy of it.
- **Tolarian Terror** — 5/5 ward 2 for 6U, but costs 1 less per instant/sorcery in your graveyard. Late game this commonly costs 1U or even U. A near-free finisher.
- **Brazen Borrower** — 3/1 flash flying for 2UUU; the Petty Theft adventure (1U: return any nonland permanent to hand) is the key play — a cheap instant-speed trigger + interaction.
- **Solemn Simulacrum** — 2/2 for 4; searches a basic land on ETB, draws a card on death. Smooths mana and replaces itself.

### Artifacts (10)

- **Sol Ring** — Essential 1-mana ramp; Galazeth Prismari also lets it contribute to spell costs later.
- **Arcane Signet** — 2-mana rock for Blue/Red; smooth early ramp.
- **Talisman of Creativity** — 2-mana UR rock; consistent color fixing with a minor life cost.
- **Fellwar Stone** — 2-mana rock producing any color an opponent's land could.
- **Everflowing Chalice** — Free artifact that scales with investment; drops early for 0 as a prowess-trigger-free ramp seed.
- **Lightning Greaves** — Equip 0; gives shroud and haste. Protects Bria or Archmage Emeritus from removal the turn they land.
- **Swiftfoot Boots** — Equip 1; gives hexproof and haste. Slightly slower than Greaves but lets you still target your own creatures.
- **Cloud Key** — 3-mana artifact; choose a spell type (instants or sorceries) to cost 1 less. Pairs with Stormcatch Mentor for aggressive cost reduction.
- **Cursed Mirror** — 2R artifact; taps for Red, or ETB copies any creature on the battlefield until end of turn with haste.
- **Soul-Guide Lantern** *(NEW)* — 1-mana artifact; ETB: exile a card from any graveyard. Tap+sacrifice: exile the opponent's entire graveyard. [1]+tap+sacrifice: draw a card. Plugs the deck's graveyard-hate gap and incidentally cantrips when the exile mode isn't needed.

### Enchantments (2)

- **Determined Iteration** — 1R enchantment; at the beginning of combat, populate (copy a creature token), and that copy has haste, sacrificed at end step. Guarantees a hasted attack every turn with any token.
- **Count on Luck** — RRR enchantment; at the beginning of your upkeep, exile the top card of your library and you may play it that turn. Continuous free card access each turn cycle.

### Instants (17)

**Cantrips & Cheap Triggers (1 mana)**
- **Opt** — U instant; scry 1, draw 1. The cheapest prowess/Bria trigger.
- **Fleeting Distraction** — U instant; target creature gets -1/-0 until end of turn, draw a card. Another 1-mana cantrip that replaces itself.
- **Swan Song** — U instant; counter any enchantment, instant, or sorcery. In 1v1 the 2/2 Bird token is a manageable cost to stop the most dangerous spell types.

**Interaction (2–3 mana)**
- **Into the Roil** — 1U instant; return any nonland permanent to hand. Kicker (1U): draw a card. Flexible bounce.
- **Reality Shift** — 1U instant; exile target creature, owner manifests the top card of their library. Clean creature removal that avoids death triggers.
- **Abrade** *(NEW)* — 1R instant; deal 3 damage to any target creature, or destroy target artifact. The most efficient targeted removal in the deck — answers both key creatures and mana rocks.
- **Quick Study** — 2U instant; draw 2 cards. Simple, efficient instant-speed refuel.
- **Thirst for Knowledge** — 2U instant; draw 3, discard an artifact card or discard 2.
- **Resculpt** — 2U instant; exile target artifact or creature; its controller creates a 4/4 Shapeshifter. Answers threats without generating graveyard fodder.
- **Chaos Warp** — 2R instant; shuffle any permanent into its owner's library; they reveal the top card and put it into play if it's a permanent. Universal answer for problem permanents.
- **Prismari Command** — 1UR instant; choose two: 2 damage to any target / draw 2 discard 2 / create a Treasure / destroy an artifact. Incredibly flexible.
- **Cancel** — 1UU instant; counter any spell. Reliable backup counterspell.

**Card Draw**
- **Pull from Tomorrow** *(NEW)* — XUU instant; draw X cards, then discard 1. With Galazeth Prismari and Storm-Kiln Artist active, X=5 or higher becomes realistic — one of the best single-card refuels in the game for 1v1 endgames.

**High-Impact Payoffs**
- **Big Score** — 3R instant; discard a card, draw 2, create 2 Treasure tokens.
- **Unexpected Windfall** — 3R instant; discard a card, draw 2, create 2 Treasure tokens. Identical upside to Big Score; running both ensures reliable Treasure generation.
- **Magma Opus** — 4UR instant; deal 4 damage divided among any targets, tap two permanents, create a 4/4 Elemental token, draw 2 cards.
- **Dig Through Time** — 6UU instant (delve); look at top 7, keep 2. With a fueled graveyard, typically costs UU–1UU.

### Sorceries (13)

**Card Selection & Draw**
- **Treasure Cruise** — 7U sorcery (delve); draw 3. Usually 1U or less with a fueled graveyard.
- **Expressive Iteration** — UR sorcery; look at top 3, one to hand, one exile (play this turn), one to bottom. Nets two cards of selection for 2 mana.
- **Deep Analysis** — 3U sorcery (flashback 1U + 3 life); draw 2 twice over two casts.
- **Fabricate** — 2U sorcery; tutor for any artifact. Finds Sol Ring, Cloud Key, Greaves, Soul-Guide Lantern, or Cursed Mirror.
- **Replication Technique** — 2U sorcery; create a token copy of target artifact or creature you control.

**Board Reset & Disruption**
- **Aether Gale** *(NEW)* — 3UU sorcery; return up to 6 target nonland permanents to their owners' hands. In 1v1 this clears your opponent's entire developed board for 5 mana. Follow up with Bria on the attack for a turn of uncontested pressure. Combine with Rootha, Mercurial Artist to copy it ([2] + return Rootha to hand) for a 12-permanent bounce.

**Token Generators & Combat Tricks**
- **Twinflame** — 1R sorcery (strive); choose any number of your creatures and create hasted token copies of each. Copy Bria or a key creature for a final swing.
- **Rite of Replication** — 2UU sorcery (kicker 5); create 1 token copy of a creature, or kicked: 5 copies. Kicked copies of Archmage Emeritus nets 5 draw triggers per spell cast that turn.
- **Surge to Victory** — 4RR sorcery; exile a target instant/sorcery from your graveyard; your creatures get +X/+0 until end of turn (X = CMC), and whenever a creature deals combat damage this turn, copy the exiled card for free.

**Cascade Engines**
- **Volcanic Torrent** — 4R sorcery with cascade; deals X damage to each creature and planeswalker opponents control where X = number of spells you've cast this turn.
- **Throes of Chaos** — 3R sorcery with cascade and retrace; retrace: discard a land to recast repeatedly from the graveyard, re-triggering cascade each time.

**Late-Game Finishers**
- **Creative Technique** — 4R sorcery; shuffle library, reveal until a nonland card, cast it for free. High-variance but can win games on the spot.
- **Dance with Calamity** — 7R sorcery; exile cards repeatedly from the top of your library; if total mana value is 13 or less, cast all of them free.

## Key Synergies

- **Veyran + Harmonic Prodigy + Archmage Emeritus**: Every instant or sorcery draws 4 cards instead of 1. Archmage triggers once; Veyran doubles to 2; Harmonic Prodigy (Wizard trigger) doubles to 4.
- **Galazeth Prismari + Storm-Kiln Artist**: Galazeth lets all artifacts tap for spell mana. Storm-Kiln creates a Treasure per spell. After casting 2–3 spells, your entire artifact base produces mana exclusively for more instants and sorceries — a self-sustaining loop. Feeds Pull from Tomorrow for a massive refuel.
- **Prismari Pianist + Bria**: Every spell creates at least one 1/1 Elemental (three at CMC 5+). Bria can make any of them unblockable. A spell chain of 4 spells (including one CMC 5+) produces 6 tokens; two of them connect for lethal math over a few turns.
- **Rootha, Mercurial Artist + Aether Gale**: Cast Aether Gale (bounce up to 6 opponent permanents), then pay [2] + return Rootha to hand to copy it — bouncing up to 12 permanents total for 7 mana. Opponent is left with nothing; you untap and win.
- **Mirrorwing Dragon + Removal Deterrence**: In 1v1 an opponent cannot safely aim a targeted spell at only Mirrorwing — it copies for every other creature they control that the spell could target. This forces them to use board wipes or artifact-destruction, both of which are harder to deploy at instant speed. A 4/5 flier that essentially requires a non-targeting answer.
- **Manaform Hellkite + Redoubled Stormsinger + Brudiclad**: Cast a big spell (e.g., Magma Opus at CMC 6) to create a 6/6 Dragon token. Redoubled Stormsinger doubles it as a tapped attacker. If Brudiclad is in play, convert all other tokens to 6/6 Dragons before combat.
- **Rootha, Mastering the Moment + Delve Spells**: Cast Treasure Cruise or Dig Through Time (CMC 8 before delve) to create an 8/8 flying haste Elemental token at the start of combat.
- **Surge to Victory + Dig Through Time**: Exile Dig Through Time from your graveyard; your team gets +8/+0 until end of turn. A single unblockable creature (provided by Bria) connecting copies Dig for free.
- **Throes of Chaos + Retrace + Bria**: Discard excess lands in the late game to recast Throes of Chaos repeatedly, cascading into free spells and making a creature unblockable on each cast.

## Mulligan and Play Notes

**Opening hand targets (1v1):** 2–3 lands, 1 mana rock (Sol Ring, Arcane Signet), and at least 1 of Stormcatch Mentor / Opt / Expressive Iteration. Mulligan any hand without mana or without meaningful turn-2 action.

**Early game (turns 1–4):**
- T1: Sol Ring or land into Soul-Guide Lantern (hedges graveyard early) or Swiftfoot Boots.
- T2: Drop a 2-mana rock. Hold Abrade open if opponent has an early artifact threat.
- T3: Cast Bria, Riptide Rogue. Then cast any 1-mana spell immediately to make a creature unblockable and trigger prowess.
- T4: Chain 2 instants on the same turn. Even a 2-damage hit begins lethal math fast in 1v1.

**Mid game (turns 5–8):**
- Layer spell-payoff creatures: Archmage Emeritus → Storm-Kiln Artist → Veyran is the ideal curve.
- Prismari Pianist on the board means every spell creates blockers and attackers; commit to the aggressive line rather than slowing down.
- Use Abrade to clear key blockers or opponent's mana rocks before the big turn.
- Hold Cancel or Swan Song for the opponent's most dangerous answer; flash in Brazen Borrower // Petty Theft to clear blockers at end step.
- Mirrorwing Dragon on turn 5 (3RR) forces the opponent into non-targeting answers.

**Late game:**
- Win with combat: cast Surge to Victory, exile Magma Opus or Dig Through Time from graveyard, buff team by 6–8, swing with Bria-protected unblockable creature and copy the spell on connect.
- Win with Aether Gale: bounce the opponent's board for 5 mana (3UU), copy it with Rootha, Mercurial Artist for [2] (12 permanents total bounced), then swing wide through an empty board.
- Pull from Tomorrow at X=6 restores a depleted hand in a single shot once Galazeth Prismari is active.
- Retrace Throes of Chaos repeatedly with excess land-heavy hands to cascade into free 3-mana spells each iteration.

## Analysis

**Strengths:**
- Extremely high synergy density: almost every card improves every other card.
- Combat evasion is built into the commander — no need to rely on flying or trample for the kill swing.
- Multiple independent win conditions: combat damage, Surge to Victory copying, and Aether Gale tempo resets.
- **Tighter 1v1 speed**: replacing expensive multiplayer cards (CMC 5–10) with efficient 1–3 CMC removal and token generation makes the deck faster and more consistent.
- Resilient to removal: Bria coming back from the command zone still reactivates the whole engine.

**Weaknesses / Missing Staples:**
- **Stax / Rule of Law:** Enchantments that lock to one spell per turn (Rule of Law, Arcane Laboratory) shut the entire strategy down. The deck still has no enchantment removal.
- **Artifact removal depth:** Abrade + Resculpt + Prismari Command cover most threats, but the deck can still be overwhelmed by multiple key artifacts simultaneously.
- **No tutor for key creatures:** The deck can't consistently find Archmage Emeritus or Veyran. Mystical Tutor or Spellseeker would dramatically increase consistency.
- **Reduced sweepers:** Only Volcanic Torrent remains as a board-clear. If the opponent lands and protects multiple large threats before you can answer them, the deck may struggle to claw back.

## Suggested Deck Names

- **Rogue Wave** — A rogue wave is a massive, unpredictable surge that overwhelms without warning; ties to Bria (*Riptide Rogue*), the combat turns where you chain five spells and swing unblocked out of nowhere, and the chaotic Izzet energy underneath it all.
- **Through the Breach** — The whole deck is about forcing creatures through: Bria granting unblockable each spell, Surge to Victory opening lanes, Aether Gale clearing the board. Punchy and aggressive.
- **The Unstoppable Torrent** — Leans into Volcanic Torrent + the cascade/spell-chain game plan; describes both the flood of instants/sorceries and the combat wave that follows.
- **Chain Reaction** — The name describes the entire game plan: one spell triggers Archmage, which draws into another, which funds the next — an accelerating chain nobody can stop.
- **Nothing to Sea Here** — A pun in three directions: the ocean theme, the unblockable creatures slipping past defenders unseen, and the blank-faced innocence of casting five spells and saying "what, this? perfectly normal." Move along.
