# Smaug the Impenetrable — Rakdos Dragons & Treasure Commander

Commander: Smaug the Impenetrable ({5}{B}{R}, Black/Red)

## Overview
- **Strategy**: Smaug is the biggest Dragon in a deck of 23 of them. Cost reducers and Dragon-only mana (Dragonspeaker Shaman, Dragonlord's Servant, Herald's Horn, Orb of Dragonkind) put 4- to 7-drops on the table early, and Treasures from Smaug's own trigger and a dozen other sources pay for the rest. Damage aimed at your own indestructible commander (Dragon Tempest, Scourge of Valkas, the burn suite, Leyline Tyrant) becomes still more Treasures, and the pod dies to flying Dragons, spread damage (Parapet Thrasher, Drakuseth, Firespitter Whelp, Goldlust Triad's myriad) or Exsanguinate off the hoard.
- **Intended for**: Multiplayer pod — four sweepers (two of them, Seismic Rupture and Breath Weapon, leave your Dragons standing), group-scaling reach, a monarch engine, an answer to opposing Dragons, and removal that also hits artifacts and enchantments.
- **Card pool**: Avoid reserved decks — nothing from Full Deployment, Claws for Concern, Table Manners or Dance of the Elements; spare copies of cards those decks use are still eligible (Swiftfoot Boots, for instance, has two free copies).

## Lore

Heroes arrive daily to slay Smaug and leave daily as cautionary tales. He yawns, and every dragon in the sky answers the dinner bell. *Treasure is nice, but a proper horde makes the hoard.*

## Key Synergies

- **Dragon cost reducers + Smaug**: Dragonspeaker Shaman ({2} less), Dragonlord's Servant, Herald's Horn and Hazoret's Monument each knock mana off Smaug, who is red, black and a Dragon. With the first two he costs {2}{B}{R}; with all four he costs {B}{R}. They cut every other Dragon in the deck the same way, and Orb of Dragonkind pays for the rest.
- **Dragon enter-triggers aimed at your own Smaug**: Scourge of Valkas and Dragon Tempest each deal damage equal to your Dragon count whenever a Dragon enters; Smaug is indestructible, so that is X Treasures per Dragon. Lathliss, Dragonmaster Outcast, Skyline Despot, Manaform Hellkite and Goldlust Triad's myriad copies all supply more entering Dragons.
- **Burn + Smaug**: Lightning Bolt, Burst Lightning, Tweeze, Stingerquill Charm, Spicy Oatmeal Pizza, The Black Arrow, Fiery Annihilation, Leyline Tyrant, Smaug the Magnificent and Drakuseth can all put damage on Smaug at will, so the Treasures keep coming even when no Dragons are entering. Each is also removal or reach, so none is ever a dead card.
- **Parapet Thrasher + Smaug**: any Dragon that connects lets Thrasher deal 4 damage to **each other** opponent (8 across a three-opponent pod), destroy an artifact, or impulse-draw. Goldlust Triad's myriad copies trigger it again for each opponent they hit.
- **Thundermane Dragon + a top-heavy creature suite**: 17 of your creatures have power 4 or more, so Thundermane Dragon casts them off the top of the library with haste.
- **Seismic Rupture / Breath Weapon + a deck of fliers and Dragons**: both sweepers one-sidedly kill the ground game of an opposing pod. Only your six small non-flying, non-Dragon creatures are at risk.
- **Treasure hoard + Exsanguinate / Shivan Devastator**: when the hoard gets large, Exsanguinate is a table-wide drain and Shivan Devastator is a hasty finisher, both sized by however many Treasures you made.

## Short Mulligan and Play Notes

- **Early game (turns 1–4)**: Keep two- and three-landers with a rock or a cost reducer. Sol Ring, a Signet or Mind Stone, Sarkhan (a Treasure for revealing a Dragon) and Dragonspeaker Shaman are the plays that make a turn-four Dragon real. Ship one-landers — the curve is built around reaching five mana.
- **Mid game (turns 5–8)**: Land Smaug (or whichever Dragon the reducers made cheap) and attack. Each Dragon that enters with Dragon Tempest or Scourge of Valkas out is a free Treasure ping at Smaug, and Parapet Thrasher turns every hit into reach across the pod. Keep Seismic Rupture and Breath Weapon for the turn the pod has ground creatures and you have only fliers.
- **Late game**: Blasphemous Act is the reset; it also gives Smaug thirteen Treasures to rebuild with. Exsanguinate and Shivan Devastator convert a large hoard into a finish. Drakuseth, Terror of Mount Velus and Tyrant's Familiar close games from the air.
- **Key interactions**:
  - Smaug's trigger is **noncombat** damage only; combat damage and blockers make no Treasures.
  - Swiftfoot Boots stops targeted removal, but not sweepers, edicts or -X/-X. Thunderbreak Regent punishes the targeting itself.
  - Indestructible does not save Smaug from -X/-X, exile or sacrifice effects; Toxic Deluge-style spells still answer him. Haven of the Spirit Dragon is the recursion for the *other* Dragons.
  - Blasphemous Act, Chain Reaction and the burn kill your own Dragons as well. Plan around Atsushi, Midnight Reaper, Riveteers Requisitioner and Immersturm Predator, which pay you back or survive.

## Why These Choices (Summary)

- **Tribal first, Treasure second**: with 23 Dragons, cost reducers and Dragon-only mana rocks do real work, and Dragon enter-triggers (Dragon Tempest, Scourge of Valkas, Lathliss) turn every Dragon into value.
- **Ramp and fixing**: 8 mana rocks, four cost reducers (Dragonlord's Servant, Dragonspeaker Shaman, Herald's Horn, Hazoret's Monument) and a Treasure-making two-drop in Sarkhan sit on top of 35 lands. Red is the main colour (about 70 red pips to 9 black); black is limited to Smaug, Smaug, Wicked Worm, Immersturm Predator, Midnight Reaper and a few spells, and Treasures and Haven of the Spirit Dragon help fix it.
- **Pod-ready interaction**: four sweepers (two of them one-sided), removal that also hits artifacts and enchantments (Abrade, Chaos Warp, Burn, Burn, Tree and Fern, Parapet Thrasher), and The Black Arrow as a clean answer to an opposing Dragon.
- **Group win conditions**: Parapet Thrasher, Drakuseth, Firespitter Whelp, Goldlust Triad's myriad, Exsanguinate and Dragonhawk all scale with the number of opponents, and the monarch engine of Skyline Despot keeps the cards coming.
- **Notable omissions**: planeswalkers (none in the collection fit Black/Red), counterspells, mass artifact/enchantment removal and a second hexproof piece — see below.

## Analysis

**Strengths**:
- A commander immune to damage-based wipes, and a deck that does not need him: almost any Dragon is a threat, so removal on Smaug costs the pod a card but not you the game.
- Heavy cost reduction and ramp make the 4–7 drops that dominate the list castable a turn or two early.
- Several independent win conditions (flying Dragons, spread damage, Exsanguinate off the hoard) plus a monarch engine for the long game.
- Resilient to sweepers: Atsushi, Midnight Reaper, Riveteers Requisitioner, Immersturm Predator, Hoarding Dragon and Blasphemous Act's Treasures all pay out when the board is cleared.

**Weaknesses/Missing Staples**:
- **Mana demands**: RR is everywhere and RRR shows up in Scourge of Valkas and Drakuseth, with BB in Exsanguinate. Counting fetchlands, roughly 26 red and 18 black sources, plus Treasures, are enough but not generous, and seven lands enter tapped.
- **Top-end weight**: four seven-drops (Terror of Mount Velus, Tyrant's Familiar, Drakuseth, Skyline Despot) plus Blasphemous Act. The reducers fix this only when you draw them.
- **Protection is thin**: Swiftfoot Boots and Thunderbreak Regent are all there is. Exile, edicts and -X/-X still answer Smaug.
- **Enchantment/artifact removal is spot-only**: Abrade, Chaos Warp, Burn, Burn, Tree and Fern and Parapet Thrasher answer one permanent at a time. Nevinyrral's Disk is the only mass answer in the pool, and it kills your Dragons too.
- **Graveyard hate and counters**: none included (Bojuka Bog and Soul-Guide Lantern are owned if the pod needs them), and there is little lifegain beyond Spicy Oatmeal Pizza and Exsanguinate.
