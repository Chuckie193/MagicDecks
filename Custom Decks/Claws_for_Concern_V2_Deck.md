# Claws for Concern V2 — Gruul Werewolf Tribal Commander

Commander: Tovolar, Dire Overlord // Tovolar, the Midnight Scourge ({1}{R}{G}, Red/Green)

## Overview
- **Strategy**: The Claws for Concern draft with **8 swaps from the collection**, aimed at its three biggest weaknesses: a night plan that relied entirely on Tovolar, no fliers, and Heroic Intervention as the only answer to a destroy-wipe. Cheap werewolves still flood the board, Tovolar's upkeep trigger flips the team to night, Garruk's Uprising gives everything trample and draws off 4-power creatures entering, Hollowhenge Overlord multiplies the board every upkeep, and Zopandrel and Unnatural Growth double power and toughness at every combat.
- **Intended for**: Both — a low curve (average CMC ~3.27, 20 cards at 1–2 mana) and 13 pieces of interaction for 1v1, with Hollowhenge Overlord's token engine and Avabruck Caretaker's team hexproof scaling into a pod.
- **Card pool**: Avoid reserved decks — every swap-in has a spare copy outside Full Deployment, Claws for Concern, Table Manners, Hoard Mentality, Dance of the Elements and Multiverse Reforged. No new purchases beyond the original draft's 18.

## Lore

The moon used to wait for the pack. Not any more. Now it rises on command, the sky holds teeth of its own, and the old guard's swapped claws for sharper ones. *Something's afoot — four of them, actually.*

### Commander (1)
- **Tovolar, Dire Overlord // Tovolar, the Midnight Scourge** {1}{R}{G} · **Red/Green** — Draws a card whenever a Werewolf or Wolf you control deals combat damage to a player. At your upkeep with three or more Wolves/Werewolves it becomes night — flipping every daybound werewolf automatically — **and then** transforms any number of your old-border Human Werewolves. ## Key Synergies

- **Garruk's Uprising + any two anthems = every Wolf token draws a card.** Garruk's checks power **as a creature enters, with anthems already applied**, and the deck runs five +1/+1 anthems for Wolves — Immerwolf, Howlpack Alpha, Nightpack Ambusher, Lord of the Ulvenwald and Howlpack Resurgence. With any two out, a 2/2 Wolf token **enters as a 4/4 and draws you a card.** Hollowhenge Overlord making six to nine tokens at your upkeep then draws six to nine cards.
- **Hollowhenge Overlord + a developed board**: a 2/2 Wolf for **each Wolf or Werewolf you control**, every upkeep, and those tokens count themselves next turn. Flash dodges sorcery-speed removal.
- **Three ways to make it night**: Tovolar's upkeep trigger, Unnatural Moonrise and **Into the Night** — the last needs no board at all. If Tovolar is removed, the plan no longer dies with him.
- **Halana and Alena + any doubler**: at the beginning of combat Halana and Alena puts +1/+1 counters equal to their power on another creature and gives it haste. Order that trigger **before** Zopandrel's or Unnatural Growth's and the counters are doubled with everything else. It also gives haste to a freshly cast Hollowhenge Overlord or Avabruck Caretaker, stacking with Rhythm of the Wild.
- **Avabruck Caretaker + Immerwolf**: flip the Caretaker once and Immerwolf locks it on its night side, so **all your other permanents have hexproof for the rest of the game**. This is mutually exclusive with repeat value from Ulrich, Huntmaster and Daybreak Ranger, which all want to keep flipping. Choose one plan per game.
- **Zopandrel + Unnatural Growth**: both double at the beginning of each combat and compound, so a 2/2 Wolf attacks as an 8/8 — or 16/16 carrying two anthems. They fail to *different* answers — creature removal versus enchantment removal. Respond to the trigger with Kessig Wolf Run or Tovolar's `{X}{R}{G}` and the pump gets doubled too.
- **Full Moon's Rise + Heroic Intervention**: two separate answers to a destroy-based wipe. Full Moon's Rise regenerates **every Werewolf** and costs nothing to hold up once it's on the battlefield; Heroic Intervention covers everything else.
- **Rhythm of the Wild + the top end**: riot means Hollowhenge Overlord, Avabruck Caretaker, Aerid Konstrari, Ulrich and every other five- and six-drop attacks the turn it lands.
- **Immerwolf + The Celestus**: The Celestus loots whenever day and night *change*, and Immerwolf keeps your non-Human Werewolves on their night faces while it changes underneath them — night stats *and* the loot.

## Short Mulligan and Play Notes

- **Mulligan to *deploy and protect* Tovolar, not to find him.** You want mana to cast him turn 3–4, two or three cheap bodies for his threshold, and ideally Air Shoes.
- **Early (1–3)**: Llanowar Elves or Sol Ring into a three-drop — there are twenty. Garruk's Uprising or Growing Rites on turn three are the best non-Tovolar starts.
- **Mid (4–6)**: Avabruck Caretaker is the highest-value six-drop. Hollowhenge Overlord starts compounding from the upkeep after it lands. Halana and Alena on turn four makes every later threat hasty.
- **Late (7+)**: Either doubler ends the game on a wide board.
- **Into the Night counts as a spell.** Cast it plus one more spell on your turn and it becomes day again next turn. Cast it alone, or cast it on a turn when Tovolar's trigger will re-flip it at your next upkeep anyway.
- **Hold Moonrager's Slash and Konstrari Charm for opponents' turns.** Spells cast on someone else's turn never count towards day/night, and Moonrager's Slash costs one mana while it's night.
- **The Immerwolf decision is the deck's biggest piloting call.** It locks your night-side stats in, but permanently switches off **three** flip-back effects: Huntmaster's Wolf-and-life, Ulrich's fight and Daybreak Ranger's pinger. Play it when you want the board frozen at its best.
- **Don't put +1/+1 counters on Pyreheart Wolf.** Wilderland Scrounger, Ranger Class L2, Hound Tamer, Packsong Pup, Arlinn's +1, **Halana and Alena** and **Konstrari Charm's counters mode** all permanently disable its undying. Riot on Rhythm of the Wild should be **haste** for it.
- **Full Moon's Rise regenerates Werewolves only.** True Wolves and Wolf tokens still die to a destroy-wipe — hold Heroic Intervention if they're the bulk of the board.
- **The Celestus is a toggle.** If it's already night, activating it turns it back to **day**. It also makes it day on entry if it's neither.
- **Realmwalker's naming choice is real**: Wolf gets you Hollowhenge Overlord and Pyreheart Wolf off the top; Werewolf gets you the 23-card half.
- **Day/night, stated correctly**: it becomes night when the active player casts **zero** spells during their own turn; one spell changes nothing; two or more flips it back to day.
- **Key rules**: daybound permanents can't be transformed by any effect, so Moonmist and Tovolar's manual clause only reach the 8 old-border werewolves. A werewolf **transforming is not entering**, so Garruk's Uprising draws off creatures you *cast*.

## Analysis

**Strengths**
- **The night plan survives losing Tovolar.** Into the Night makes it night with no conditions and never reverses.
- **Garruk's Uprising plus two anthems turns every Wolf token into a card.** With Hollowhenge Overlord that is six to nine cards an upkeep.
- **An answer to fliers, and a flier of its own**: Konstrari Charm deals 6 to a flier, Halana and Alena add a second reach body beside Zopandrel, and Aerid Konstrari is the deck's first flying threat.
- **Two answers to destroy-wipes** — Heroic Intervention and Full Moon's Rise.
- Team hexproof from Avabruck Caretaker, locked permanently by Immerwolf.
- Two P/T doublers that fail to different answers, plus permanent team trample.
- Interaction rises from 11 to 13 cards, and both new removal spells are instants.

**Weaknesses**
- **Fewer tribal bodies.** 31 Wolves and Werewolves plus Realmwalker (32), down from 38 — below The Bark Ages' 35. Tovolar's three-creature threshold and Hollowhenge Overlord's token count are still easy to reach with tokens, but Master of the Wild Hunt now has **eight** true Wolves to tap rather than nine.
- **Heroic Intervention and Full Moon's Rise both answer destruction only.** Toxic Deluge, Cyclonic Rift, Farewell and edicts still wipe the board, and with 39 creatures and no counterspells **a resolved non-destroy sweeper still ends the game.**
- **Zero counterspells, discard or graveyard hate.** An instant-speed combo cannot be interacted with.
- **Twenty of 62 nonland cards cost exactly three**, so the first four turns still look similar most games.
- Of the 13 interaction pieces, only Beast Within, Chaos Warp and Moonrager's Slash have no board requirement.

**Power level**: **Bracket 3, low-to-mid** — unchanged. V2 is more resilient (it no longer folds to a single removal spell on Tovolar) rather than more powerful.
