# Ygra, Eater of All — Golgari Food Aristocrats Commander

Commander: Ygra, Eater of All (3 generic, Black, Green — Black/Green)

## Overview
- **Strategy**: Ygra rewrites the battlefield so that *every other* creature — yours and your opponents' — is a Food artifact. That does two things at once: any creature dying while she is on the battlefield puts two +1/+1 counters on her, and every "destroy target artifact" effect becomes unconditional creature removal in a colour pair that normally cannot do that. Grind incremental drain while Ygra balloons, then close with a trampling, Zopandrel-doubled commander swing.
- **Intended for**: Multiplayer pod
- **Card pool**: Avoid reserved decks (Full Deployment, Squirreled Away, Dance of the Elements)

## Lore

Ygra pads through the battlefield and every hero starts smelling faintly of *lunch*. Armies become appetisers. Champions become leftovers. Her pack serves each course gladly. Nobody leaves the table until she *cleans her plate*.

## Decklist (100 cards)

### Commander (1)
- **Ygra, Eater of All** — Makes every *other* creature a Food artifact with a built-in sacrifice ability, and grows +2/+2 whenever any Food hits a graveyard from the battlefield. She is **not** a Food herself, which matters twice: she does not trigger on her own death, and she survives effects that destroy all artifacts.

### Creatures (32)
- **Llanowar Elves** — Turn-one ramp into a turn-two three-drop; later, disposable Food.
- **Studious First-Year // Rampant Growth** — A {G} 1/1 Bear that enters *prepared*, letting you cast a copy of Rampant Growth for {1}{G}. **Cast the land copy before you sacrifice the Bear** — the copy is exiled only while the creature stays on the battlefield and prepared, so eating it early destroys the ramp. Also the deck's only Bear, which switches on Mirkwood's otherwise-dead ability.
- **Cankerbloom** — A 2-mana 3/2 that sacrifices itself to destroy an artifact. Under Ygra that reads "destroy any creature on the table", and the sacrifice feeds Ygra and Vengeful Bloodwitch.
- **Outland Liberator // Frenzied Trapbreaker** — Same trick for {1}, on a 2/2 body. The night face repeats it on every attack.
- **Leech Collector // Bloodletting** — Becomes prepared the first time you gain life each turn, then {B} casts a copy of Bloodletting: **each opponent loses 2**. Vengeful Bloodwitch and Arnyn gain you life off sacrifices you are already making, and Vampire Spawn, Hazel's Nocturne, Pull from the Grave, Illegitimate Business and every Food sacrifice all do it too — so it comes online essentially every turn. It must survive on the battlefield to hold the copy, and can only prepare once per turn.
- **Duskwatch Recruiter // Krallenhorde Howler** — {2}{G} digs three deep for a creature. A flood outlet and consistency engine in a 32-creature deck.
- **Bakersbane Duo** — A **Squirrel** that makes a Food token on entry; feeds Ygra and Honored Dreyleader twice over.
- **Vinereap Mentor** — A Squirrel making Food on entry *and* on death, so it triggers Ygra twice across its life.
- **Courier of Comestibles** — Tutors a Food card, or creates a Food token if it finds nothing.
- **Thornvault Forager** — A Squirrel mana dork whose forage cost (sacrifice a Food) becomes "sacrifice any creature for two mana" once Ygra lands.
- **Wood Elves** — Fetches a Forest **onto the battlefield untapped**: real ramp, real fixing, and a body that is later Food.
- **Ice Cream Kitty** — Literally an Artifact Creature Food. Its draw outlet is sorcery-speed only, so use it proactively, never as a response to removal.
- **Hungry Ghoul** — Cheap repeatable sacrifice outlet that grows each time it eats.
- **Reassembling Skeleton** — The engine's best fodder: returns itself from the graveyard for {1}{B}. It has **no sacrifice ability of its own**, so the loop needs one of Hungry Ghoul, Ice Cream Kitty, Tom Bert and William, Technodrome or Zopandrel alongside it.
- **Vengeful Bloodwitch** — Drains a *single* target opponent whenever any creature you control dies, **and gains you 1 life** — which is what turns Leech Collector on each turn.
- **Druid of the Cowl** — Green ramp on a body that later becomes Food.
- **Noxious Newt** — Deathtouch blocker that also ramps; excellent defensive card in a pod.
- **The Sackville-Bagginses** — Sacrifice outlet on entry, plus a drain trigger every time you sacrifice a token.
- **Rat King, Verminister** — At *your* end step, if one of *your* permanents left the battlefield, makes a Rat and puts a +1/+1 counter on itself. Its second ability — **{T}, Sacrifice three Rats: return a creature card and every card with that name from your graveyard to the battlefield tapped** — becomes a reanimation engine once it has made a few Rats.
- **Gollum the Abandoned** — Drains each opponent 2 on entry and can't block. Its sacrifice ability is a *graveyard* recursion cost, not a battlefield outlet.
- **Technodrome** — "{T}, sacrifice another artifact: draw a card **and put a +1/+1 counter on this creature**" reads as "sacrifice any creature: draw" under Ygra, and each counter is also a Terrasymbiosis trigger. It **can't attack or block unless its power is 6 or greater** — but three activations take it from 3/3 to 6/6, at which point it becomes a real trampling body.
- **Azog, Moria's Ruin** — Point it at an opponent for removal, or at your own Reassembling Skeleton for an Ygra trigger, a Bloodwitch drain, a Ninja Teen drain, an Army token and a card.
- **Arnyn, Deathbloom Botanist** — Deathtouch blocker draining a target opponent 2 when a creature of yours with power *or* toughness ≤1 dies. **Twelve creatures in the deck qualify** — three of the four mana dorks (Llanowar Elves, Druid of the Cowl, Noxious Newt, but *not* the 2/2 Thornvault Forager), Reassembling Skeleton, Vengeful Bloodwitch, Courier, Ice Cream Kitty, Azog, Rat King, Honored Dreyleader, Wood Elves and Studious First-Year — plus every Rat, Insect and Offspring token.
- **Honored Dreyleader** — Enters with a counter for each other **Squirrel and/or Food** you control, then grows on each one that enters. The deck has three other Squirrels, so it is strong even without Ygra; under her, every creature you control counts.
- **Vampire Spawn** — Drains 2 from each opponent on entry.
- **Eccentric Farmer** — Mills for value and returns a land; the mill feeds your recursion.
- **Thrashing Brontodon** — Sacrifice outlet stapled to artifact/enchantment removal — any creature under Ygra.
- **Elvish Regrower** — Returns any permanent from the graveyard.
- **Thornplate Intimidator** — Punisher forcing a sacrifice, discard, or 3 life. Offspring makes a **token copy**, so you get the punisher trigger **twice**. Ygra does give opponents an easy sacrifice, softening the punish.
- **Lord Dregg, Insect Invader** — Flying token generator. Its draw outlet costs **{3}{G}, sacrifice a token** — four mana a card, the worst sac rate here; use it only with spare mana.
- **Tom, Bert, and William** — **{1}, sacrifice another creature**: draw cards equal to its power, then discard one. Returns itself as an artifact when it dies.
- **Zopandrel, Hunger Dominus** — Doubles your whole board's power and toughness at *every* combat. Its second ability, **{G/P}{G/P}, sacrifice two other creatures: put an indestructible counter on Zopandrel**, is a near-free sacrifice outlet (payable with 4 life) that also protects it.

### Enchantments (3)
- **Ninja Teen** — A Class. Level 1: every creature **you control** that leaves the battlefield drains all three opponents. Level 2 ({1}{B}): your creatures get **+1/+0 and menace**, which directly supports the Zopandrel combat kill. Level 3 ({B}): creature cards in your graveyard gain sneak, letting you cast them from there.
- **Terrasymbiosis** — Whenever you put one or more +1/+1 counters on a creature **you control**, draw that many, once each turn. Ygra puts **two** counters on herself per Food death, and "once each turn" means once per *player* turn — up to 8 cards a round in a four-player pod. It is not commander-dependent either: Honored Dreyleader's ETB (often 3–5 counters at once), Hungry Ghoul, Technodrome, Rat King, Snakeskin Veil, Cankerbloom's proliferate mode, Oakhollow Village and Ronin III all trigger it.
- **The Last Ronin** — A three-turn plan, not a one-shot. **I**: destroys all creatures, **including Ygra** — see the Play Notes for the Overprotect line that makes it one-sided. **II**: mill four and return a creature from your graveyard to hand, rebuilding after your own wipe. **III**: whenever a creature attacks alone, it gets **three +1/+1 counters, trample, lifelink and indestructible** — on a solo-attacking Ygra that is a second win condition, and three more Terrasymbiosis cards.

## Key Synergies
- **Ygra turns green artifact removal into unconditional creature removal**: she grants every *other* creature the artifact type, so Seedship Impact, Cankerbloom, Outland Liberator and Thrashing Brontodon all become "destroy any creature". Golgari does not normally get catch-all removal at these rates, and all four still hit real artifacts and enchantments when she is absent.
- **The Last Ronin + Overprotect + Supper for Spiders — the deck's best turn, and it costs 10 mana**: Chapter I destroys *all* creatures and Ygra has no innate protection, so by default she dies with the board and gets **no counters**. Hold Overprotect ({1}{G}) and cast it in response to the Chapter I trigger: Ygra survives with indestructible, everything else dies, she gets +2/+2 per creature, and Supper for Spiders ({1}{B}) then takes everything that died from all three graveyards. Ronin costs 6, so the full line needs 10 mana in one turn — 8 gets the one-sided wipe, 10 gets the corpses. Do not have Power Sneakers equipped; its shroud stops you targeting her. (Snakeskin Veil's hexproof does *not* save her here — Ronin doesn't target.)
- **Ygra + Terrasymbiosis**: Two counters per Food death, and Terrasymbiosis fires once per *player* turn — a pod where creatures die every turn draws you up to eight extra cards a round off a three-mana enchantment.
- **Ygra + Technodrome / Thornvault Forager**: Their costs read "sacrifice another artifact" and "sacrifice a Food". Under Ygra, every creature you control satisfies both.
- **Ygra + Reassembling Skeleton + a sacrifice outlet + Vengeful Bloodwitch**: Each turn, pay {1}{B} to return the Skeleton, then feed it to any outlet — drain an opponent for 1, gain 1 life (arming Leech Collector), and put two counters on Ygra (drawing off Terrasymbiosis). Note this is a **four-card** engine: the Skeleton has no sacrifice ability itself, so the real cost is {1}{B} plus the outlet's activation.
- **Ygra + Zopandrel + Sword of Vengeance**: A 12/12 Ygra equipped precombat is 14/12, doubled at beginning of combat to a **28/24 trampler** with haste.

## Short Mulligan and Play Notes
- **Early game (turns 1–4)**: Keep hands with two lands and a ramp piece. Llanowar Elves, Studious First-Year, Druid of the Cowl, Wood Elves, Sol Ring and Arcane Signet all get Ygra out early. Don't over-commit creatures before you have a sacrifice outlet.
- **Mid game (turns 5–8)**: Land Ygra and look for Reassembling Skeleton, Terrasymbiosis or a Food producer. Once she is online, let the table fight — every creature that dies grows her. Hold Snakeskin Veil or Overprotect for the first removal aimed at her.
- **Late game**: Two routes. Grind with Vengeful Bloodwitch, Ninja Teen and Leech Collector, or set up the combat kill — Sword of Vengeance for trample, Zopandrel to double her, swing for 21+ commander damage. Ninja Teen level 2 adds menace to push the last points through.
- **The marquee line (10 mana)**: The Last Ronin chapter I → respond with Overprotect to save Ygra → she eats the whole board for counters → Supper for Spiders reclaims the corpses.
- **Prepared cards are not sacrifice fodder**: Studious First-Year and Leech Collector each hold their spell copy in exile **only while they remain on the battlefield and prepared**. Cast the copy first; eat the body afterwards. This is the easiest misplay in the deck.
- **Destroy vs. exile — know which you want**: Eaten Alive, Bogslither's Embrace and Gnashing of Teeth's -5/-5 mode all **exile**, which means no Food hits the graveyard, so Ygra gets **no counters**, Terrasymbiosis does not fire, and the creature is not there for Supper for Spiders. Reach for destroy effects (Infernal Grasp, Stir Up Trouble, Fell-style kills, Seedship Impact) when you want the growth; save the exile effects for creatures with death triggers, recursion or indestructible. This is the same tension that makes Head of the Hunt a trap.
- **Key interactions**: Ygra's ability applies to opponents' creatures too, so their deaths grow her *and* their creatures are legal targets for your artifact removal. Two costs, both smaller than they look: her ward ("sacrifice a Food") and the granted sacrifice outlet both require the opponent to put a Food in the graveyard — **which still gives Ygra +2/+2**. And the granted ability costs `{2}, {T}`, so a summoning-sick or tapped creature cannot use it to dodge removal.

## Why These Choices (Summary)
- **Ygra converts narrow cards into premium ones**: four pieces of green artifact removal double as unconditional creature removal, which is the deck's answer to having almost no sweepers.
- **Sacrifice outlets over raw power**: Reassembling Skeleton, Hungry Ghoul, Technodrome, Tom Bert and William, and Zopandrel's second ability are all repeatable.
- **Drain payoffs, honestly rated**: Ninja Teen and Leech Collector hit all three opponents and genuinely scale with pod size. Vengeful Bloodwitch and Arnyn hit a *single* target opponent — consistent, not scaling — but Arnyn has twelve in-deck enablers, so it is far more live than its single-target text suggests.
- **Mana base built around a thin pool**: every real Golgari dual you own is inside the reserved Squirreled Away precon, leaving 10 usable nonbasics. The deck compensates with 26 basics weighted 14/12 toward black to match a 38:30 pip ratio, plus four mana creatures, two rocks, Wood Elves and Studious First-Year.

## Analysis

**Strengths**:
- Ygra profits from other players' removal and combat; in a four-player pod she grows without you spending cards.
- She unlocks a removal suite Golgari cannot normally access, partly compensating for the missing sweepers.
- Terrasymbiosis is a repeatable draw engine keyed directly to the commander's trigger.
- Very low curve — 36 of 64 nonland cards cost 2 or less, average mana value 2.63 — so the deck functions on four or five lands.
- Two cheap protection spells (Snakeskin Veil, Overprotect) for a commander the plan depends on.

**Weaknesses/Missing Staples**:
- **Board wipes — the big one**: exactly **one** true sweeper (The Last Ronin), against the 3–5 the pod guidance calls for. Decree of Pain, Plaguecrafter and Plumb the Forbidden are all in **Squirreled Away**; **Bane of Progress** is in **Dance of the Elements**. A forced omission of the chosen pool, not a card-quality judgement.
- **Removal is mostly single-target**: 16 of 64 nonland cards are answers, but only 9 of those do nothing else — the rest are bodies or Foods first. Against three opponents you still cannot 1-for-1 your way out.
- **Four removal pieces are Ygra-dependent** for hitting creatures: Seedship Impact, Cankerbloom, Outland Liberator, Thrashing Brontodon. Three of the four leave a body regardless, so only Seedship Impact has a genuine dead-card floor.
- **Mana base**: 10 nonbasic lands, none untapped duals; Escape Tunnel produces no mana at all.
- **Food payoffs**: Academy Manufactor and Bastion of Remembrance are locked in Squirreled Away, which is why the deck leans on drain creatures rather than a dedicated Food engine.

## Next Steps (Optional Suggestions)
- **The single best upgrade is dismantling Dance of the Elements for Bane of Progress.** "Destroy all artifacts and enchantments" under Ygra is a **one-sided board wipe she survives** — she is explicitly not a Food herself — while she eats +2/+2 per creature destroyed. It is arguably the best card in the collection for this deck.
- If you dismantle **Squirreled Away** instead, take **Decree of Pain**, **Academy Manufactor** and **Plumb the Forbidden**, plus the eleven Golgari duals (Llanowar Wastes, Woodland Cemetery, Twilight Mire, Bojuka Bog, Temple of Malady and more) that would fix the mana base outright.
- **Wear Down** ({1}{G}: destroy *two* artifacts if you gift a card) is available and is the only 2-for-1 removal in the legal pool — worth testing over Gnashing of Teeth if your pod is creature-heavy rather than token-heavy.
- **Do not add Head of the Hunt**, despite how it reads. Replacing opponents' creature deaths with exile turns off Ygra's growth from all three opponents, halves Terrasymbiosis, and blanks Supper for Spiders.
- Re-run the Scryfall fetch with card-face parsing: `card_details.md` has empty oracle text and mana cost for all 59 double-faced/adventure cards, so any automated pass silently under-rates them. `cards_cache.json` has the data; only the markdown export is lossy.
