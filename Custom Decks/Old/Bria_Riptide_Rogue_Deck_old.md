# Bria, Riptide Rogue — Izzet Spellslinger Commander

Commander: Bria, Riptide Rogue (3 mana, U/R)

Overview
- Strategy: Spellslinger / Prowess commander. Use cheap instants/sorceries and value spells to trigger Bria's abilities: (1) give your creatures prowess (pump) and (2) make a creature unblockable each time you cast a noncreature spell. Win by repeatedly poking with evasive, buffed creatures and generating value from card draw, treasures, and artifact synergies.

Short mulligan and play notes
- Early game: lay rocks (Sol Ring/Signet), cast cheap cantrips (Opt, Iteration) to trigger Bria and grow your small creatures.
- Mid game: convert card advantage into board presence with treasure makers (Big Score, Unexpected Windfall) and big value spells (Dig Through Time).
- Attack steps: use Bria's unblockable trigger to punch through with a buffed creature each spell, then close with extra damage from prowess triggers.
- Protection: Lightning Greaves / Swiftfoot Boots keep Bria or a key creature safe and allow repeated unblockable attacks.

Why these choices (summary)
- Focus on cheap noncreature spells and card advantage (Opt, Expressive Iteration, Dig, Deep Analysis) to constantly trigger Bria and prowess creatures.
- Artifact ramp (Sol Ring, Signet, Chalice, Cornucopia) enables high spell density and more triggers per turn.
- Evasion/protection (Greaves/Swiftfoot) turns Bria's unblockable trigger into consistent damage.
- Selected creatures either grow with spells (Brineborn, Coruscation Mage, Clinquant) or provide direct value on casting spells (Archmage Emeritus, Chrome Host Seedshark).
