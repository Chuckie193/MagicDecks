# Hazel_of_the_Rootbloom_Deck — Golgari Token Aristocrats Commander

Commander: Hazel of the Rootbloom ({2}{B}{G}, Black/Green)

## Overview
- **Strategy:** Hazel creates a self-sustaining engine: making Food tokens triggers Squirrel creation, and making Squirrel tokens triggers Food creation. Stack token-doubling effects (Chatterfang, Second Harvest, Garruk) to overwhelm the board, then drain opponents with aristocrat payoffs (Zulaport Cutthroat, Nadier's Nightblade, Bastion of Remembrance, Poison-Tip Archer) as tokens die in combat or are sacrificed. Skullclamp converts every 1/1 Squirrel into two cards; Shamanic Revelation draws twenty in a token flood.
- **Intended for:** Both (1v1 and multiplayer)

---

## Lore

Hazel's recipe: one acorn, one Squirrel, one Food — *stir until infinite*. The forest floor seethes as her army doubles and redoubles. Opponents trade their best creatures for a single 1/1, drain two life, and slowly realise — *there's no bottom to the bag.*

## Decklist (100 cards)

### Commander (1)
- **Hazel of the Rootbloom** ({2}{B}{G}, Legendary Elf Druid) — Hazel's triggered abilities create a self-sustaining loop: whenever you create a Food token, you create a Squirrel token, and vice versa. Every Food-generating card in the deck also produces Squirrels; every Squirrel-generating card produces Food. Academy Manufactor breaks this loop wide open.

### Creatures (27)

Hazel's Food trigger creates a Squirrel; that Squirrel trigger creates Food; Academy Manufactor intercepts each Food creation and also produces a Clue and a Treasure. ## Key Synergies

- **Hazel + Academy Manufactor**: Each Food creation now also creates a Clue and Treasure. Hazel converts the Food into a Squirrel; that Squirrel triggers Hazel to create a Food; Academy Manufactor intercepts that Food and also creates Clue + Treasure. With Chatterfang doubling each token creation, this chain generates several tokens per trigger cycle.
- **Skullclamp + 1/1 Tokens**: Equip Skullclamp to any 1/1 (it becomes a 0/1 and dies to the next damage source or sacrificed). When it dies, draw two cards. With Squirrel Nest + Hazel generating 2+ tokens per land tap, and Chatterfang doubling them, Skullclamp converts each tapped land into two cards drawn.
- **Zulaport Cutthroat + Nadier's Nightblade + Bastion of Remembrance + Poison-Tip Archer**: Stack all four for 4 life drain per token death. Swing wide, your tokens die in combat (or are sacrificed), and each death triggers all four payoffs. Opponents lose life faster than they can stabilize.
- **Chatterfang + Deep Forest Hermit**: Hermit creates 4 Squirrels on entry → Chatterfang creates 4 more → 8 Squirrels total → Hazel creates 8 Foods → 8 more Squirrel triggers → Chatterfang doubles again. When Hermit eventually dies from Vanishing, all those Squirrels are still in play.
- **Second Harvest + Any Token Army**: Instant-speed doubling of every token you control. In response to a board wipe, Second Harvest before the wipe resolves doubles your tokens while the opponent wastes their sweeper. Or cast before combat to swing with twice as many attackers.
- **Swarmyard Massacre + Maskwood Nexus**: With Maskwood Nexus, your creatures are every creature type including Squirrel. Swarmyard Massacre's -1/-1 effect excludes Squirrels — so ALL your creatures survive while opponent's standard creatures shrink. Board asymmetry that you win at most board states.

## Short Mulligan and Play Notes

- **Early game (turns 1–3):** Lead with Sol Ring or a Signet, then deploy Hazel on turn 3. Immediately play any Food generator (Gilded Goose, Tireless Provisioner) to start the loop. Skullclamp on turn 2 sets up card draw as soon as tokens arrive.
- **Mid game (turns 4–6):** Chatterfang doubles every token creation event — getting it in play before the loop starts is often the most important play. Layer aristocrat drains (Zulaport, Nadier's Nightblade) before swinging. Squirrel Nest on a land starts producing Squirrels every single turn.
- **Late game (turns 7+):** Shamanic Revelation when you have 12+ creatures draws a full grip; keep it all with Reliquary Tower. Swing wide to activate Beastmaster Ascension (7 creatures attacking = all are 6/6). Plumb the Forbidden converts a board of tokens into a game-winning hand.
- **Key interactions:**
  - Skullclamp kills any 1/1 on equip (0/1 base power). Don't wait — equip to Squirrels freely.
  - Chatterfang creates additional tokens equal to the original number. Two Foods created → two Squirrels. The doubling grows multiplicatively with Hazel's loop.
  - Swarmyard regenerates Squirrels (and all creatures via Maskwood Nexus) for free. Use aggressively.
  - Grim Backwoods sac outlet on a land is invisible to opponents and easy to miss — great way to draw in response to a board wipe.

## Why These Choices

- **Cuts focus on mana efficiency.** Beledros Witherbloom (8 mana for a 4/4 making tiny tokens), End-Raze Forerunners (8 mana "win-more"), and Decree of Pain (8 mana board wipe) were all cut for costing too much to reliably fit the engine.
- **Binding the Old Gods** is cut despite being versatile — a 3-chapter saga spread over three turns is too slow when the deck wants to explode on turn 4-5.
- **Moonstone Eulogist** depends on opponents' creatures dying (Blood tokens) rather than your own; in 1v1 it often underperforms.
- **Beast-Kin Ranger** solves the deck's biggest combat weakness: all your 1/1 tokens can be chump-blocked. Now every token enters with trample, dealing excess damage through blocks.
- **Hero's Downfall** fills the gap for cheap interaction that answers planeswalkers (critical in both 1v1 and multiplayer pods).
- **Clifftop Lookout** provides creature-based ramp that also smooths the land count. This deck runs 39 lands and wants to hit every land drop.
- **Reliquary Tower** is essential: Shamanic Revelation and Plumb the Forbidden can draw 15+ cards. Without unlimited hand size, you discard your engine.

## Analysis

**Strengths:**
- Hazel's engine is self-sustaining once established: no net mana cost to create both a Food and Squirrel token every cycle, meaning every activation of a Food generator also generates a Squirrel for free
- Multiple redundant drain effects (Zulaport, Nadier's Nightblade, Bastion, Poison-Tip Archer) mean opponents lose life even when attacking into your tokens favorably
- Resilient to targeted removal: the strategy doesn't rely on any single creature — losing Hazel slows the engine but Chatterfang and Squirrel Nest still produce tokens independently
- Academy Manufactor breaks the game: if it's in play when Hazel triggers, the token multiplication is effectively tripled

**Weaknesses/Missing Staples:**
- **Enchantment/artifact removal:** The deck has Putrefy, Tear Asunder, Haywire Mite, and Thrashing Brontodon, but nothing that hits multiple permanents at once. A Reclamation Sage or Caustic Caterpillar would add redundancy.
- **Flying blockers:** The deck has Arasta (Spider with reach) and Poison-Tip Archer (reach), but most tokens are ground creatures. Evasive opponents can attack freely over your army until End-Raze Forerunners-style effects come online.
- **Fast combo hate:** If an opponent plans to win on turn 4-5 with a compact combo, the deck has limited counterspell access and relies on Plaguecrafter and Windgrace's Judgment as disruptive pieces.

## Next Steps

- **Test Hazel's exact oracle text interaction with Academy Manufactor.** If the Food/Squirrel loop triggers Academy Manufactor's replacement effect, the token count could be exponentially larger than expected. Know before playing.
- **Squirrel Nest + Earthcraft (not owned):** If ever acquired, Earthcraft lets any creature tap to untap a basic land. Untap the Squirrel Nest enchanted land, make another Squirrel, untap, repeat. This is a known infinite loop — consider whether you want that in the deck.
- **Consider Genesis Chamber (not owned):** Creates a 1/1 Myr whenever any nontoken creature enters — both players. In a token deck, you benefit from opponents' creature plays too.

## Suggested Deck Names

- **Acorn Supremacy** — "Supremacy" captures the overwhelming token board state; "Acorn" grounds it in Squirrel flavor. Works as a pun on "corn" too.
- **Many Tiny Bites** — Describes both the combat strategy (a hundred small creatures) and the aristocrat drain (small, repeated life loss). Death by a thousand tiny nibbles.
- **Nuts About Tokens** — "Nuts" = acorns + enthusiastic obsession; the deck is literally nuts about making as many tokens as possible, and it's a little nuts to play against.
- **The Squirreling** — Plays on the precon name "Squirreled Away" with horror-movie energy ("The Shining"). Once Hazel's engine starts, there's no stopping the flood.
