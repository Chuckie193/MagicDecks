# Dr_Eggman_Deck — Grixis Artifact Villain Commander

Commander: Dr. Eggman (2 generic, Blue, Black, Red — Blue, Black, Red)

## Overview
- Strategy: Dr. Eggman churns out card advantage every end step while forcing opponents into a villainous choice — discard a card or watch you cheat a Construct, Robot, or Vehicle directly onto the battlefield for free. The deck loads your hand with big artifact threats (Egg Hammer, Tornado/Sonic's Biplane), accelerates via Treasure tokens, and closes games with Knuckles the Echidna's double-strike pressure or a Super State one-shot that hits every opponent simultaneously.
- Intended for: Multiplayer pod

## Lore

Dr. Eggman's robots materialize from nowhere while opponents scramble to contain endless steel. His machines arrive uninvited and uninstructable. *The Doctor is IN — appointments strictly optional, compliance thoroughly mandatory.*

## Key Synergies

- **Dr. Eggman + Egg Hammer (Myr Battlesphere)**: Hold the 7-mana Construct in hand; at your end step you draw, then each opponent faces the choice — discard a card or watch you put a 4/7 that creates four Myr tokens onto the battlefield for free. Either outcome is devastating.
- **Knuckles the Echidna + Knuckles's Gloves (The Reaver Cleaver)**: Equip the gloves on Knuckles and attack. With double strike and trample, even a 3-toughness blocker means Knuckles first-strikes for 3+, creating 3+ Treasures, then tramples through the rest. With three or four opponents, you can generate 10+ Treasures per turn and rapidly approach the 30-artifact win condition.
- **Dr. Eggman + Mischievous Mystic + Erudite Wizard**: Eggman draws you a card at your end step. Any other draw that turn — Opt, Quick Study, Thieving Otter — triggers both the Mystic (1/1 Flying Faerie token) and the Wizard (+1/+1 counter). Consistent incremental board growth and counter accumulation from the draw engine.
- **Vampire Neonate + Vampire Spawn + Bloodtithe Collector**: Activate Neonate to drain each opponent 1 life, then cast Vampire Spawn to drain each opponent 2 more. At your opponent's next end step, cast Bloodtithe Collector into a stacked discard — opponents now lost life twice this turn, so everyone discards again. Two rounds of forced discard in one sequence.
- **Eaten Alive + Myr Battlesphere / Dragon Fodder tokens**: Exile-based removal for 1 Black by sacrificing a disposable Myr or Goblin token. Answers indestructible commanders and other threats that destroy effects can't touch, at minimal cost.

## Short Mulligan and Play Notes

- **Early game (turns 1–3)**: Sol Ring or Starting Column on turn 1–2; drop Palladium Myr or equipment to protect Eggman; Vampire Neonate or Spectral Sailor provide early blockers and start triggering life-loss conditions. Keep hands with 3–4 lands, Sol Ring or Palladium Myr, and at least one big artifact (Egg Hammer or Tornado) to threaten with Eggman.
- **Mid game (turns 4–6)**: Cast Dr. Eggman with Lightning Greaves or Swiftfoot Boots ready; start forcing the villainous choice each end step. Drop Knuckles with Knuckles's Gloves if the coast is clear for a massive Treasure swing. Use Bloodtithe Collector + Vampire Neonate to compound discard pressure.
- **Late game (turn 7+)**: Eggman cheats Egg Hammer into play for free while opponents are hellbent; crew Tornado/Sonic's Biplane to dig for Super State; equip Super State to Eggman or Mouth of the Storm for a one-hit, table-damage kill. Knuckles tracking toward 30 artifacts is a surprise backup win opponents may ignore until too late.
- **Key interactions**: Eaten Alive uses your Myr or Goblin tokens as fuel — keep at least one disposable token in play for exile removal. Harmonized Crescendo scales best after Myr Battlesphere ETB (name "Myr" to draw 4). Dire Downdraft costs only [3] against attacking creatures — leave mana open after swinging.

## Why These Choices (Summary)

- **Construct/Robot/Vehicle suite is intentionally small**: The collection contains only four confirmed cheat targets (Barkform Harvester, Buzz Bots, Egg Hammer, Tornado). The strategy compensates by filling hands with draw spells, keeping threats fresh, and using Mouser Attack! to create Robot tokens. The threat of the cheat is often enough to force discards anyway.
- **Treasure subtheme**: Knuckles, Rapacious Dragon, Corsair Captain, Knuckles's Gloves, and Unexpected Windfall all generate Treasures. This accelerates Eggman's curve and builds toward Knuckles's 30-artifact alternate win without requiring dedicated combo pieces.
- **Discard synergy chain**: Vampire Neonate drains, Vampire Spawn drains harder on ETB, then Bloodtithe Collector converts the accumulated life loss into a forced discard from every opponent. This supplements Eggman's own villainous-choice discard.
- **No board wipes**: The Grixis portion of this collection lacks traditional sweepers. Mouth of the Storm's -3/-0 ETB and Dire Downdraft handle the most threatening attackers, but this deck's primary defence is Eggman pressure demanding answers every turn rather than reactive wiping.
- **Lands**: Swamp count is kept to 8 (the available maximum after precon reservation) and supplemented with four Uncharted Haven for three-colour flexibility.

## Analysis

**Strengths:**
- Guaranteed card advantage every end step from the commander with no setup required
- Villainous choice is always in your favour — opponents either run out of cards or you cheat in free artifacts
- Multiple win vectors: combat damage, Knuckles alternate win, Super State table wipe
- Treasure generation is abundant, enabling quick recovery from removal
- Pirate, Vampire, and Artifact creature synergies provide meaningful card-type groupings

**Weaknesses / Missing Staples:**
- **Board wipes**: No Blasphemous Act, Decree of Pain, or similar sweepers available in the pool; the deck struggles against go-wide token strategies
- **Cheat targets**: Only 4 Robot/Construct/Vehicle targets; Eggman's ability is weaker if he draws one and holds nothing to threaten with
- **Ramp density**: Sol Ring, Palladium Myr, Starting Column, and Treasure generation provide ramp but no land acceleration; colour-screwed hands can stall
- **Super State is slow**: Costs 7 mana and requires a safe creature plus another full turn to attack; combo-heavy pods can win before you assemble it

## Next Steps (Optional Suggestions)

- If more Sonic Secret Lair cards become available, prioritise acquiring additional Robots or Constructs to increase the cheat-target count to 6–8
- Consider adding any cheap artifact tutors that fit the Grixis identity (Whir of Invention, Transmute Artifact) if the collection expands

- Escape Tunnel's unblockable mode on Vampire Neonate or Riveteers Requisitioner can open combat damage triggers; keep it in mind when opponents have only large blockers
