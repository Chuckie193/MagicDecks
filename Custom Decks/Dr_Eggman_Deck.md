# Dr_Eggman_Deck — Grixis Robot Factory Commander

Commander: Dr. Eggman ({2}{U}{B}{R}, Blue/Black/Red)

## Overview
- **Strategy**: Dr. Eggman draws an extra card every end step, then each opponent makes a villainous choice: discard a card, or let you put a Construct, Robot or Vehicle from your hand onto the battlefield for free. The deck is packed with expensive Robots, Constructs and Vehicles, so every "let him have it" answer is a free 5–7 mana threat. Maskwood Nexus makes every creature card you own a Robot, and artifact ramp, draw and Fateful Discovery keep the factory running. The deck wins with a Myr Battlesphere swarm, a Super State one-shot across the table, a Depthshaker Titan alpha strike or Knuckles' 30-artifact alternate win, and a full suite of sweepers and spot removal keeps three opponents in check.
- **Intended for**: Multiplayer pod
- **Card pool**: Full collection

## Lore

Atop his flying fortress, the Doctor sips tea while his robot army does the dirty work. Every rival gets a choice: toss a secret, or watch another metal monster roll off the line. *Resistance is futile — and fully automated.*

## Key Synergies
- **Dr. Eggman + Maskwood Nexus**: Every creature card in your hand becomes a Robot, so each villainous choice can deploy Archon of Cruelty, Knuckles or Jhoira, not just artifact creatures.
- **Dr. Eggman + Thopter Fabricator**: Eggman's end-step draw is reliably your second draw each turn, so Fabricator makes a flying Thopter every turn without any other help.
- **Dr. Eggman + Depthshaker Titan / Myr Battlesphere**: The two 7-drops are the best "you chose wrong" punishment. A free Titan gives the whole artifact army melee, trample and haste for an immediate multiplayer swing.
- **Super State + any evasive creature**: 9/9 flying, first strike, trample and haste, and its combat damage to one opponent is dealt to every other opponent too. Two hits usually end a four-player game.
- **Knuckles + Myr Battlesphere + Sai/Hangarback + rocks**: Tokens, Treasures, Talismans and artifact lands stack up fast toward Knuckles' 30-artifact upkeep win.
- **Fateful Discovery + token makers**: Battlesphere alone draws five cards (itself plus four Myr).

## Short Mulligan and Play Notes
- **Early game**: Keep hands with 3+ lands and a rock or cost reducer. Turn 2–3 ramp (Sol Ring, Talismans, Signet, Foundry Inspector, Etherium Sculptor) lets you land Eggman on turn 3–4. Put Air Shoes or Power Sneakers on him as soon as you can.
- **Mid game**: Hold your big Robots and Constructs in hand rather than casting them, since each end step forces opponents to choose between discarding and giving you a free deploy. Cast cheap engines (Jhoira, Sai, Fateful Discovery, Scrawling Crawler) with your actual mana. Maskwood Nexus turns every creature card into valid ammo.
- **Late game**: Close with Super State on a flyer, a free Depthshaker Titan alpha strike, Battlesphere damage, or Knuckles at 30 artifacts. Steel Hellkite and Lich's Relic clear the way.
- **Key interactions**: Opponents choose discard vs. deployment, not you, so keep at least one Construct/Robot/Vehicle in hand to make the choice painful. Blasphemous Act and Decree of Pain kill your board too, so cast them when you're behind or when Hangarback/Buzz Bots/Interceptor make the trade favourable. Brilliance Unleashed and Buried Ruin rebuild afterwards.

## Why These Choices (Summary)
- **Cheat targets first**: Over twenty Robots, Constructs, Vehicles and changelings, weighted toward expensive ones (Battlesphere, Titan, Skysovereign, Thought Monitor, Cyberdrive Awakener) so the free deployment is worth the most mana.
- **Artifact-dense ramp**: Fourteen mana sources besides lands (rocks, Talismans, mana creatures, Caravan) plus three artifact lands. They fuel affinity, Improvise, Master of Etherium and Knuckles' count while ramping into Eggman.
- **Multiplayer interaction**: Four sweepers (Blasphemous Act, Chain Reaction, Decree of Pain, Steel Hellkite), Lich's Relic as a one-per-opponent wrath, eight spot removal spells, two counterspells, and Scrawling Crawler as group draw.
- **Multiple win routes**: Combat with an artifact army, a Super State table-wide hit, Myr Battlesphere direct damage, and Knuckles' alternate win.
- Built from the full collection, so it draws on Commander precons (Counter Intelligence contributes the most, 25 cards) and on currently reserved decks; see Analysis.

## Analysis

**Strengths**:
- Card advantage every turn from the commander, with a real punishment attached in a 4-player pod (three villainous choices per turn)
- Deep, mutually reinforcing artifact synergies: cost reducers, lords, draw engines and token makers
- Several independent win conditions, including a non-combat alt-win
- Strong multiplayer removal: Lich's Relic, Steel Hellkite and Super State all scale with the number of opponents

**Weaknesses/Missing Staples**:
- **Artifact hate**: A single Vandalblast-style effect or an artifact wipe hurts badly. Padeem's hexproof only stops targeted removal. Holding up Counterspell or Arcane Denial is the main defence.
- **Self-damage from sweepers**: The wipes hit your own robots. Time them carefully.
- **Few tutors**: Only Fabricate. Cards like Whir of Invention or Tezzeret the Seeker would help find Battlesphere/Maskwood.
- **Physical availability**: Built from the full collection, so `reserved_decks.md` was ignored. 32 cards here have no spare copy outside a reserved deck: mostly **Full Deployment** (e.g. Cyberdrive Awakener, Crystalline Crawler, Hangarback Walker, Jhoira, Arcane Denial and most of the artifact/Izzet lands), plus **Multiverse Reforged** (Archon of Cruelty, all three Talismans, most black duals), **Table Manners** (Decree of Pain, Infernal Grasp, Lich's Relic) and **Hoard Mentality** (Blasphemous Act, Feed the Swarm, Deadly Dispute, Firdoch Core, Temple of Malice). Sleeving this deck means dismantling or borrowing from those; `Dr_Eggman_Unreserved_Deck.md` is the buildable-today alternative.
