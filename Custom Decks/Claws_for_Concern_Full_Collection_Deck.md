# Claws for Concern: Off the Leash — Gruul Werewolf Tribal Commander

Commander: Tovolar, Dire Overlord // Tovolar, the Midnight Scourge (2RG, Red/Green)

## Overview
- **Strategy**: The Tovolar werewolf tribal aggro plan with **no card pool restrictions at all** — every reserved deck dismantled. Cheap werewolves flood the board, Tovolar's upkeep trigger flips the whole team to night, and Garruk's Uprising gives the pack permanent trample while drawing on every 4-power creature that enters. Zopandrel and Unnatural Growth double the board at each combat; Toski keeps drawing through a wrath.
- **Intended for**: Both — a low curve (average CMC ~3.11, 23 cards at 1–2 mana) and scaling removal for 1v1, while the doubling pair, Endurance, Fury and Shamanic Revelation all scale into a pod.
- **Card pool**: **Full collection.** All three reserved decks — Full Deployment, Squirreled Away and Dance of the Elements — assumed dismantled. This is a *hypothetical* build: sleeving it costs you all three.
- **This is the third of three variants.** See the comparison table below.

## Lore

The moon comes up and the fence comes down. Every leash in the woods snaps at once — bark, ember and something small and indestructible that will not stop taking notes. Tovolar doesn't lead the pack tonight. *He's just the loudest one in it.*

## Decklist (100 cards)

### Commander (1)
- **Tovolar, Dire Overlord // Tovolar, the Midnight Scourge** — Draws a card whenever a Werewolf or Wolf you control deals combat damage to a player. At your upkeep with three or more Wolves/Werewolves it becomes night (flipping every daybound werewolf automatically) **and then** transforms any number of your old-border Human Werewolves. Night side is a 4/4 with `{X}{R}{G}: target Wolf or Werewolf gets +X/+0 and trample`.

- **Beorn the Fierce** — 6/6 trampler that permanently converts one creature into a Bear each combat, handing it a trample counter and his own "other Bears get +2/+2." At three Bears he draws **two cards a turn** — and Realmwalker is a changeling, therefore already a Bear, so that can be his first combat.
- **Breakneck Rider // Neck Breaker** — Old-border werewolf; night side gives attackers +1/+0 and trample.
- **Burly Breaker // Dire-Strain Demolisher** — 6/5 ward {1}, 8/7 ward {3} at night. The biggest body and the best Selvala target.
- **Child of the Pack // Savage Packmate** — Daybound werewolf that pays {2}{R}{G} for a Wolf; night side tramples and anthems +1/+0.
- **Daybreak Ranger // Nightfall Predator** — Pings fliers by day, repeatable fight machine by night.

- **Fury** — `{3}{R}{R}` double striker, or **free** by exiling a red card. On entry it deals **4 damage divided** among any number of creatures and planeswalkers. The evoke fodder is your own red werewolves, so "free" has a real cost.

- **Hound Tamer // Untamed Pup** — Trampler with a {3}{G} counter sink; night side gives Wolves and Werewolves trample.

- **Huntmaster of the Fells // Ravager of the Fells** — Wolf and 2 life on every flip to day, burn on every flip to night. Immerwolf switches the flip-back half off permanently — and with Cult of the Waxing Moon gone, that is now Immerwolf's **only** cost.

- **Immerwolf** — Anthem for Wolves/Werewolves (+1/+1, intimidate) that locks non-Human Werewolves on their night side. With Cult of the Waxing Moon gone its only remaining cost is that it permanently switches off Huntmaster of the Fells' flip-back-to-day Wolf-and-2-life trigger — a much smaller price than in the other two builds, so here it is close to an auto-include.

- **Kessig Naturalist // Lord of the Ulvenwald** — Mana on attack by day; a Wolf/Werewolf lord at night.
- **Kruin Outlaw // Terror of Kruin Pass** — First strike by day; double strike at night, and **Werewolves** gain menace (Wolf tokens do not).
- **Llanowar Elves** — Turn-one acceleration into a turn-two three-drop; the deck has twenty.
- **Mayor of Avabruck // Howlpack Alpha** — Human anthem by day (Realmwalker counts), Wolf/Werewolf anthem plus an end-step Wolf by night.
- **Migloz, Maze Crusher** — Three-mana 4/4 with five oil counters for vigilance/menace, +2/+2, or artifact/enchantment removal.
- **Nightpack Ambusher** — Flash lord making a Wolf at every end step you didn't cast a spell — and the only *free* route to night without Tovolar.
- **Outland Liberator // Frenzied Trapbreaker** — Cheap artifact/enchantment removal on a body.
- **Packsong Pup** — Two-mana Wolf that grows a +1/+1 counter every combat for free, feeds Tovolar's threshold, and turns on Wargling's ferocious and Werewolf Pack Leader's pack tactics.
- **Realmwalker** — Changeling: a Wolf, a Werewolf, a Human for Mayor's anthem and a Bear for Beorn. Name Werewolf and cast creatures off the top of your library.
- **Reckless Stormseeker // Storm-Charged Slasher** — +1/+0 and haste each combat by day, +2/+0 and trample by night.
- **Runebound Wolf** — `{3}{R}, {T}`: damage equal to your Wolf/Werewolf count **to an opponent**. Four mana a shot is steep, but it is the deck's **only** way to deal damage without attacking — the answer to a fog, a stalled board, or an opponent hiding behind a wall.
- **Scorned Villager // Moonscarred Werewolf** — Mana dork by day, two-mana vigilant dork by night.
- **Selvala, Heart of the Wilds** — `{G}, {T}: Add X mana in any combination of colours, where X is the greatest power among creatures you control.` A night-side Burly Breaker is 8/7, so that's eight mana off one green. **Her draw trigger is symmetric** — an opponent playing a bigger creature draws off her too.
- **Silverfur Partisan** — A 2/2 trampler (not a lord) minting a Wolf whenever a Wolf/Werewolf you control becomes the target of an **instant or sorcery spell**: here Bite Down, Snakeskin Veil, Unnatural Moonrise and Wolf Strike.
- **Toski, Bearer of Secrets** *(new)* — Uncounterable, **indestructible**, and draws a card whenever *any* creature you control connects. Its value here is not raw draw — Tovolar already draws off 32 tribal bodies — it is that **Toski is the only draw engine in the entire collection that survives a board wipe.** That answers the deck's #2 stated weakness directly. Costs: it **must attack each combat**, and being a Squirrel it collects none of your anthems.
- **Tovolar's Huntmaster // Tovolar's Packleader** — Two Wolves on entry; two more on every attack at night, and `{2}{G}{G}` makes another Wolf/Werewolf **fight**.
- **Village Messenger // Moonrise Intruder** — Hasty 1-drop, menacing 2/2 at night.
- **Volatile Arsonist // Dire-Strain Anarchist** — Menace/haste pinger hitting a creature, a player and a planeswalker on attack.
- **Wargling** — Two-mana Wolf granting **all your creatures trample** when it attacks while you *control* a 4-power creature — that creature needn't attack. Partly redundant with Garruk's Uprising, but it's a cheap Wolf and covers the turn Garruk's gets answered.
- **Weaver of Blossoms // Blossom-Clad Werewolf** — Any-colour dork by day; 3/4 tapping for two at night.
- **Werewolf Pack Leader** — Draws when you attack with 6-plus total power; can become a 5/3 non-Human trampler. Single-faced, never transforms.
- **Wolfkin Outcast // Wedding Crasher** — Costs {2} less with a Wolf/Werewolf out; night side draws whenever a Wolf or Werewolf you control dies.
- **Zopandrel, Hunger Dominus** — 4/6 **reach** doubling the power and toughness of each creature you control at the beginning of *every* combat phase in the turn cycle, opponents' included.

### Enchantments (5)
- **Full Moon's Rise** — **Werewolf** creatures get +1/+0 and trample; sacrifices to regenerate all **Werewolves**. It does nothing for Wolf *tokens* or the **seven** non-Werewolf Wolves (Ascendant Packleader, Immerwolf, Nightpack Ambusher, Packsong Pup, Runebound Wolf, Silverfur Partisan, Wargling).
- **Garruk's Uprising** — Draws on entry with a 4-power creature, gives **every** creature trample permanently, and **draws whenever a creature with power 4 or greater enters**. The best card any unlock provides.
- **Howlpack Resurgence** — Flash anthem: Wolves *and* Werewolves get +1/+1 and trample — unlike Full Moon's Rise it covers the tokens.
- **Ranger Class** — Wolf on entry, +1/+1 counter on an attacker at Level 2, casts creatures off the top at Level 3.
- **Unnatural Growth** — `{1}{G}{G}{G}{G}`: doubles power and toughness of each creature you control at each combat. Survives the wraths that kill Zopandrel; if both resolve the doublings stack.

### Artifacts & Mana (5)
- **Arcane Signet** — Untapped two-mana fixing.
- **Air Shoes (Swiftfoot Boots)** *(new)* — **Hexproof** and haste for {2}, equip {1}. The upgrade that deletes a whole paragraph of caveats: hexproof stops opponents targeting Tovolar while blocking **none** of your own thirteen effects that shroud used to shut off. The one thing you give up is Lightning Greaves' `{0}` equip, so moving it now costs a mana.
- **Chromatic Lantern** — "Lands you control have `{T}`: Add one mana of any color" turns all 13 Mountains, Kessig Wolf Run and an unhelpful Exotic Orchard into green sources, taking the deck from 22 green lands to **36**. It is what makes `{G}{G}{G}{G}` genuinely castable. Risk: colour fixing now leans on an artifact.
- **Sol Ring** — The best ramp piece in Commander.
- **The Celestus** — Ramps and fixes, and can push the day/night cycle, though `{3}` plus a tap produces no net mana that turn.

### Instants (8)
- **Abrade** — 3 damage to a creature, or destroy an artifact.
- **Bite Down** — Fight spell using your creature's power; hits planeswalkers, and targets your own Wolf for a Silverfur token.
- **Broken Wings** — Destroys an artifact, an enchantment, **or a creature with flying**.
- **Chaos Warp** — Catch-all for any permanent.
- **Moonlight Hunt** — Every Wolf/Werewolf you control damages a single target. On a board of eight Wolves that is 16-plus damage for two mana at instant speed — **the only card in the deck that kills an arbitrarily large creature.** Its target is a creature you don't control, so it does *not* trigger Silverfur Partisan.
- **Moonmist** — A fog for everything that isn't a Wolf or Werewolf, and it transforms all Humans. **Daybound permanents can't be transformed by other effects**, so it flips only the **11 old-border** werewolves, not the 14 daybound ones.
- **Snakeskin Veil** — Protects a threat, leaves a permanent counter, mints a Silverfur Wolf.
- **Wolf Strike** — Pumps (more at night) then fights; targets your own Wolf for Silverfur.

### Sorceries (2)
- **Shamanic Revelation** *(new)* — **Draw a card for each creature you control** — no "non-Human" clause, so unlike Return of the Wildspeaker it is never night-gated. On a board of ten that is ten cards, plus 4 life per 4-power creature. The caveat worth knowing: it draws *zero* after a wrath, so it is a "when ahead" card rather than a rebuild.
- **Unnatural Moonrise** — Forces night and makes a creature a trampling card-draw threat; target your own Wolf and it mints a Silverfur token. Flashback makes it a two-for-one, and it is the only proactive night-forcer besides Tovolar.

### Planeswalkers (2)
- **Arlinn Kord // Arlinn, Embraced by the Moon** — Haste and pump or a Wolf by day; anthem, 3 damage and a haste-and-ping emblem by night.
- **Arlinn, the Pack's Hope // Arlinn, the Moon's Fury** — Day: +1 gives creatures flash and an extra counter as they enter; −3 makes two Wolves. Night: `+2: Add {R}{G}`, and `0:` makes her a 5/5 **Werewolf** with trample, indestructible and haste — counting toward Tovolar's threshold.

### Lands (36)
- **Command Tower** — Red or Green, no downside.
- **Exotic Orchard** — Untapped, and its coloured mana is **unrestricted**, which is why it beats every tribal land in the pool. Can whiff against mono-blue or mono-white.
- **Forest** (x13) — Green basics.
- **Mountain** (x13) — Red basics.
- **Gongaga, Reactor Town** — Tapped Gruul dual.
- **Kessig Wolf Run** — Untapped finisher land; pump and trample scaling with mana.
- **Oran-Rief, the Vastwood** *(new)* — Taps for green and puts a **+1/+1 counter on each green creature that entered this turn** — every Wolf token is green, so a Tovolar's Huntmaster turn makes two 3/3s. Note this is a **trade, not a strict upgrade**: it replaces a land that tapped for R *or* G, so red sources drop from 22 to 21 in a deck with five double-red costs.
- **Path of Ancestry** — Gruul dual with a scry on a shared-type creature.
- **Raging Ravine** — Tapped dual becoming a self-growing 3/3 that dodges sorcery-speed wraths.
- **Rugged Highlands** — Tapped Gruul dual with 1 life.
- **Sheltered Thicket** — Tapped Gruul dual with cycling.
- **Temple of Abandon** — Tapped Gruul dual with a scry.

## Key Synergies

- **Toski + a wrath**: Toski is indestructible and can't be countered, so the sweeper that resets the board leaves your draw engine online. Rebuild into a Toski that is already drawing. It is the single most important card in this build that isn't in the other two.
- **Garruk's Uprising + your 4-power creatures**: it draws whenever a creature with power 4 or greater **enters** — and the deck casts nine of them: Burly Breaker, Tovolar's Huntmaster, Beorn the Fierce, Zopandrel, Migloz, Nightpack Ambusher, Volatile Arsonist, Wolfkin Outcast and Greenwarden. **Important limit: a werewolf *transforming* is not entering**, so flipping Burly Breaker to an 8/7 draws you nothing — a transforming permanent never changes zones and never triggers enter-the-battlefield abilities. Wolf tokens are 2/2 and only cross the four-power line once two or more anthems are out. What Garruk's reliably gives you is permanent team-wide trample plus a card off most of your top end, which alongside Tovolar's combat-damage draw is two engines off one board.
- **Selvala + Burly Breaker**: a night-side Burly Breaker is 8/7, so Selvala taps for eight mana off one green — enough for Unnatural Growth and Endurance in the same turn.
- **Zopandrel + Unnatural Growth**: both double at the beginning of each combat and the effects compound, so a 2/2 Wolf attacks as an 8/8 — or **16/16** carrying Immerwolf and Nightpack Ambusher's anthems. They fail to different answers, which is why both are here.
- **Doubling timing**: both trigger at the **beginning of combat**, before attackers are declared, and each locks X at resolution (CR 701.9b). Anything applied after is added at face value — but Kessig Wolf Run and Tovolar's `{X}{R}{G}` are instant-speed, so use them **while the doubling trigger is still on the stack** to get them doubled too.
- **Moonlight Hunt as the scaling answer**: with eight Wolves out it deals 16-plus damage to one creature for two mana. Nothing else in the deck kills a resolved 10/10.
- **Oran-Rief + a token turn**: Tovolar's Huntmaster at night makes two green Wolves on attack, and Oran-Rief counters **only green creatures that entered this turn** — so it grows the two *new* tokens into 3/3s, not the whole swarm. Activate it after the attack trigger resolves.

### What I deliberately did *not* take from the 90 unlocked cards
- **Beastmaster Ascension** — a third copy of an over-served effect. The deck already has two P/T doublers; +5/+5 needs seven attack triggers and does nothing the turn it lands. Not worth Runebound Wolf's slot.
- **Second Harvest** — copying eight Wolf tokens looks lethal, but **the copies have summoning sickness and this deck has no mass haste** (every haste source is single-target), so it must be cast on the opponent's end step and telegraphs the kill for a full turn cycle.
- **Skullclamp** — the board is mostly 2/2 tokens that become 3/1 and *survive*; there is no sacrifice outlet.
- **Maskwood Nexus** — actively anti-synergistic. Making every creature every type means everything is a Human, which would have turned off Cult of the Waxing Moon, broken Immerwolf's transform lock, and blanked Moonmist.
- **Blasphemous Act** — you are always the widest board at the table.
- **End-Raze Forerunners**, **Titan of Industry**, **Kindred Summons** — powerful, but seven-to-eight mana win-more with two doublers already in.
- **Wolfwillow Haven** — genuinely tempting as two-mana *green* acceleration, but with Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Chromatic Lantern, The Celestus, Llanowar Elves, Scorned Villager, Weaver of Blossoms and Selvala the deck is already slightly over-ramped for a 3.11 curve.
- The Full Deployment artifact shell (Goldspan Dragon, Hangarback Walker, Steel Overseer, The Ozolith, Solemn Simulacrum) — powerful cards, wrong deck.

### What does **not** change, in any build
**No card in the entire 444-card collection is a night-forcer.** Dismantling all three decks does nothing about the deck's defining weakness: without Tovolar the night plan barely functions and you are a pile of below-rate day-side 2/2s and 3/3s. Every build here is **more powerful, not less fragile** — and this one looks strong enough that a reader would assume otherwise.

## Short Mulligan and Play Notes

- **Mulligan to deploy and protect Tovolar, not to find him.** He's always in the command zone. You want mana to cast him turn 3–4, two or three cheap bodies for his threshold, and ideally Air Shoes.
- **Early (1–3)**: Llanowar Elves or Sol Ring into a three-drop — twenty of them. Garruk's Uprising on turn three is the best start after Tovolar himself.
- **Mid (4–6)**: Land Tovolar with three bodies out. Toski turns every attack into cards and cannot be answered by removal. Selvala turns a big werewolf into a mana engine.
- **Late (7+)**: Either doubler ends the game on a wide board. Shamanic Revelation refills while you're ahead; Greenwarden rebuys whatever was answered.
- **Day/night, stated correctly**: it becomes night when a player casts **zero** spells during their own turn; casting one changes nothing; two or more flips it back to day. **Tovolar's upkeep trigger is your real night button.**
- **Key interactions**: Daybound permanents can't be transformed by anything except the day/night cycle, so Moonmist and Vildin-Pack Alpha only flip the 11 old-border werewolves. **Toski must attack every combat** — plan around it rather than getting blown out by a fight spell. Air Shoes grant hexproof, so unlike the old Power Sneakers you *can* still target the equipped creature with your own spells. Fury's evoke cost is a red card from hand — check whether that werewolf is worth more than the removal.

## Analysis

**Strengths**
- **A draw engine that survives a board wipe.** Toski is uncounterable and indestructible; nothing in the previous builds could say that.
- Four independent card-advantage sources: Tovolar, Garruk's Uprising, Toski and Beorn, plus Shamanic Revelation as a refill
- Two P/T doublers failing to different answers, stacking multiplicatively
- Permanent team-wide trample rather than a conditional attack trigger
- Colour fixing genuinely solved — 36 effective green sources with the Lantern out
- Free spells: Endurance and Fury both cost zero when it matters
- Removal that **scales** — Moonlight Hunt kills anything the board is big enough to outdamage

**Weaknesses**
- **The night plan is still commander-dependent**, and no card in the entire collection fixes it. Unchanged across all three builds and still the defining fragility.
- **Board wipes**: still none worth running — you are always the widest board.
- Colour fixing leans on an artifact; losing Chromatic Lantern reverts `{G}{G}{G}{G}` to 22 sources
- Selvala's draw trigger is symmetric and helps a pod opponent with bigger creatures
- Toski's forced attack is a genuine liability into a fight spell or a big blocker
- Tribal density is down to 33 from Claws for Concern's 35 — the core is intact, but the marginal tribal filler has been traded for individually stronger, individually off-tribe cards

**Power level**: **Bracket 3, mid.** No tutors anywhere, no fast mana beyond Sol Ring, no combo, no stax, and nothing on the Game Changers list. What it has is a genuinely good fair deck — Toski, Garruk's Uprising, Shamanic Revelation, Endurance, Fury, Swiftfoot Boots, Chromatic Lantern and Selvala together are a real step up from the constrained build, without approaching bracket 4.

## Next Steps (Optional Suggestions)

- **This deck costs you all three assembled decks.** If that's not happening, the Elements Unlocked build gets most of the way there for one precon, and Claws for Concern is what you can actually sleeve today.
- If the metagame is token-heavy, **Second Harvest** is the first card to reconsider — but only alongside a mass-haste enabler, which the collection does not currently contain.
- **Wolfwillow Haven** is the cut I'd revisit first if the mana feels short in testing rather than over-built.
- If you later acquire a night-forcer — anything that changes the day/night designation without needing Tovolar — it does more for this deck than any card in this file.
