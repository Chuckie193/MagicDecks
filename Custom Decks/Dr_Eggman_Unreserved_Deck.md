# Dr_Eggman_Unreserved_Deck — Grixis Robot Factory Commander

Commander: Dr. Eggman ({2}{U}{B}{R}, Blue/Black/Red)

## Overview
- **Strategy**: Dr. Eggman draws an extra card every end step, then each opponent makes a villainous choice: discard a card, or let you put a Construct, Robot or Vehicle from your hand onto the battlefield for free. The deck is packed with expensive Robots, Constructs and Vehicles, so every "let him have it" answer is a free 5–7 mana threat. Maskwood Nexus makes every creature card you own a Robot, and artifact ramp, draw and Fateful Discovery keep the factory running. The deck wins with a Myr Battlesphere swarm, a Super State one-shot across the table, a Depthshaker Titan alpha strike or Knuckles' 30-artifact alternate win. This version uses only cards that aren't sitting in an assembled deck, so it can be sleeved right now without dismantling anything.
- **Intended for**: Multiplayer pod
- **Card pool**: Avoid reserved decks (Full Deployment, Claws for Concern, Table Manners, Dance of the Elements, Multiverse Reforged)

## Lore

Atop his flying fortress, the Doctor sips tea while his robot army does the dirty work. Every rival gets a choice: toss a secret, or watch another metal monster roll off the line. *Resistance is futile — and fully automated.*

## Key Synergies
- **Dr. Eggman + Maskwood Nexus**: Every creature card in your hand becomes a Robot, so each villainous choice can deploy Knuckles, Sai or Vedalken Archmage, not just artifact creatures.
- **Dr. Eggman + Thopter Fabricator**: Eggman's end-step draw is reliably your second draw each turn, so Fabricator makes a flying Thopter every turn without any other help.
- **Dr. Eggman + Depthshaker Titan / Myr Battlesphere**: The two 7-drops are the best "you chose wrong" punishment. A free Titan gives the whole artifact army melee, trample and haste for an immediate multiplayer swing.
- **Super State + any evasive creature**: 9/9 flying, first strike, trample and haste, and its combat damage to one opponent is dealt to every other opponent too. Two hits usually end a four-player game.
- **Knuckles + Myr Battlesphere + Sai + rocks**: Tokens, Treasures, mana rocks and artifact lands stack up fast toward Knuckles' 30-artifact upkeep win.
- **Fateful Discovery + token makers**: Battlesphere alone draws five cards (itself plus four Myr).

## Short Mulligan and Play Notes
- **Early game**: Keep hands with 3+ lands and a rock or cost reducer. Turn 2–3 ramp (Sol Ring, Signet, Mind Stone, Thought Vessel, Foundry Inspector, Etherium Sculptor) lets you land Eggman on turn 3–4. Put Air Shoes or Power Sneakers on him as soon as you can.
- **Mid game**: Hold your big Robots and Constructs in hand rather than casting them, since each end step forces opponents to choose between discarding and giving you a free deploy. Cast cheap engines (Vedalken Archmage, Sai, Fateful Discovery, Scrawling Crawler) with your actual mana. Maskwood Nexus turns every creature card into valid ammo.
- **Late game**: Close with Super State on a flyer, a free Depthshaker Titan alpha strike, Battlesphere damage, or Knuckles at 30 artifacts. Steel Hellkite and Meteor Golem clear the way.
- **Key interactions**: Opponents choose discard vs. deployment, not you, so keep at least one Construct/Robot/Vehicle in hand to make the choice painful. Blasphemous Act and Necrotic Hex hit your board too, so cast them when you're behind; Necrotic Hex leaves you six Zombies. Brilliance Unleashed and Buried Ruin rebuild afterwards.

## Why These Choices (Summary)
- **Cheat targets first**: Over twenty Robots, Constructs, Vehicles and changelings, weighted toward expensive ones (Battlesphere, Titan, Skysovereign, Thought Monitor, Turtle Blimp) so the free deployment is worth the most mana.
- **Artifact-dense ramp**: Thirteen mana sources besides lands (rocks, mana creatures, Caravan) plus one artifact land. They fuel affinity, Improvise, Master of Etherium and Knuckles' count while ramping into Eggman.
- **Multiplayer interaction**: Four sweepers (Blasphemous Act, Chain Reaction, Necrotic Hex, Steel Hellkite), Meteor Golem and Crude Bent Blade on artifact bodies, spot removal, two counterspells, and Scrawling Crawler as group draw.
- **Multiple win routes**: Combat with an artifact army, a Super State table-wide hit, Myr Battlesphere direct damage, and Knuckles' alternate win.
- Built from **Avoid reserved decks**: 28 cards from the full-collection version were swapped out because every copy sits in an assembled deck (see Cards Swapped below). Keen Engineering is now the biggest precon contributor (28 cards).

## Analysis

**Strengths**:
- Card advantage every turn from the commander, with a real punishment attached in a 4-player pod (three villainous choices per turn)
- Deep, mutually reinforcing artifact synergies: cost reducers, lords, draw engines and token makers
- Several independent win conditions, including a non-combat alt-win
- Strong multiplayer tools: Steel Hellkite, Necrotic Hex and Super State all scale with the number of opponents
- Fully buildable today: nothing comes out of an assembled deck

**Weaknesses/Missing Staples**:
- **Artifact hate**: A single Vandalblast-style effect or an artifact wipe hurts badly. Padeem's hexproof only stops targeted removal. Holding up Counterspell or Refute is the main defence.
- **Self-damage from sweepers**: The wipes hit your own robots. Time them carefully.
- **Few tutors**: Only Reckless Handling, and its random discard can cost you a key card. Cards like Whir of Invention or Tezzeret the Seeker would help find Battlesphere/Maskwood.
- **Weaker mana**: The good untapped duals (checklands, painlands, Turbulent duals) live in Full Deployment and Multiverse Reforged. Ten of the 22 nonbasic lands enter tapped, and four more basics make up the gap.
- **Fewer artifact lands**: Only Darksteel Citadel, so Knuckles' 30-artifact win and affinity costs come a little slower.

## Next Steps (Optional Suggestions)
- Playtest whether 36 lands is right; Eggman's extra draw may allow a trim to 35 for another Robot (Roving Actuator, Red Tiger Mechan or Mechan Navigator are ready in the collection).
- If artifact hate is common in your pod, swap Adaptive Automaton for Padeem-style protection or Spellgyre for another counter.
- If you dismantle Full Deployment, the biggest upgrades are Cyberdrive Awakener, Hangarback Walker, Jhoira, Fabricate and the artifact lands.
- Cards worth acquiring: Whir of Invention, Urza, Lord High Artificer, Wurmcoil Engine (a premium Construct cheat), Blightsteel/Darksteel Forge.
