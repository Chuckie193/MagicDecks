# Inspirit_Flagship_Vessel_Deck — Artifact Station Commander

Commander: Inspirit, Flagship Vessel (1WUR, White/Blue/Red)

## Overview
- **Strategy:** Inspirit, Flagship Vessel uses the Station mechanic — tapping powerful creatures to load charge counters onto the ship. At 1+, each combat step loads additional counters or +1/+1 counters onto your other artifacts, creating a snowball of artifact power. At 8+, Inspirit gains flying and grants all other artifacts hexproof and indestructible, making your entire artifact base near-impervious. Win via creature combat, Lux Artillery's 30-counter trigger (10 damage to each opponent), Darksteel Reactor's 20-counter alternative, or Titan Forge / Golem Foundry token armies.
- **Intended for:** Both (1v1 and multiplayer)

## Lore

The Inspirit's crew keeps her charged — and at full power, no force in the cosmos can circuit-break her. *She runs on pure momentum; the only way to stop her is to cut the current.*

## Decklist (100 cards)

### Commander (1)
- **Inspirit, Flagship Vessel** (1WUR, Legendary Artifact — Spacecraft, 5/5) — The hub of the deck. Station her each turn by tapping your most powerful creature; at 1+ counters she begins buffing artifacts in combat, and at 8+ she locks your artifact board behind hexproof and indestructible.

- **Dirgur Focusmage // Braingeyser** (2U / XUU, Djinn Monk, 1/4) — Flexible threat and big draw spell; cast Dirgur early for a sturdy blocker or hold the Braingeyser adventure for a massive hand refill.

- **Hangarback Walker** (XX, Construct, 0/0) — Enters with X +1/+1 counters; taps to add a counter; when it dies, creates X 1/1 Thopter tokens with flying. Provides high-power Station fuel when large, and a swarm of Skullclamp targets when it falls.

- **Surge Conductor** (3, Robot, 3/2) — Whenever another nontoken artifact you control enters the battlefield, proliferate. Every artifact played adds counters to Inspirit, Darksteel Reactor, Etched Oracle, Mindless Automaton, and more. The deck's engine room.

### Artifacts & Mana (15)
- **Arcane Signet** (2) — Two-mana rock producing any color in the commander's identity. Essential early ramp.
- **Darksteel Reactor** (4, indestructible) — Gains a charge counter at the start of each upkeep. At 20 counters, you win the game. Indestructible, and once Inspirit is at 8+, it also gains hexproof. A silent clock opponents must remove immediately or lose to.
- **Empowered Autogenerator** (4) — Enters tapped; each turn taps to put a charge counter on itself and produces X mana of any one color, where X is its charge count. Grows from 1 mana to enormous ramp over several turns; Surge Conductor proliferates extra counters onto it.
- **Gavel of the Righteous** (2, Equipment) — Gains a charge counter at the start of each combat. Equipped creature gets +1/+1 for each counter. At four or more counters, the equipped creature has double strike. Equip by paying 3 or removing a counter — puts your best Station target in the danger zone.
- **Golem Foundry** (3) — Whenever you cast an artifact spell, put a charge counter here. Remove three counters: create a 3/3 colorless Golem artifact creature token. In a deck casting 5-10 artifacts, this generates a Golem nearly every other turn.
- **Insight Engine** (2U) — Pay 2 and tap: put a charge counter on this artifact, then draw a card for each charge counter on it. Scales as an exponential draw engine alongside Surge Conductor's proliferate.
- **Long-Range Sensor** (2R) — Whenever you attack a player, put a charge counter here. Remove two counters: Discover 4, activatable as a sorcery. Converts attacks into free spells or creatures mid-combat.
- **Lux Artillery** (4) — At the beginning of your end step, if there are 30 or more counters among artifacts and creatures you control, this artifact deals 10 damage to each opponent. With Inspirit, Darksteel Reactor, Empowered Autogenerator, Etched Oracle, Mindless Automaton, Gavel of the Righteous, Patrolling Peacemaker, and proliferate effects, 30 total counters is very achievable by turns 8-10.
- **Lux Cannon** (4) — Tap to add a charge counter; tap and remove three charge counters: destroy any target permanent. The deck's premium hard removal that bypasses indestructibility workarounds, scales as a repeatable answer engine.
- **Moxite Refinery** (2) — Pay 2, tap, remove X counters from an artifact or creature you control: put X charge counters on target artifact OR X +1/+1 counters on target creature (sorcery speed). Transfers counters from Etched Oracle, Mindless Automaton, or creature tokens to supercharge Inspirit in one activation.
- **Skullclamp** (1, Equipment) — Equipped creature gets -1/+0; when equipped creature dies, draw two cards. Equip 1. Transforms every 1/1 Gnome from Threefold Thunderhulk, every Thopter from Hangarback Walker, every Goblin from Dragon Fodder, and Coretapper itself into two cards upon death.
- **Sol Ring** (1) — The Commander staple. T: add CC. Accelerates Inspirit onto turn 3 consistently.
- **Soul-Guide Lantern** (1) — When it enters, exile a target card from a graveyard. Tap, Sacrifice: exile each opponent's graveyard. Pay 1, Tap, Sacrifice: draw a card. Graveyard hate in artifact form that triggers Jhoira and Surge Conductor, with emergency card draw.
- **Titan Forge** (3) — Pay 3 and tap: add a charge counter. Tap and remove three charge counters: create a 9/9 colorless Golem artifact creature token. With Surge Conductor proliferating and Moxite Refinery loading extra counters, this churns out 9/9 Golems remarkably fast.
- **Uthros Research Craft** (2U, Artifact — Spacecraft, 0/8) — A second Station target: activates as an artifact creature at 12+ charge counters. At 3+ counters, draws a card and adds a charge counter whenever you cast an artifact spell. Pairs with Jhoira for double draw on every artifact cast.

## Key Synergies

- **Inspirit + Coretapper + Moxite Refinery**: Coretapper charges Inspirit directly (tap for 1, sacrifice for 2). Moxite Refinery can move counters from Etched Oracle or Mindless Automaton onto Inspirit to reach 8 in a single turn.
- **Surge Conductor + Every Artifact Entering**: Every nontoken artifact ETB triggers Surge Conductor's proliferate, stacking counters on Inspirit, Darksteel Reactor, Empowered Autogenerator, Gavel of the Righteous, and Lux Artillery simultaneously.
- **Depthshaker Titan + Inspirit 8+ Protection**: Animate all noncreature artifacts as 3/3 hasted trampling threats, protected by Inspirit's indestructibility blanket. Opponents can't remove them with spot removal before the attack resolves.
- **Jhoira, Weatherlight Captain + Artifact-Dense Build**: With 15 non-creature artifacts and 12 artifact creatures (all historic), Jhoira draws a card nearly every spell cast. Combined with Emry, Thought Monitor, Insight Engine, and Curiosity Crafter, the deck is nearly inexhaustible.
- **Skullclamp + Token Engines**: Threefold Thunderhulk creates 1/1 Gnomes equal to its power (3+ each time it enters or attacks). Hangarback Walker dies creating X 1/1 Thopters. Dragon Fodder creates 2 Goblins. Each 1/1 becomes two cards with Skullclamp attached.
- **Lux Artillery + Proliferate Package**: Patrolling Peacemaker (proliferate on opponent crime), Ripples of Potential (instant proliferate), Surge Conductor (proliferate on artifact ETB), and Empowered Autogenerator (grows each upkeep) stack counters across many permanents. Once total counters across artifacts and creatures reach 30, Lux Artillery ends the game.
- **Rootha, Mercurial Artist + Mass Spells**: Return Rootha to your hand to copy Organic Extinction (double board wipe), Aether Gale (return 12 permanents), or Pull from Tomorrow (draw twice as many cards).

## Short Mulligan and Play Notes

- **Early game (turns 1-4):** Prioritise lands hitting all three colors. Sol Ring and Arcane Signet enable turn-3 Inspirit. Play Etherium Sculptor or Enthusiastic Mechanaut to reduce future artifact costs. Keep an eye out for a cheap 2-3 power creature to begin Stationing on turn 5.
- **Mid game (turns 5-8):** Station Inspirit every turn, using the biggest available creature to reach 8 counters as fast as possible. Surge Conductor multiplies each artifact entry into a proliferate. At 8+, Inspirit gains flying and all other artifacts gain hexproof + indestructible — now you're almost untouchable.
- **Late game:** Swing with Depthshaker Titan to animate your artifact field for a lethal alpha strike. Lux Artillery becomes a win condition once 30 counters are spread across permanents. Titan Forge and Threefold Thunderhulk generate armies. Darksteel Reactor ticks up quietly as a game-winning alternate clock.
- **Key interactions:**
  - Ripples of Potential can phase out Inspirit before a board wipe while adding a counter (it re-enters phased in at your next untap, counters intact).
  - Organic Extinction ignores all your artifact creatures — cast when opponents have built creature boards while you have artifacts.
  - Renegade Bull's attack trigger can exile + recast Organic Extinction or Aether Gale from your graveyard for free.
  - Emry + Coretapper = infinite Station loop: Coretapper sacrifices to add 2 charges, Emry recasts it from the graveyard, repeat.

## Why These Choices

- **CounterIntelligence precon as the spine:** Inspirit is the head of that precon; Coretapper, Surge Conductor, Lux Cannon, Titan Forge, Insight Engine, and Lux Artillery are all built-in synergy cards that play together naturally.
- **Cost-reduction package:** Etherium Sculptor, Enthusiastic Mechanaut, Stormcatch Mentor, and Thunderclap Drake let you cast 2-3 artifacts or spells a turn from mid-game, flooding the board and triggering Surge Conductor repeatedly.
- **Jhoira as draw engine:** In a deck where every artifact is historic, Jhoira draws a card nearly every spell. This eliminates the typical artifact deck problem of running out of cards mid-game.
- **Lux Artillery win condition:** Reaching 30 counters is highly achievable: Inspirit grows toward 8 (or beyond), Darksteel Reactor ticks up, Empowered Autogenerator grows, Gavel of the Righteous charges every combat, and proliferate multiplies all of it. A single Ripples of Potential or Surge Conductor activation can push you over the 30 threshold.
- **Notable omissions:** Swan Song, Swords to Plowshares, Tekuthal, Thrummingbird, Karn's Bastion, and many other premier Jeskai cards are allocated to other decks. The deck compensates with the Counter Intelligence spell package and FDN removal.

## Analysis

**Strengths:**
- Near-impervious artifact board once Inspirit reaches 8+ counters (hexproof + indestructible)
- Multiple win conditions: combat, Lux Artillery 30-counter trigger, Darksteel Reactor 20-counter win, Titan Forge 9/9 Golem army
- Exceptional card draw via Jhoira, Thought Monitor, Insight Engine, Emry, and Curiosity Crafter
- Resilient artifact recursion through Emry and The Mycosynth Gardens

**Weaknesses/Missing Staples:**
- **Early vulnerability:** Inspirit must reach 8 counters before protection kicks in; targeted removal during this window is dangerous
- **Removal suite:** Many premier Jeskai removal spells (Swan Song, Swords to Plowshares, Dispatch, Generous Gift) are already in other decks; this deck leans on less efficient alternatives
- **Counterspell gaps:** Limited counterspell access (only Negate); one heavy counterspell turn from an opponent can disrupt the Station plan
- **Enchantment threats:** Charmed Sleep and Chaos Warp are the only answers to problematic enchantments; Organic Extinction doesn't touch them

## Next Steps (Optional Suggestions)

- Track whether Lux Artillery triggers are achievable by turn 9-10 in practice; if too slow, consider adding more proliferate pieces.
- Test whether Stormcatch Mentor's prowess triggers generate meaningful Station value — the +1/+1 boost is temporary so it helps if you Station in the same main phase after casting a spell.
- If you acquire a second Reliquary Tower, it would be excellent here given the Jhoira draw engine.
- Consider Tezzeret's Gambit if it ever becomes available — proliferate plus draw is ideal.

## Suggested Deck Names

- **Full Charge** — Directly references the Station mechanic and the military charge of a flagship; as simple and powerful as the deck's strategy.
- **Current Affairs** — Double meaning: the crew's ongoing mission, and the electrical "current" that keeps the ship powered. The deck is always running.
- **Shield Protocol** — Evokes Inspirit's 8+ protection triggering on all artifacts; sounds like a sci-fi emergency security system.
- **Overcharged** — A single punchy word: having more charge counters than anyone expected, and the board state to show for it.
