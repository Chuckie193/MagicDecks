## Findings worth keeping

**Only four cards in all of Magic can make it night** — Tovolar, The Celestus, Unnatural Moonrise and Into the Night — and **you own all four.** The deck's defining weakness, that the night plan barely functions without the commander, is **unbuyable**. No card on any list changes it.

**Lifting the price cap adds zero werewolves.** The entire Gruul-legal Werewolf pool is 70 cards and the most expensive is Avabruck Caretaker at about €5. Money buys off-theme goodstuff, never tribe — so every premium card costs a slot that could hold a Wolf or Werewolf.

**Wolf and Werewolf are separate creature types.** The deck is 28 Werewolves to 9 Wolves, and its damage comes from 2/2 *Wolf* tokens while its card mass is *Werewolves*. Any "choose a creature type" card covers one group or the other. The anthems already in the deck work because they say "Wolf **or** Werewolf."

**Daybound permanents cannot be transformed by any effect** other than the day/night cycle. 15 of the deck's 28 werewolves are daybound, so Moonmist and Vildin-Pack Alpha only reach the 12 old-border ones — and Tovolar's transform clause targets that same group.

**A werewolf transforming is not entering.** Garruk's Uprising draws off the 4-power creatures you *cast*, not off flips.
