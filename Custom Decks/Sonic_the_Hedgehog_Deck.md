# Sonic the Hedgehog — Jeskai Haste/Flash Commander

Commander: Sonic the Hedgehog (1URW, White/Blue/Red)

## Overview
- **Strategy:** Play Sonic early and attack every turn to stack +1/+1 counters on every creature with flash or haste. Layer in proliferate engines — Kilo, Tekuthal, Thrummingbird, Tezzeret's Gambit — to multiply those counters exponentially. The deck generates Treasure tokens whenever flash/haste creatures take damage, fueling a self-reinforcing mana and counter snowball. Win by turning a board of speed-boosted creatures into an unstoppable flood of massive attackers, often after a single Deepglow Skate doubles everything in one shot.

## Decklist (100 cards)

### Commander (1)
- **Sonic the Hedgehog** — 2/4 for 1URW with haste. "Gotta Go Fast" — whenever Sonic attacks, put a +1/+1 counter on each creature you control with flash or haste. Whenever a creature with flash or haste is dealt damage, create a tapped Treasure token. He pumps the whole team on every attack and converts combat damage into a mana engine.

### Creatures (24)

**Haste Creatures (Sonic's Direct Beneficiaries)**
- **Knuckles the Echidna** — 4/4 for 2RR with double strike, trample, and haste. Gets +1/+1 counters from Sonic every attack; with 5 counters he's an 9/9 double striker that tramples over anything. The premier threat in the deck.
- **Kilo, Apogee Mind** — 3/3 for URW with haste; whenever Kilo becomes tapped (e.g., by attacking), proliferate. Gets Sonic counters AND proliferates all existing counters every combat. With Tekuthal out, attacking with Kilo proliferates twice.
- **Goldspan Dragon** — 4/4 flying haste for 3RR; creates a Treasure when it attacks or is targeted, and your Treasure tokens produce double mana. Triggers Sonic's counter ability and generates Treasures simultaneously.
- **Muddle, the Ever-Changing** — 3/3 for 2UR with haste; whenever you cast an instant or sorcery, it becomes a copy of a nonlegendary creature you control until end of turn with myriad. Copy Knuckles for a double-strike myriad attack hitting every opponent, or copy Kilo to proliferate three times in one spell cast.
- **Goblin Smuggler** — 2/2 for 2R with haste; pay Red to give another creature haste until end of turn. A direct Sonic beneficiary that also enables any creature to become a haste-trigger for future turns.
- **Stormcatch Mentor** — 1/1 haste prowess for UR; instant and sorcery spells cost 1 less. Gets +1/+1 from Sonic, grows from spells via prowess, and makes the entire spell package cheaper.
- **Manaform Hellkite** — 4/4 flying for 2RR; whenever you cast a noncreature spell, create an X/X red Dragon Illusion token with flying and haste, where X = mana spent on that spell. Every instant or sorcery creates a haste token that triggers Sonic's counter ability on the next attack.
- **Rionya, Fire Dancer** — 3/4 for 3RR; at the beginning of combat, create X hasted token copies of another creature you control, where X = 1 plus instants/sorceries cast this turn. Cast 2 spells, get 3 copies of Kilo or Knuckles for one massive swing.
- **Mirrorwing Dragon** — 4/5 flying for 3RR; when any player targets Mirrorwing with an instant or sorcery, that player copies it for each other creature they own that the spell could target. Point any buff at Mirrorwing and it propagates to all your creatures — also makes opponents think twice about targeted removal.
- **Brudiclad, Telchor Engineer** — 4/4 for 4UR; creature tokens you control have haste. At the beginning of combat, creates a 2/1 Myr token, then may copy it for all other tokens. Every token Sonic generates (Manaform Hellkite dragons, Seedshark incubators) gets haste — and Sonic's counter trigger fires on all of them every attack.

**Flash Creatures (Sonic's Direct Beneficiaries)**
- **Brazen Borrower // Petty Theft** — 3/1 flash flying for 2UUU; the Petty Theft adventure (1U: return any nonland permanent) is the key play — an instant-speed trigger + interaction. The creature gets Sonic's counter on the next attack.
- **Faerie Mastermind** — 2/1 flash flying for 1U; whenever an opponent draws their second card each turn, draw a card. Flash makes it a Sonic beneficiary; the draw trigger is passive free card advantage.
- **Brineborn Cutthroat** — 2/1 flash for 1U; gets a +1/+1 counter whenever you cast a spell with flash. Stacks Sonic counters AND its own flash counters — with proliferate, both double.
- **Spectral Sailor** — 1/1 flash flying for U; tap to loot (1U: draw a card, discard a card). Cheap flash trigger for Sonic and repeatable late-game filtering.
- **Miles "Tails" Prower** — 3/1 flash for 1WU; whenever a Vehicle you control enters, draw a card if it has flying, else put a flying counter on it. A flash creature that draws off the Weatherlight vehicle, and triggers Sonic's counter ability at instant speed.

**Proliferate & Counter Engines**
- **Tekuthal, Inquiry Dominus** — 3/5 flying for 2UU; if you would proliferate, proliferate twice instead. With Kilo's attack trigger: one attack proliferates twice. With Tezzeret's Gambit: draw 2 + proliferate twice. The core multiplier of the deck.
- **Thrummingbird** — 1/1 flying for 1U; whenever it deals combat damage to a player, proliferate. Grows from Sonic's counter on each attack; as it grows, it becomes a better proliferate trigger — a self-accelerating loop.
- **Deepglow Skate** — 3/3 for 4U; when it enters, double the number of each kind of counter on any number of target permanents. Drop this after 5 turns of Sonic attacks and Kilo proliferates — suddenly all your creatures have 20+ counters. The deck's most explosive single card.
- **Steel Overseer** — 2/2 for 2; tap to put a +1/+1 counter on each artifact creature you control. With Chrome Host Seedshark's incubator tokens and Crystalline Crawler, keeps all artifact creatures growing.
- **Crystalline Crawler** — 1/1 (grows via Converge) for 4; enters with a +1/+1 counter for each color of mana spent (up to 3 in Jeskai). Remove a counter to add a mana of any color. A mana battery that grows from proliferate and refuels the turn you need it most.

**Support**
- **Chrome Host Seedshark** — 2/4 flying for 2U; whenever you cast a noncreature spell, incubate X (create a 0/0 artifact with X +1/+1 counters, which can transform into a Phyrexian). Artifact tokens benefit from Steel Overseer, proliferate, and Cyberdrive Awakener turning them into 4/4 fliers.
- **Phyrexian Metamorph** — variable for 3U; enters as a copy of any artifact or creature on the battlefield. Clone Kilo for double proliferate, copy Deepglow Skate for another doubling, or copy the biggest threat on the board.
- **Solemn Simulacrum** — 2/2 for 4; searches a basic land on ETB, draws a card on death. Smooths mana and replaces itself.
- **Cyberdrive Awakener** — 4/4 flying for 5U; until end of turn, each noncreature artifact becomes a 4/4 artifact creature with flying and haste. Flash with Goblin Smuggler to make all your incubator tokens and rocks into 4/4 flying haste creatures — Sonic's counter trigger fires on each of them.

### Artifacts (11)

**Ramp**

- **Arcane Signet** — 2-mana Jeskai rock; smooth early ramp.

- **Pentad Prism** — Enters with 2–3 charge counters (Sunburst, Jeskai = 3); each counter can be sacrificed for any color mana. Proliferate refills the counters, making this a permanent repeatable mana source.

**Equipment**
- **Lightning Greaves** — Equip 0; gives shroud and haste. Protects Sonic or Knuckles the turn they land.
- **Swiftfoot Boots** — Equip 1; gives hexproof and haste. Slightly slower but lets you still target your own creature.
- **The Reaver Cleaver** — Equip for 2R; equipped creature gets +1/+1, trample, and "whenever this creature deals combat damage to a player or planeswalker, create that many Treasure tokens." Attach to Knuckles (double strike, trample) — on a 12/12 double strike you get 24 Treasures in one hit.
- **Sword of Vengeance** — 3-mana equip 2; equipped creature gets +2/+0 and first strike, vigilance, trample, and haste. Turns any creature into a Sonic beneficiary and provides comprehensive keyword package.

### Enchantments (2)

- **Resourceful Defense** — 2W enchantment; whenever a permanent you control leaves the battlefield with counters on it, put those counters on another target permanent you control. Protects your counter investment — when a pumped creature is removed, all its accumulated counters move to the next creature.
- **Prayer of Binding** — 3W enchantment with flash; when it enters, exile target nonland permanent. Flash means it's instant-speed removal that also triggers Sonic's counter ability on the next swing... on an enchantment body.

### Instants (16)

**Cheap Interaction (1 mana)**
- **Swan Song** — U instant; counter any enchantment, instant, or sorcery for 1 mana. Opponent gets a 2/2 Bird but their combo or board wipe is stopped.
- **Swords to Plowshares** — W instant; exile any creature for W. The gold standard of white removal.
- **Dispatch** — W instant; tap target creature. Metalcraft — exile that creature instead (active with 3+ artifacts). With 11+ artifacts plus tokens, metalcraft is almost always online.
- **Opt** — U instant; scry 1, draw 1. Cheapest cantrip; one spell trigger for Sonic-relevant abilities.

**Interaction (2 mana)**
- **Into the Roil** — 1U instant; return any nonland permanent to hand (kicker 1U: draw a card). Flexible bounce; kicked version draws a card.
- **Reality Shift** — 1U instant; exile target creature, owner manifests top card. Clean creature removal avoiding death triggers.
- **Experimental Augury** — 1U instant; scry 2, draw a card. Efficient card selection for 2 mana.
- **Run Away Together** — 1U instant; choose two target creatures controlled by different players, return each to owner's hand. Bounces a threat AND one of your own creatures for ETB re-use.

**Removal (3 mana)**
- **Chaos Warp** — 2R instant; shuffle any permanent into library, owner reveals and plays top card if it's a permanent. Universal answer for anything.
- **Quick Study** — 2U instant; draw 2 cards. Simple instant-speed refuel.
- **Resculpt** — 2U instant; exile artifact or creature, its controller creates a 4/4 Shapeshifter. Clean exile-based removal.
- **Generous Gift** — 2W instant; exile any permanent, give controller a 3/3 Elephant token. The white Chaos Warp — works on lands, planeswalkers, anything.

**High-Impact**
- **Thirst for Knowledge** — 2U instant; draw 3, discard an artifact or discard 2. With 11 artifacts in the deck, rarely need to discard 2.
- **Prismari Command** — 1UR instant; choose two of: 2 damage / draw 2 discard 2 / create Treasure / destroy artifact. Incredibly flexible.
- **Dig Through Time** — 6UU instant (delve); look at top 7, keep 2. With a spent graveyard of cantrips and spells, typically costs 1UU or less.
- **Big Score** — 3R instant; discard a card, draw 2, create 2 Treasures. Filters hand and generates Treasures to feed The Reaver Cleaver or Goldspan Dragon synergies.

### Sorceries (10)

**Card Selection & Draw**
- **Expressive Iteration** — UR sorcery; look at top 3, one to hand, one exile (play this turn), one to bottom. Best 2-mana card selection in Jeskai.
- **Tezzeret's Gambit** — 3U (or 3+2 life) sorcery; draw 2 cards, then proliferate. With Tekuthal: draw 2 + proliferate twice. The premier proliferate sorcery.

**Tutors**
- **Open the Armory** — 1W sorcery; search library for an Aura or Equipment card. Finds Lightning Greaves to protect Sonic, The Reaver Cleaver to weaponize Knuckles, or Sword of Vengeance for a haste enabler.
- **Fabricate** — 2U sorcery; tutor for any artifact. Finds Sol Ring, Astral Cornucopia, Pentad Prism, or The Reaver Cleaver situationally.

**Board Wipes**
- **Fumigate** — 3WW sorcery; destroy all creatures, gain 1 life per creature destroyed. Resets aggressive boards while gaining you life.
- **Blasphemous Act** — 8R sorcery (costs 1 less per creature on the battlefield); deals 13 damage to each creature. Typically costs 1R or 2R with 10+ creatures across the table.
- **Chain Reaction** — 2RR sorcery; deals X damage to each creature where X = number of creatures on the battlefield. A cheaper Blasphemous Act option; can hit harder in creature-light games.

**Lategame Power**
- **Treasure Cruise** — 7U sorcery (delve); draw 3. Usually costs U or 1U late game with a full graveyard.
- **Replication Technique** — 2U sorcery (or storm: 4U); create a token copy of any artifact or creature you control. Copy Kilo for two attack-step proliferates per turn. Storm mode replicates the token for each spell cast that turn.
- **Wake the Past** — 5RW sorcery; return all artifact cards from your graveyard to the battlefield with haste until end of turn. Returns Sol Ring, Astral Cornucopia, The Reaver Cleaver, Pentad Prism — and they all have haste, triggering Sonic's counter ability.

## Key Synergies

- **Sonic + Kilo, Apogee Mind + Tekuthal**: Attack with Sonic → Kilo gets +1/+1 counter (it attacked, so it's tapped) → Kilo's tap ability triggers and proliferates → Tekuthal doubles it to two proliferates → all counters on all permanents multiply. Every attack step is exponential counter growth.
- **Knuckles the Echidna + The Reaver Cleaver + Counters**: Knuckles deals double damage with double strike. With 10 +1/+1 counters (14/14 double strike) + The Reaver Cleaver, connect once and create 28 Treasures. One attack can fund the rest of the game.
- **Deepglow Skate + Late-Game Board**: After 6 turns of Sonic attacks and Kilo proliferates, every creature has 10–20 counters. Drop Deepglow Skate: everything doubles instantly. Thrummingbird becomes a 20/20+ flying proliferate machine; Kilo becomes enormous.
- **Manaform Hellkite + Wake the Past + Cyberdrive Awakener**: Manaform Hellkite creates flying haste Dragon tokens from spells. Wake the Past returns all artifacts with haste. Cyberdrive Awakener turns every noncreature artifact into a 4/4 flier with haste. Any of these generates a wave of flash/haste creatures that Sonic pumps all at once.
- **Rionya, Fire Dancer + Kilo**: Cast 2 spells in a turn → Rionya creates 3 hasted Kilo copies at the start of combat → those copies all attack → each taps and proliferates → with Tekuthal, that's 6 proliferate triggers from a single combat step.
- **Thrummingbird + Proliferate Stack**: Thrummingbird is 1/1 flying, gets +1/+1 from Sonic each attack. At 5/5, it deals 5 flying damage → proliferates → all counters grow → Thrummingbird grows → deals 6 damage next hit → faster proliferate → accelerating loop.

## Mulligan and Play Notes

**Opening hand targets:** 2–3 lands including at least one of each color (or fixing land), a 2-mana rock, and ideally a flash or haste creature alongside Sonic. Mulligan any hand that can't cast Sonic by turn 4 or that has no action in the first two turns.

**Early game (turns 1–4):**
- T1: Sol Ring, or a pain land + Spectral Sailor (flash, can hold up).
- T2: 2-mana rock. Keep mana open for Swan Song or Dispatch if opponents are threatening.
- T3: Cast Sonic. Then immediately cast any instant (Opt, Swan Song) to hold up interaction.
- T4: Attack with Sonic + one flash/haste creature. Two counters placed. Keep up interaction on opponents' turns.

**Mid game (turns 5–8):**
- Priority: Get Kilo or Tekuthal into play alongside Sonic. The first Kilo + Sonic attack puts counters AND proliferates — this is when the engine ignites.
- Use Tezzeret's Gambit (with Tekuthal = draw 2 + 2 proliferates) to double-proliferate at sorcery speed.
- Use Goblin Smuggler to give any creature haste, enabling surprise beneficiaries of Sonic's trigger.
- Deploy Brineborn Cutthroat on opponents' end steps to get it a flash counter + Sonic's counter on the next attack.

**Late game:**
- Win with Deepglow Skate after 5–6 proliferate turns: all creatures have 15+ counters, Deepglow doubles everything.
- Win with Knuckles + The Reaver Cleaver: one connected double-strike hit generates overwhelming Treasure and mana.
- Win with Wake the Past + Cyberdrive Awakener on the same turn: all artifacts return with haste AND become 4/4 fliers, Sonic triggers on all of them.
- Rionya + 3 spells + Kilo = 4 hasted Kilo copies proliferating twice each = 8 proliferates in one combat step.

## Analysis

**Strengths:**
- Counter snowball is extremely difficult to interact with — removing one creature doesn't stop the strategy; the others still have their counters.
- Multiple independent win conditions: combat damage, The Reaver Cleaver combo, Deepglow Skate double, Wake the Past alpha.
- Heavy equipment tutors (Open the Armory, Fabricate) make key pieces consistent.
- All lands are functional: no non-producing lands, no pure enters-tapped without upside.

**Weaknesses / Missing Staples:**
- **Enchantment removal:** The deck has no dedicated enchantment removal beyond Generous Gift and Chaos Warp. Rule of Law (one spell per turn) completely shuts down the token generation from Manaform Hellkite.
- **Stax vulnerability:** Rest in Peace or Leyline of the Void hurts Dig Through Time and Treasure Cruise. No graveyard hate in deck either.
- **Counter hate:** Solemnity (no +1/+1 counters) or Atraxa opponents proliferating their own counters while you can't use yours.
- **Blue/White overrepresentation:** The deck leans slightly heavy on Blue and Blue/White cards; some hands may struggle to cast Red spells early.

## Next Steps

- **Test Kilo + Tekuthal consistency:** Does the proliferate engine come online fast enough? If not, consider swapping Mirrorwing Dragon for another cheap flash/haste threat.
- **Consider adding:** Strionic Resonator (copies Sonic's attack trigger for double counters), Swiftfoot Boots was included but if you build this physically, you'll need to take it from the Bria draft.
- **Possible cuts:** Mirrorwing Dragon (5 CMC with no haste/flash, slow setup) → Coruscation Mage (if given haste via equipment, pings all opponents per spell).
- **Note on shared cards with Bria draft:** Several cards appear in both draft decks. If building physically, you'll need to pick one deck for each: Goldspan Dragon, Manaform Hellkite, Rionya Fire Dancer, Brazen Borrower, Chrome Host Seedshark, Stormcatch Mentor, Solemn Simulacrum, Faerie Mastermind, Talisman of Creativity, Lightning Greaves, Swiftfoot Boots, and many instants/sorceries.

## Suggested Deck Names

- **Gotta Go Fast** — Sonic's own named ability on the card; instantly recognizable to anyone who's seen it and captures the entire game plan in three words.
- **Speed Demon** — Punchy two-word name; aggressive and evocative, describes the haste-heavy play style and the Jeskai chaos.
- **Counter Rush** — Captures both halves of the strategy: the +1/+1 counter accumulation and the relentless attack-every-turn tempo.
- **Rings and Rings** — Sonic collects rings; this deck collects counters and Treasure tokens, and "rings" works on both the game-mechanic and flavor levels.
