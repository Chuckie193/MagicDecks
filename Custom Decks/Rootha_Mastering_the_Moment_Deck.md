# Rootha_Mastering_the_Moment_Deck — Izzet Spellslinger Commander

Commander: Rootha, Mastering the Moment ({2}{U}{R}, Blue/Red)

## Overview
- **Strategy:** Cast instants and sorceries with high printed mana values (made cheap through Delve, cost reduction, and Treasure ramp) to trigger Rootha's combat ability, creating massive X/X flying haste Elemental tokens each turn. Stack magecraft payoffs (Archmage Emeritus, Storm-Kiln Artist, Prismari Pianist, Manaform Hellkite) to generate value from every spell, then close with a flying token army — supercharged by Brudiclad converting all tokens into copies of your largest Elemental.
- **Intended for:** Both (1v1 and multiplayer)

---

## Lore

Rootha's genius is measured in power/toughness. One cheap delve spell *cracks the sky open* and out tumbles an elemental the size of a stadium. Her crew matches every crescendo. *The bigger the cast, the bigger the cast.*

## Key Synergies

- **Rootha + Delve Spells**: Treasure Cruise (CMC 8) cast for {U} and Dig Through Time (CMC 9) cast for {U}{U} both trigger Rootha for 8/8 or 9/9 flying haste Elementals at effectively zero spell cost. Furygale Flocking (CMC 10) and Volcanic Salvo (CMC 12) push Rootha's token to 10/10 or 12/12.
- **Rootha + Veyran, Voice of Duality**: Veyran doubles all magecraft and "spellcaster" triggered abilities — Rootha's beginning-of-combat trigger fires **twice**, generating two massive tokens from a single spell cast. Every other magecraft card also doubles.
- **Storm-Kiln Artist + Harmonic Prodigy**: Storm-Kiln is a Shaman; Harmonic Prodigy makes Shaman triggers fire an additional time. Each spell generates **two** Treasure tokens instead of one, enabling explosive chaining.
- **Galazeth Prismari + Treasure tokens**: Every Treasure (from Storm-Kiln, Goldspan Dragon, Big Score, Unexpected Windfall, Prismari Command) can tap to contribute toward spell mana via Galazeth. A board of four Treasures = effectively four free mana for your next instant/sorcery.
- **Brudiclad + Token Army**: Brudiclad creates a 2/1 Myr at the start of combat, then converts every other token into a copy of your chosen token. One 9/9 Elemental from Rootha plus ten 1/1 tokens from Prismari Pianist = eleven 9/9 flying haste Elementals swinging in.
- **Mystic Sanctuary + Delve Spells**: Returning Treasure Cruise or Dig Through Time from your graveyard to the top of your library with Mystic Sanctuary lets you reuse your highest-impact Delve spell. Works especially well if you cast it once, fill the graveyard, and loop it.

## Short Mulligan and Play Notes

- **Early game (turns 1–3):** Lead with mana rocks (Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Fellwar Stone) and cost reducers (Stormcatch Mentor, Thunderclap Drake). You want 4–5 mana and at least one magecraft payoff by turn 4.
- **Mid game (turns 4–6):** Cast Rootha and immediately follow with any instant or sorcery the same turn (even Expressive Iteration) to get your first Elemental token at the start of combat. Begin filling your graveyard with instants/sorceries to fuel Delve.
- **Late game (turns 7+):** Cast Treasure Cruise or Dig Through Time (often for {U}–{U}{U}) to generate 8/9 Rootha tokens each combat. Once you have 2–3 tokens, deploy Brudiclad to convert the entire board into copies of the biggest one and attack for lethal.
- **Key interactions:**
  - Delve cards pay for themselves: casting Treasure Cruise for {U} generates an 8/8 Elemental. It's almost never correct to pay full price.
  - Veyran must be in play **before** you cast the spell that triggers Rootha — the trigger doubling happens at the moment the trigger would fire.
  - Mystic Sanctuary can put Treasure Cruise on top of your library to reuse it. Pair with Terramorphic Expanse or Fabled Passage sacrifices to enable Sanctuary's untapped condition.
  - Hall of Oracles scales multiplicatively — with 6 Elemental tokens, it generates 6 mana of any color. Excellent for casting Surge to Victory or Volcanic Salvo.

## Why These Choices

- **Delve spells are the core engine.** Treasure Cruise (CMC 8, draw 3) and Dig Through Time (CMC 9, tutor 2) cost effectively zero mana once your graveyard is stocked, while generating 8/9 Rootha tokens. The deck is built to fill the graveyard fast through Thirst for Knowledge, Deep Analysis flashback, Expressive Iteration, and natural spell casting.
- **Veyran is the most important card after Rootha.** Two tokens instead of one every combat is exponentially better, especially when one is already a 9/9.
- **Cost reducers stack.** Stormcatch Mentor + Thunderclap Drake together save {2} per instant/sorcery. With Storm-Kiln Artist creating Treasures and Galazeth converting those Treasures to spell mana, the deck can cast 3–4 spells in a turn by turn 6.
- **Brudiclad is the surprise finisher.** Opponents rarely see it coming. You might only have a 3/3 and some 1/1s on board, but Brudiclad turns the full token army into 3/3 copies with haste at the start of combat.
- **Added Idol of Oblivion** for reliable mid-game card draw. This deck creates tokens every single turn (Rootha trigger, Prismari Pianist, Manaform Hellkite, Brudiclad Myr) — Idol draws a card for free every turn.
- **Added Swan Song** as the cheapest possible protection. Countering a Cyclonic Rift or board wipe for {U} with no setup is invaluable.
- **Added Bria, Riptide Rogue** because a board of flying Elementals is still blockable. Bria's attack trigger makes your token army unblockable (they're all Elementals sharing a type after Brudiclad), enabling lethal out of nowhere.

## Analysis

**Strengths:**
- Exponential token scaling: one Treasure Cruise in a full graveyard generates an 8/8 token, 2+ Treasures, and potentially 3 cards — all for {U}
- Multiple overlapping value engines (Archmage Emeritus draws, Storm-Kiln makes mana, Prismari Pianist makes more tokens) make the deck resilient; any two engines together generate inevitability
- Flexible interaction: removal (Abrade, Chaos Warp, Reality Shift), counterspells (Swan Song, Arcane Denial), bounce (Aether Gale), and board wipes (Blasphemous Act, Chain Reaction) allow the deck to answer any threat type
- Brudiclad is a one-card finisher once you have a critical token mass

**Weaknesses/Missing Staples:**
- **Graveyard hate:** Exile effects like Rest in Peace or Tormod's Crypt shut down the Delve engine. The deck has no graveyard protection beyond Mystic Sanctuary as a minor recovery tool.
- **Stax/tax effects:** Rule of Law, Arcane Laboratory, or Drannith Magistrate each effectively turn off the deck. Arcane Denial and Swan Song help but can't stop everything.
- **Early aggression:** The deck has limited interaction before turn 3 and is weak to fast aggressive starts. Blasphemous Act and Chain Reaction are strong catches but arrive late.
- **Commander reliance:** Without Rootha in play, the deck generates token value through Prismari Pianist and Manaform Hellkite but loses its explosive potential. Lightning Greaves + Air Shoes help protect her.

## Suggested Deck Names

- **The Bigger the Cast** — Leans into the double meaning: "cast" as in spellcasting and the theatrical sense of a cast of characters. The bigger the spell, the bigger the token army.
- **Masterclass** — Rootha runs a masterclass in turning cheap spells into catastrophic threats; works as both a Prismari/artistic identity and a mechanical descriptor.
- **Colossal Artistry** — References the Prismari Artistry precon while capturing the deck's strategy of creating colossal X/X tokens from artfully cheap spells.
- **Deluge of Grandeur** — "Deluge" echoes Delve (the engine) and the wave of Elementals crashing down; "Grandeur" fits the Prismari performing-arts flavor.
