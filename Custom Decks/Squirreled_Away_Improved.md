# Squirreled Away Improved — Golgari Squirrel Tokens Commander
*(Working title — see Suggested Deck Names below)*

**Commander:** Hazel of the Rootbloom (2 generic, Black, Green; Black/Green)

## Overview
- **Strategy:** Flood the board with Squirrel tokens using Hazel's end-step doubling trigger, then win through combat or drain opponents to zero with stacked death-trigger aristocrats (Nadier's Nightblade, Zulaport Cutthroat, Bastion of Remembrance, Poison-Tip Archer). Hazel also taps tokens for coloured mana, so a critical mass of Squirrels lets you chain expensive spells for free. Chatterfang amplifies every token creation event, and Skullclamp converts 1/1 deaths into two cards each.
- **Intended for:** Both (1v1 and multiplayer pod)

## Lore

Hazel taps her chittering horde as a living mana battery, then each sunset doubles them. Her enemies brace for an army — but it's the nibbling that kills them. *Every acorn counts.*

## Decklist (100 cards)

### Commander (1)
- **Hazel of the Rootbloom** — The engine of the whole deck: taps tokens to produce coloured mana and doubles Squirrel tokens at each end step; protects her early with Air Shoes.

### Creatures (31)
- **Academy Manufactor** — Converts any Food, Clue, or Treasure creation into all three; massively amplifies every token synergy in the deck.
- **Beledros Witherbloom** — Creates a Pest token each upkeep for sacrifice value; the 10-life untap-all-lands ability enables explosive turns when you're ahead on board.
- **Chatterfang, Squirrel General** — Whenever you create tokens, create that many extra Squirrels; effectively doubles all token production and is the deck's best threat.
- **Chittering Witch** — Creates one 1/1 black Rat token per opponent on ETB; sacrifice a creature to shrink any threat for 1B.
- **Deep Forest Hermit** — ETB creates four 1/1 Squirrels and pumps all Squirrels; Vanishing means it eventually dies itself, triggering all your death effects.
- **Druid of the Spade** — While you control any token, this is effectively a 4/3 trampler for 3 mana; a powerful early attacker in this token-dense deck.
- **End-Raze Forerunners** — 7-mana finisher that gives all creatures +2/+2, vigilance, trample, and haste; ends games in a single attack step.
- **Gilded Goose** — 1-drop that creates a Food on ETB; can tap to produce any colour via Food sacrifice; excellent early ramp and Academy Manufactor trigger.
- **Haywire Mite** — 1-drop artifact creature that exiles noncreature artifacts or enchantments on sacrifice; cheap, on-demand removal.
- **Hazel's Brewmaster** — Squirrel creature that creates a Food on ETB or attack, and lets Foods copy activated abilities from exiled creature cards; synergises with Academy Manufactor.
- **Honored Dreyleader** — Gains a +1/+1 counter for each Squirrel or Food already in play when it enters, and for every subsequent one; can rapidly become a massive trampler.
- **Insatiable Frugivore** — Creates Food on ETB; sacrifice X Foods to give all creatures +X/+0 and menace until end of turn; a surprise combat finisher.
- **Llanowar Elves** — 1-drop green ramp; enables a turn-3 Hazel with a land drop on turn 2.
- **Morbid Opportunist** — Draws a card the first time each turn a creature dies; excellent, consistent card draw in sacrifice strategies.
- **Nadier's Nightblade** — Each time any token leaves the battlefield, each opponent loses 1 life; a relentless drain engine when tokens are sacrificed or die in combat.
- **Plaguecrafter** — Forces each player to sacrifice a creature or planeswalker, or discard; clean ETB removal that also disrupts opponents' strategies.
- **Poison-Tip Archer** — Reach and deathtouch; opponents lose 1 life whenever any of your creatures die; stacks with Zulaport Cutthroat and Bastion of Remembrance.
- **Prosperous Innkeeper** — Creates a Treasure on ETB; gains 1 life whenever another creature you control enters (life adds up fast with token floods).
- **Ravenous Squirrel** — On-tribe 1-mana Squirrel that grows with every sacrifice; activated ability converts any sacrifice into 1 life + draw for 1BG.
- **Reassembling Skeleton** — Returns from graveyard to battlefield tapped for 1B; infinite sacrifice fodder for Skullclamp draws, Woe Strider scries, and drain effects.
- **Scurry of Squirrels** — Double Myriad creates temporary attacking copies across multiple opponents; deals combat damage to draw cards via its triggered ability.
- **Skyfisher Spider** — Sacrifices any creature on ETB to destroy a nonland permanent; flexible removal wrapped in a 3/3 reach body.
- **Squirrel Sovereign** — Tribal lord that gives all other Squirrels +1/+1; stacks on top of Deep Forest Hermit and Chitterspitter buffs.
- **Squirrelanoids** — 1-drop Squirrel with deathtouch; blocks large creatures profitably and counts toward every Squirrel synergy.
- **The Odd Acorn Gang** — 5/5 Legendary Squirrel with menace, trample, and reach; lets any Squirrel pump another for +2/+2 trample; draws cards when Squirrels deal combat damage.
- **Thrashing Brontodon** — 3/4 body; sacrifice it at any time to destroy any artifact or enchantment; reliable on-demand removal.
- **Tireless Provisioner** — Landfall creates a Food or Treasure token; every land drop further fuels the token and mana engine.
- **Toski, Bearer of Secrets** — Indestructible Squirrel that can't be countered; draws a card whenever any of your creatures deals combat damage; outstanding long-game engine.
- **Vinereap Mentor** — Squirrel Druid that creates a Food token on both ETB and on death; triggers Honored Dreyleader, Academy Manufactor, and gives sacrifice value.
- **Woe Strider** — Free sacrifice outlet that scries 1; creates a Goat token on ETB; can escape from graveyard as a 5/4; keeps sacrifice effects flowing without mana.
- **Zulaport Cutthroat** — Drains each opponent 1 life whenever any creature you control dies; stacks directly with Nadier's Nightblade, Bastion, and Poison-Tip Archer.

### Artifacts & Mana (10)
- **Air Shoes (Swiftfoot Boots)** — Gives Hazel (or any key creature) hexproof and haste; the single most important upgrade — protects the engine from removal and lets Hazel attack or activate abilities the turn she lands.
- **Arcane Signet** — 2-mana rock that produces Black or Green; core ramp.
- **Chitterspitter** — Creates Squirrel tokens for G; accumulates acorn counters when you sacrifice tokens, giving all Squirrels +1/+1 per counter; snowballs with Hazel's doublers.
- **Golgari Signet** — 2-mana rock; standard Golgari colour fixing.
- **Idol of Oblivion** — Draws a card whenever creature tokens enter under your control; converts every token creation event into card advantage.
- **Maskwood Nexus** — Makes all creatures every type; every creature in the deck becomes a Squirrel, unlocking all tribal synergies and creating 2/2 Shapeshifters.
- **Skullclamp** — Equipping to a 1/1 immediately kills it and draws two cards; draws absurd amounts with a board of Squirrel tokens.
- **Sol Ring** — Format staple; 2 free colourless mana from turn 1.
- **Sword of the Squeak** — Auto-attaches to any Squirrel entering; scales its bonus with the count of 1-power creatures you control; natural synergy with the token strategy.
- **Talisman of Resilience** — 2-mana rock that taps for Black or Green; standard Golgari ramp.

### Enchantments (6)
- **Bastion of Remembrance** — Creates a Human Soldier token on ETB; drains each opponent 1 life and gains you 1 life every time any of your creatures die.
- **Beastmaster Ascension** — Accumulates quest counters as creatures attack; 7+ counters means all your creatures get +5/+5; a swarm of Squirrels can load this in a single combat step.
- **Binding the Old Gods** — Saga: destroys a nonland permanent, ramps a Forest, then grants all your creatures deathtouch for a turn; flexible utility and ramp.
- **Gourmand's Talent** — Class enchantment that makes all your artifacts Foods; Level 2 creates 3/3 Raccoons when you gain life for the first time each turn; Level 3 puts +1/+1 counters on all creatures when you gain life.
- **Moldervine Reclamation** — Draw a card and gain 1 life whenever any creature you control dies; outstanding sustained card advantage in sacrifice strategies.
- **Squirrel Nest** — Enchants a land to give it a tap-to-create-Squirrel ability; continuous, resilient token generation that Hazel then doubles at end step.

### Instants (6)
- **Cache Grab** — Mill 4 and return a permanent card to hand; creates a Food if you control a Squirrel or returned a Squirrel card; excellent card selection and recovery.
- **Deadly Dispute** — Sacrifice a creature at instant speed to draw two cards and create a Treasure; powers up Academy Manufactor and generates mana from death.
- **Plumb the Forbidden** — Sacrifice creatures to draw cards; copies for each creature sacrificed; spectacular when you have a board of tokens to spend.
- **Putrefy** — Destroy target artifact or creature (can't be regenerated); staple flexible instant removal in Golgari.
- **Second Harvest** — Creates a copy of every token you control; doubles an entire Squirrel army at instant speed in response to any threat.
- **Tear Asunder** — Exile target artifact or enchantment; kick 1B to exile any nonland permanent instead; flexible removal with a high ceiling.

### Sorceries (7)
- **Casualties of War** — 6-mana bomb: destroy a creature, artifact, enchantment, planeswalker, and land simultaneously; the best single-target catch-all in the deck.
- **Chatterstorm** — Storm sorcery that creates Squirrels; cast after several other spells for multiplicative token production in one turn.
- **Decree of Pain** — Destroys all creatures and draws a card for each one that dies; devastating board reset that refills your hand.
- **Maelstrom Pulse** — Destroy a nonland permanent and all others with the same name; excellent answer to tokens, staples, or multiple copies of a threat.
- **Shamanic Revelation** — Draws cards equal to the number of creatures you control; gains life for each creature with the same power; with 10+ Squirrels, often draws 10+ cards.
- **Swarmyard Massacre** — Creates 2 Squirrel tokens, then deals -1/-1 to every creature that isn't an Insect, Rat, Spider, or Squirrel for each such creature you control; a one-sided board wipe that spares your army.
- **Windgrace's Judgment** — Destroy up to X nonland permanents (X = number of opponents); scales perfectly from 1v1 to multiplayer pod.

## Key Synergies
- **Hazel of the Rootbloom + Chatterfang, Squirrel General + Any Token Creator**: Hazel doubles Squirrel tokens at end step; Chatterfang adds equal Squirrels to every token creation event. One Food token from Gilded Goose triggers Chatterfang (adding a Squirrel), and Hazel doubles it at end step into two. Cascades into an army in 2–3 turns.
- **Skullclamp + 1/1 Tokens**: Equipping Skullclamp to any 1/1 (Squirrels, Pests, Rats) kills it (–1 toughness) and immediately draws two cards. With 15+ tokens, a single Skullclamp can draw the rest of your deck in one turn cycle.
- **Nadier's Nightblade + Zulaport Cutthroat + Bastion of Remembrance + Poison-Tip Archer**: Stack all four drain effects. Each creature death drains each opponent 4 life. Sacrificing 25 tokens in a pod of 3 removes 300 total life across opponents — enough to kill everyone simultaneously.
- **Academy Manufactor + Gilded Goose / Tireless Provisioner / Vinereap Mentor**: Each Food creation becomes Food + Clue + Treasure. Combined with Hazel and Chatterfang, a single Landfall step can produce 9+ tokens, which Hazel taps for free mana, then doubles at end step.
- **Reassembling Skeleton + Skullclamp + Woe Strider**: Return Skeleton from graveyard for 1B → equip Skullclamp (kills it, draws 2 cards) → return again → draw 2 more. Costs 2B per cycle but generates infinite cards and drains from Nadier/Zulaport each time.

## Short Mulligan and Play Notes
- **Early game (turns 1–4):** Prioritise mana accelerants (Llanowar Elves, Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Gilded Goose). Aim to enchant a Forest with Squirrel Nest by turn 2–3. Cast Hazel on turn 3 off an early ramp piece. Equip Air Shoes immediately on Hazel.
- **Mid game (turns 5–8):** Activate Hazel's end-step ability every turn — doubly Squirrels is the core game plan. Cast Chatterfang to double all token production. Use Skullclamp to convert 1/1s into cards and keep your hand stocked. Look to establish Moldervine Reclamation or Morbid Opportunist before sacrificing tokens en masse.
- **Late game (turn 9+):** With 15+ Squirrels, tap them all through Hazel for free mana to cast Decree of Pain, Casualties of War, or End-Raze Forerunners. If Beastmaster Ascension has 7 counters, a single attack wave with even 5 Squirrels is lethal. Stack drain effects (Nadier, Zulaport, Bastion) before a Plumb the Forbidden or mass sacrifice to close out multiple opponents simultaneously.
- **Key interactions:** Chatterfang's token-doubling triggers from Hazel's copies — protect Chatterfang at all costs. Swarmyard can regenerate key Squirrels (Toski, Chatterfang) from targeted removal. Woe Strider provides a free sacrifice outlet at any time — never be caught unable to respond to graveyard exile or bounce effects.

## Why These Choices (Summary)
- Every new creature is either a Squirrel (Squirrelanoids, Vinereap Mentor) or serves a role the precon's cuts freed up (Druid of the Spade as aggressive threat, Llanowar Elves as 1-drop ramp, Thrashing Brontodon as instant-speed artifact/enchantment removal, Reassembling Skeleton as recurring sac fodder).
- Air Shoes (Swiftfoot Boots) was the clearest missing piece — Hazel is the entire engine and dies to any instant-speed removal without it. Equipping on the same turn she lands (haste) immediately lets you abuse her mana ability.
- Cuts prioritised removing off-tribe cards (Arasta's Spiders, Ogre Slumlord's Rats) and low-impact one-offs (Saw in Half, Wolfwillow Haven aura) that competed with more synergistic slots.

## Analysis

**Strengths:**
- Explosive, self-reinforcing token engine: Hazel doubles Squirrels every turn, Chatterfang doubles all token creation, and Squirrel Nest generates tokens without spending cards.
- Multiple redundant win conditions: combat via Beastmaster Ascension or End-Raze Forerunners; drain via Nadier + Zulaport + Bastion + Poison-Tip; value engine via Toski + Skullclamp.
- Resilient to single-card removal: the deck functions even after losing Hazel, though slower.

**Weaknesses / Missing Staples:**
- **Flying defence:** Very few fliers; an opponent with an air army can go over your squirrels. Swarmyard Massacre helps but doesn't give you permanent aerial coverage.
- **Counter magic:** Golgari has no counterspells; key pieces (Hazel, Chatterfang, Skullclamp) are all vulnerable to removal on the stack unless Air Shoes is in play.
- **1v1 speed:** The curve peaks at 3–4 CMC; against aggressive 1v1 builds you need to find Llanowar Elves or Sol Ring in the opening hand or you'll be behind.

## Next Steps (Optional Suggestions)
- If Gourmand's Talent never levels up past Level 1, consider cutting it for a cheap interactive spell or additional removal — the class is genuinely powerful at Level 2+ but relies on having Academy Manufactor in play.
- Doubling Season (not currently owned) would be the single strongest upgrade: it doubles counters AND token creation, stacking with Hazel and Chatterfang.
- Second Harvest is your flash "combo" turn in 1v1 — hold it as a response to a board wipe to immediately recreate your army at instant speed.

## Suggested Deck Names

- **Acorn to Acorn** — Hazel's doubling engine turns one acorn into an exponentially growing army; also a pun on "from here to there," because there is nowhere the squirrels won't reach.
- **Going Nuts** — Punchy two-word option; works literally (squirrels) and mechanically (the deck goes completely off the rails by turn 6).
- **Roots & Ruin** — Captures the Golgari duality of Hazel's Rootbloom legacy (green token growth) and aristocrat sacrifice (ruin), with a nice alliteration.
- **The Nibble Protocol** — Hazel's drain strategy makes opponents feel nibbled to death; puns on "the kill protocol" with appropriate squirrel energy.
