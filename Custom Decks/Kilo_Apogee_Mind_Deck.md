# Kilo_Apogee_Mind_Deck — Station Proliferate Commander (1v1)

Commander: Kilo, Apogee Mind (1WUR, Blue/Red/White — Jeskai)

## Overview
- **Strategy:** Built from the Counter Intelligence Commander precon with Kilo swapped in as commander in place of Inspirit. Kilo, Apogee Mind is a 3-mana 3/3 Legendary Artifact Creature with Haste; whenever it becomes tapped, proliferate. The **Station** mechanic is the primary tap engine — tapping Kilo to Station a Spacecraft (Inspirit or Uthros Research Craft) contributes 3 charge counters to the Spacecraft **and** fires a proliferate trigger simultaneously. Inspirit moves from the command zone to the 99 as a dual-purpose Station target and board protector (at 8+ charge counters, all your other artifacts — including Kilo, a Legendary Artifact Creature — gain hexproof and indestructible). Tekuthal, Inquiry Dominus doubles every proliferate, turning each Kilo tap into two proliferations. Primary win condition: Darksteel Reactor at 20 charge counters. Secondary: Lux Artillery's 30-total-counter trigger deals 10 damage per player; Titan Forge 9/9 Golems; combat beatdown.
- **Intended for:** Single opponent (1v1 / duel)
- **Base:** Counter Intelligence Commander precon — 5 upgrades applied (see Upgrades section)

## Lore

Kilo doesn't wait for permission — every tap fans the charge across the fleet. Station her. Proliferate. The reactor climbs. *They came to fight a ship. They didn't know the ship was a clock.*

### Commander (1)
- **Kilo, Apogee Mind** (1WUR, Legendary Artifact Creature — Robot Artificer, 3/3, Haste) — The engine. Haste means it attacks the turn it arrives. As a 3/3, Kilo contributes 3 charge counters per Station activation. **Swap from precon:** Kilo was in the 99 of Counter Intelligence with Inspirit as commander; they switch roles here.

### Instants (8)

- **Chaos Warp** (2R) — Shuffles any permanent into its controller's library and replaces it with the top card. Universal answer to anything — indestructible permanents, hexproof threats, problematic enchantments that Jeskai otherwise can't remove cleanly.
- **Dispatch** (W, Metalcraft) — Taps target creature; with Metalcraft (three or more artifacts — trivial here), exiles it instead. The best 1-mana exile effect in the deck.
- **Experimental Augury** (1U) — Look at top 3, put one in hand, rest on bottom, then proliferate. Card selection and a proliferate trigger in one 2-mana instant; ideal on the opponent's end step.
- **Pull from Tomorrow** (XUU) — Draw X cards, discard one. Massive late-game hand refill; X=5 or more is common with Empowered Autogenerator or Crystalline Crawler online.
- **Ripples of Potential** (1U) — Proliferate, then choose any number of permanents that gained a counter this way — those phase out until your next turn. Proliferate then phase out Kilo, Darksteel Reactor, or Inspirit to protect them from a board wipe, leaving them untouchable until you untap.
- **Swan Song** (U) — Counter any instant, sorcery, or enchantment. Opponent gets a 2/2 Bird, but shutting down a game-ending spell or hate piece is always the right trade.
- **Swords to Plowshares** (W) — Gold-standard creature exile for 1 mana.
- **Thirst for Knowledge** (2U) — Draw 3, discard 2 unless you discard an artifact card. Trivially satisfied with spare mana rocks or Soul-Guide Lantern. Exceptional instant-speed card advantage.

### Sorceries (7)

- **Chain Reaction** (2RR) — Deals X damage to each creature where X = total creatures on the battlefield. A board wipe that scales with crowded boards; your artifact creatures survive if Inspirit's 8+ protection is active.
- **Fabricate** (2U) ★ **Added upgrade.** Search your library for any artifact card, reveal it, put it into your hand. Tutors for Darksteel Reactor to start the win clock, Inspirit to activate Station, Tekuthal to double proliferates, Swiftfoot Boots to protect Kilo, or any other artifact answer you need.
- **Fumigate** (3WW) — Destroy all creatures; gain 1 life per creature destroyed. Symmetric wrath kept as a reset button when the board state requires clearing everything; Inspirit's 8+ protection has to be temporarily absent for this to affect your own artifact creatures.
- **Organic Extinction** (8WW, Improvise) — Destroy all nonartifact creatures. Improvise lets you tap artifacts to pay 1 each, making the real cost often 2WW or less. One-sided board wipe that leaves your artifact creatures standing.
- **Tezzeret's Gambit** (3U/P) — Draw 2 cards and proliferate. With Tekuthal, proliferates four times. Cast whenever possible; the Phyrexian mana option makes this 4 mana or 3 mana + 2 life.
- **Universal Surveillance** (XUUU, Improvise) — Draw X cards. With many artifacts on board, this draws 4-8 cards for minimal real mana investment.
- **Wake the Past** (5RW) — Return all artifact cards from your graveyard to the battlefield with haste. Explosive rebuild after a board wipe; each artifact entering fires Surge Conductor's proliferate. Fire Wake the Past before casting Tekuthal if both are in hand for maximum counter spread.

## Upgrades Applied (5 changes from the Counter Intelligence precon)

### Cards Added
| Added Card | Source | Replaces |
|---|---|---|
| Galazeth Prismari | PrismariArtistry | Cloud Key |
| Swiftfoot Boots (Air Shoes) | SonictheHedgehog TurboGear | Patrolling Peacemaker |
| Fabricate | SonictheHedgehog ChasingAdventure | Chrome Host Seedshark |
| Fabled Passage | PrismariArtistry | Evolving Wilds (land) |
| Reliquary Tower | PrismariArtistry | Lonely Sandbar (land) |

### Reasoning
- **Galazeth Prismari replaces Cloud Key:** Cloud Key reduces one spell type by 1 mana — marginal. Galazeth gives every artifact a tap ability for mana, meaning Kilo can be tapped for mana on the way to casting an instant or sorcery. That tap fires Kilo's proliferate **before combat**, giving two proliferate triggers per turn with Tekuthal in play. With Tekuthal, that's four proliferations per turn. Cloud Key was never going to do that.
- **Swiftfoot Boots replaces Patrolling Peacemaker:** In 1v1, your opponent will always have removal pointed at Kilo. Patrolling Peacemaker (a 0/0 that enters with two +1/+1 counters) adds minor counter density but doesn't protect your engine. Swiftfoot Boots gives Kilo hexproof (no targeting) and haste (attack immediately), costing 1 to equip. It's the difference between Kilo surviving or dying the turn you play it.
- **Fabricate replaces Chrome Host Seedshark:** Chrome Host Seedshark creates Incubator tokens when you cast noncreature spells — those tokens can be proliferated but don't directly advance Inspirit or the Reactor. Fabricate tutors for any artifact in the deck: Darksteel Reactor to start the win clock, Inspirit if it hasn't appeared, Tekuthal to double proliferates, Swiftfoot Boots to protect Kilo. Tutoring for the exact piece you need is categorically stronger than generating side tokens.
- **Fabled Passage replaces Evolving Wilds:** Fabled Passage does exactly what Evolving Wilds does (sacrifice, fetch a basic land) but once you control 4+ lands, the fetched land enters untapped. Strictly better in every game beyond turn 4 — an automatic upgrade.
- **Reliquary Tower replaces Lonely Sandbar:** Lonely Sandbar is a cycling land that's nearly always just a tapped Island. Reliquary Tower eliminates your maximum hand size, which matters significantly in a deck with Pull from Tomorrow, Thirst for Knowledge, Jhoira Weatherlight Captain, Universal Surveillance, and Thought Monitor all generating large card counts.

## Key Synergies

- **Kilo + Station (Inspirit or Uthros):** Tapping Kilo to Station a Spacecraft contributes 3 charge counters (Kilo's power) AND fires a proliferate trigger simultaneously. Spread those proliferate counters to Darksteel Reactor, Empowered Autogenerator, and Gavel of the Righteous at once. Station Uthros Research Craft for 3 counters + Uthros draw trigger + proliferate in one tap.
- **Kilo + Galazeth Prismari (double trigger per turn):** Galazeth grants all artifacts tap for mana on instants/sorceries. Tap Kilo for mana in your main phase = proliferate before combat. Kilo attacks = second proliferate. With Tekuthal in play, that's four proliferations every turn from Kilo alone.
- **Tekuthal doubles everything:** Every proliferate happens twice. One Tezzeret's Gambit = four proliferations. Karn's Bastion activation = two proliferations. Surge Conductor trigger on each artifact = two proliferations per artifact cast. Tekuthal is the must-remove threat.
- **Inspirit 8+ protects Kilo itself:** At 8+ charge counters, Inspirit grants all your other artifacts hexproof and indestructible. Kilo is a Legendary **Artifact** Creature — this protection applies to it directly. Targeted removal and most board wipes cannot touch your commander once this is online.
- **Deepglow Skate as a closer:** When it enters, double all counters on any number of target permanents. Targets Darksteel Reactor (10 counters → 20 = you win), Inspirit (4 counters → 8 = full protection online), Empowered Autogenerator (doubled mana production), and Astral Cornucopia (doubled mana production) all in one trigger.
- **Surge Conductor passive engine:** Every nontoken artifact that enters fires Surge Conductor's proliferate. A turn where you cast Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, and Insight Engine is three proliferate triggers before Kilo even attacks.
- **Wake the Past as combo closer:** Returns your entire artifact graveyard with haste. Each artifact entering fires Surge Conductor's proliferate. If you have 5+ artifacts in the graveyard, Wake the Past can advance Darksteel Reactor by 5-10 counters (doubled if Tekuthal is in hand and cast immediately after).
- **Resourceful Defense preserves progress:** If Inspirit is destroyed after reaching 7 charge counters, Resourceful Defense puts all 7 counters on the Reactor instead of losing them. Also lets you consolidate scattered counters when you're ready to cross a threshold.

## Short Mulligan and Play Notes

- **Keep hands with:** 2-3 lands hitting all three colors, at least 1 mana rock (Sol Ring / Arcane Signet ideal), and either Kilo protection (Swiftfoot Boots) or early proliferate (Surge Conductor). A turn-3 Kilo with haste is the ideal opener.
- **Early game (turns 1-4):** Ramp first — Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Etherium Sculptor. Play Kilo on turn 3 with haste; equip Swiftfoot Boots immediately (equip 1, protects from targeted removal and gives haste). Turn 4: Darksteel Reactor or Surge Conductor.
- **Mid game (turns 5-8):** Station Inspirit or Uthros every turn using Kilo. If Galazeth Prismari is out, also tap Kilo for mana before combat. Land Tekuthal and watch every proliferate double. Use Fabricate to tutor for the piece you're missing. Ripples of Potential on your Darksteel Reactor is a strong defensive play — proliferates it, then phases it out of harm's way until your next turn.
- **Late game:** Close out with Darksteel Reactor at 20 counters, or build toward Lux Artillery's 30-counter trigger. Deepglow Skate entering at 10 Reactor counters ends the game immediately.
- **Key interactions:**
  - Station is sorcery speed. Plan it in your main phase after summoning sickness clears (Swiftfoot Boots grants haste on entry).
  - Galazeth's mana ability on Kilo only works if you're casting an instant or sorcery with that mana. Have a spell ready, or just attack for the proliferate instead.
  - Avoid giving Kilo vigilance — it will not tap to attack, losing the proliferate trigger. No vigilance sources in this deck intentionally.
  - Inspirit's 8+ threshold is worth prioritizing specifically; even getting to 8 without winning, the hexproof+indestructible package shuts down most opponent interaction.
  - Chain Reaction and Fumigate both destroy your own creatures. Use them only when Inspirit is at 8+ (saving your artifact creatures from destruction) or when the board state forces it.

## Analysis

**Strengths:**
- Station + Kilo is doubly efficient: 3 charge counters per tap AND a proliferate trigger simultaneously, from a single creature action.
- Inspirit's 8+ protection creates a near-impervious board state in artifact-heavy decks — hexproof and indestructible across everything including Kilo.
- Multiple win conditions prevent the opponent from pivoting to a single hate strategy (Reactor at 20, Lux Artillery at 30, Titan Forge 9/9s, combat beatdown).
- Deep card draw via Jhoira, Thought Monitor, Insight Engine, Thirst for Knowledge, and Universal Surveillance.
- Improvise package (Kappa Cannoneer, Organic Extinction, Universal Surveillance) makes high-CMC cards nearly free in the late game.

**Weaknesses:**
- **Early vulnerability:** Kilo must survive long enough to Station twice or three times before Inspirit reaches 8 counters. Swiftfoot Boots addresses this, but heavy early pressure or counterspells can delay the engine.
- **Enchantment hate:** Only Chaos Warp cleanly handles resolved enchantments. Imprisonment effects on Kilo are very disruptive; Swan Song can stop them on the stack only.
- **Telegraphed win condition:** Darksteel Reactor announces the plan. Experienced 1v1 opponents will prioritise its removal or race hard before it reaches 20.
- **Station speed:** Reaching 8 counters on Inspirit via Kilo Station alone takes 2-3 turns minimum without Proliferate acceleration. Any removal during that window resets the counter base (Resourceful Defense partially mitigates this).

## Suggested Deck Names

- **Station to Station** — Every tap is another station. Every station is another proliferate. The clock never stops.
- **Tick Rate** — How fast the reactor charges. With Tekuthal, the tick rate doubles. Harmless-sounding until it isn't.
- **Full Charge** — Station at maximum power. Kilo at full tap. Darksteel Reactor approaching 20.
- **Proliferating Circumstances** — The board state keeps getting worse for your opponent. Circumstances entirely beyond their control.
