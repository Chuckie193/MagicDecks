# Hoard Mentality — Rakdos Dragons & Treasure Commander

Commander: Smaug the Impenetrable ({5}{B}{R}, Black/Red)

## Overview
- **Strategy**: Smaug is the biggest Dragon in a deck of 24 of them (Firdoch Core counts). Cost reducers and Dragon-only mana (Dragonspeaker Shaman, Dragonlord's Servant, Nogi, Orb of Dragonkind) put 4- to 7-drops on the table early, and Treasures from Smaug's own trigger and ten other sources pay for the rest. Damage aimed at your own indestructible commander (Dragon Tempest, Scourge of Valkas, Warstorm Surge, the burn suite, Leyline Tyrant) becomes still more Treasures (twice as many under Gratuitous Violence when a creature deals it, and cards as well under Requiem Monolith), and the pod dies to flying Dragons, spread damage (Parapet Thrasher, Drakuseth, Firespitter Whelp, Goldlust Triad's myriad) Exsanguinate off the hoard, or Dawnsire's 100 damage at Smaug every attack.
- **Intended for**: Multiplayer pod — two sweepers that pay Smaug Treasures (Blasphemous Act, and The Elder Dragon War's chapter I, which you can skip with read ahead), hard exile in Extended Absence, an edict, graveyard hate, group-scaling reach, a monarch engine, and removal that also hits artifacts and enchantments.
- **Card pool**: Avoid reserved decks — nothing from Full Deployment, Claws for Concern, Table Manners, Dance of the Elements or **Multiverse Reforged** (reserved 2026-10-08); spare copies of cards those decks use are still eligible (Swiftfoot Boots, for instance, has two free copies; Command Tower, Path of Ancestry, Arcane Signet and Sol Ring each still have a spare copy outside Multiverse Reforged). Reign of Dragons, Wretched Ranks and Keen Engineering are not reserved. This deck shares most of its Dragon core with **Reign of Dragons** and owns only one copy of many of those cards, so the two can't be assembled at the same time.

## Lore

Heroes arrive daily to slay Smaug and leave daily as cautionary tales. He yawns, and every dragon in the sky answers the dinner bell. *Treasure is nice, but a proper horde makes the hoard.*

## Decklist (100 cards)

### Commander (1)
- **Smaug the Impenetrable** — {5}{B}{R} 8/7 flying, indestructible, haste Dragon. **Whenever he is dealt noncombat damage, create that many Treasure tokens.** Indestructible shrugs off lethal damage, so damage aimed at him pays out instead of hurting; -X/-X, exile and sacrifice effects still answer him. He is also one of 24 Dragons in the deck, so every Dragon payoff counts him too.

### Creatures (26)
- **Sarkhan, Dragon Ascendant** — {1}{R} 2/2. Beholding a Dragon (control one or reveal one from hand) makes a **Treasure**, so he is turn-two ramp into a turn-three Dragon. He then grows and gains flying until end of turn whenever a Dragon enters.
- **Dragonlord's Servant** — {1}{R} 1/3. Dragon spells cost {1} less.
- **Dragonspeaker Shaman** — {1}{R}{R} 2/2. Dragon spells cost {2} less. With Dragonlord's Servant, Smaug himself costs {2}{B}{R}; stack Nogi and Hazoret's Monument too and he costs {B}{R}.
- **Nogi, Draco-Zealot** — {1}{R}{R} 3/3. Dragon spells cost {1} less. Once you control three Dragons, it becomes a 5/5 flying Dragon whenever it attacks.
- **Firespitter Whelp** — {2}{R} 2/2 flying Dragon. Pings **each opponent** for 1 whenever you cast a noncreature *or* Dragon spell: 61 of the 65 nonland cards qualify, plus Smaug from the command zone.
- **Leyline Tyrant** — {2}{R}{R} 4/4 flier. Red mana doesn't empty between steps and phases, so Treasures and rocks can be banked across combat. When it dies, pay any amount of {R} and it deals that much damage to any target, which can be your own Smaug for that many Treasures.
- **Thunderbreak Regent** — {2}{R}{R} 4/4 flier. Whenever an opponent targets a Dragon with a spell or ability, that player takes 3. It taxes every removal spell aimed at Smaug or any other Dragon.
- **Parapet Thrasher** — {2}{R}{R} 4/3 flier. When Dragons connect with an opponent, choose a mode (each once per turn): destroy one of that player's artifacts, deal 4 damage to **each other** opponent, or impulse-draw a card. One Smaug hit can mean 8 more damage across a three-opponent pod. Each mode can be chosen only once per turn, so extra attackers don't repeat a mode.
- **Manaform Hellkite** — {2}{R}{R} 4/4 flier. Every noncreature spell makes an X/X flying, hasty Dragon Illusion (X = mana spent) that is exiled at the next end step. The tokens are Dragons, so they trigger Dragon Tempest and Scourge of Valkas, and they can attack the turn they appear.
- **Smaug the Magnificent** — {2}{R}{R} 4/3 flying haste. A Treasure every upkeep, and each attack deals damage equal to your Treasures to **any target** — including your own Smaug for a refill.
- **Atsushi, the Blazing Sky** — {2}{R}{R} 4/4 flying, trample. When it dies, either play the top two cards of your library or take three Treasures — insurance against your own sweepers.
- **Thundermane Dragon** — {3}{R} 4/4 flier. You may cast creature spells with power 4 or greater from the top of your library, **with haste**. 17 other creatures here qualify (most of the Dragon suite, Lathliss and Skyline Despot among them), so it is a repeating card-advantage engine.
- **Immersturm Predator** — {2}{B}{R} 3/3 flying Vampire Dragon. Grows and exiles a graveyard card every time it becomes tapped; sacrificing **another** creature makes it indestructible and taps it, re-triggering itself. It survives your own wraths by eating a Sarkhan or a token.
- **Goldlust Triad** — {4}{R} 4/3 flier. **Myriad**: attacking makes a tapped-and-attacking copy for each *other* opponent, and each hit on a player makes a Treasure. In a three-opponent pod one swing is three Dragons (the copies trigger Dragon Tempest and Scourge of Valkas) and up to three Treasures.
- **Smaug, Wicked Worm** — {3}{B}{R} 5/5 flier. Enters with X tapped Treasures (X = opposing artifacts), then draws a card and costs you 1 life whenever you cast a spell with Treasure mana. Treasure turns are card-advantage turns.
- **Hoarding Dragon** — {3}{R}{R} 4/4 flier. Tutors an artifact into exile and returns it to your hand when it dies. Finds Dawnsire, Dragonstorm Globe, Dragon's Hoard or Sol Ring, and it *wants* to die to your own sweeper.
- **Dragonhawk, Fate's Tempest** — {3}{R}{R} 5/5 flier. On entering or attacking, exile cards equal to the number of power-4-or-greater creatures you control; play them until your next end step, and at that end step it deals 2 damage to each opponent for each card still exiled. Most of this deck qualifies.
- **Scourge of Valkas** — {2}{R}{R}{R} 4/4 flier. Whenever it **or another Dragon** enters, it deals X damage to any target, where X is your Dragon count. Aim it at Smaug, a creature or a face. `{R}`: +1/+0.
- **Rapacious Dragon** — {4}{R} 3/3 flier that arrives with **two** Treasures. It pays for the next Dragon.
- **Lathliss, Dragon Queen** — {4}{R}{R} 6/6 flier. Every other **nontoken** Dragon that enters makes a 5/5 flying Dragon token — a trigger on most of the deck. `{1}{R}` gives your Dragons +1/+0.
- **Scourge of the Throne** — {4}{R}{R} 5/5 flier. Dethrone, and its first attack each turn against the player with the most life untaps your attackers and adds a combat phase. It chases down whoever is leading the pod.
- **Tyrant's Familiar** — {5}{R}{R} 5/5 flying haste. **Lieutenant**: while you control Smaug it is a 7/7 and each attack deals 7 damage to target creature defending player controls — a repeating removal spell on a haste body.
- **Drakuseth, Maw of Flames** — {4}{R}{R}{R} 7/7 flier. Each attack deals 4 damage to any target and 3 to each of up to two others. Spread it across the pod, or put 4 into Smaug for four Treasures.
- **Skyline Despot** — {5}{R}{R} 5/5 flier. Makes you the **monarch** — an extra card each turn — and a 5/5 flying Dragon every upkeep you hold the crown. Your fliers are the best defence a monarch could ask for.
- **Shivan Devastator** — {X}{R} flying, haste Dragon Hydra with X +1/+1 counters. The deck's Treasure sink: it turns a hoard into a hasty finisher, and it is a Dragon entering for every trigger above.
- **Steel Hellkite** — {6} colourless 5/5 flying Dragon. `{X}` (once a turn): destroy each nonland permanent with mana value X controlled by a player it dealt combat damage to this turn, a scalable answer to artifacts and enchantments. `{2}`: +1/+0. It needs no red mana, and every Dragon reducer and Orb of Dragonkind applies.

### Enchantments (7)
- **Dragon Tempest** — {1}{R}. Fliers you control gain haste when they enter, and every Dragon that enters deals X damage to any target, X being your Dragon count. Point it at Smaug for X Treasures.
- **Warstorm Surge** — {5}{R}. Whenever a creature you control enters, it deals damage equal to its power to **any target**. Smaug entering can hit himself for eight Treasures, and every later Dragon is 4–6 more Treasures or 4–6 damage of removal or reach.
- **The Elder Dragon War** — {2}{R}{R} Saga with read ahead. I: 2 damage to each creature and each opponent (clears tokens and pings Smaug for two Treasures, but it also kills your own Sarkhan, Dragonspeaker Shaman and Firespitter Whelp). II: discard any number of cards and draw that many. III: a 4/4 flying Dragon token. Start on chapter II or III when the sweep would hurt you.
- **Outpost Siege** — {3}{R}. Choose **Khans**: exile the top card each upkeep and play it that turn, a steady stream of cards for the whole game. (Dragons mode, which pings whenever one of your creatures leaves, is the alternative if you plan a big sweeper turn.)
- **The Misty Mountains Cold** — {2}{R} Saga. A Treasure each chapter, but it checks after each one: the moment you control four or more Treasures it sacrifices itself for a 6/6 flying Dragon.
- **Gratuitous Violence** — {2}{R}{R}{R}. Damage your creatures would deal to a permanent or player is doubled. Dragon Tempest, Scourge of Valkas, Warstorm Surge, Drakuseth and Smaug the Magnificent all make a creature the source of their damage, so aimed at Smaug every one of them makes **twice the Treasures**: Smaug entering under Warstorm Surge is sixteen. Smaug also hits for 16 in combat, and Firespitter Whelp (2 to each opponent per spell), Parapet Thrasher (8 to each other opponent) and Tyrant's Familiar (14) double too. Burn spells and Spicy Oatmeal Pizza are not creatures, so they are not doubled.
- **Breaching Dragonstorm** — {4}{R}. On entering, exile cards from the top until a nonland card and cast it free if its mana value is 8 or less (every card here except Blasphemous Act). **Whenever a Dragon you control enters, it returns to your hand**, so with 24 Dragons it is a repeatable free spell.

### Artifacts & Mana (15)
- **Sol Ring** — {1}. The fastest ramp in the format.
- **Arcane Signet** — {2}. Taps for black or red, the whole of your commander's identity.
- **Fire Diamond** — {2}. Enters tapped, taps for {R}. Played on turn two it still gives four mana on turn three, and red is the colour the deck needs most.
- **Firdoch Core** — {3} Kindred Artifact with **changeling**, so it is a Dragon: taps for any colour, often costs {1} through the Dragon reducers, and entering triggers Dragon Tempest, Scourge of Valkas, Lathliss, Spit Flame's return and Breaching Dragonstorm's return. `{4}`: it becomes a 4/4 until end of turn. (It is not a creature on entry, so Warstorm Surge doesn't trigger.)
- **Mind Stone** — {2}. Ramp early, and cash it in for a card later.
- **Orb of Dragonkind** — {1}{R}. `{1},{T}`: add two mana of any colours, spent only on Dragon spells or Dragon abilities — it pays for most of the deck. Sacrifice it late to dig seven cards deep for a Dragon.
- **Patchwork Banner** — {3}. Name **Dragon**: Dragons you control get +1/+1, and it taps for any colour. Every Dragon token benefits too.
- **Dragon's Hoard** — {3}. Taps for any colour, gains a gold counter whenever a Dragon enters, and spends them to draw. Ramp and card advantage in one artifact.
- **Dragonstorm Globe** — {3}. Taps for any colour (helping RRR and BB costs), and every Dragon you control enters with an extra +1/+1 counter.
- **Hazoret's Monument** — {3} legendary. Red creature spells cost {1} less (Smaug is red as well as black) and every creature spell lets you rummage a card away.
- **Dawnsire, Sunstar Dreadnought** — {5} legendary Spacecraft. **Station** (tap another creature you control: put charge counters equal to its power on it; sorcery speed). At **10+** counters, whenever you attack it deals **100 damage** to up to one target creature or planeswalker. The trigger is "whenever **you** attack", so any attacking creature turns it on, and it can target your indestructible Smaug for **100 Treasures every combat**, which Exsanguinate or Shivan Devastator turns into a win. Station's cost is tapping another creature, not {T}, so even a creature that just arrived can pay it. Smaug is the best creature to station with: one tap is 8 counters (he then can't attack that turn, but another creature can). **Stop between 10 and 19 counters**: at 20 it becomes a creature that dies to removal and to your own Blasphemous Act. Hoarding Dragon can tutor it.
- **Requiem Monolith** — {2}{B}. `{T}` (sorcery speed): until end of turn, target creature gains "whenever this creature is dealt damage, you draw that many cards and lose that much life", and Monolith may deal it 1 damage. On Smaug that is a Treasure and a card every turn, and a burn spell aimed at him that turn draws as many cards as it makes Treasures. The draw is **mandatory** and counts any damage, combat included: **never activate it in a turn you'll fire Dawnsire (100 cards and 100 life) or Blasphemous Act (13 life)**, and count Gratuitous Violence's doubling before a big enter-trigger.
- **Soul-Guide Lantern** — {1}. Exiles a card from a graveyard when it enters; sacrifice it to exile **each opponent's graveyard** (the deck's answer to reanimator) or pay {1} and sacrifice it to draw a card.
- **Spicy Oatmeal Pizza** — {2}{R} Food. Enters dealing 4 damage to any target and 3 to you: aimed at Smaug it is **four Treasures for three mana**. The Food afterwards gains you 3 life, a catch-up for the pod.
- **Air Shoes (Swiftfoot Boots)** — {2} Equipment, equip {1}. Smaug already has haste and indestructible; **hexproof** is the missing piece against targeted removal. It does not stop sweepers or edicts. *(Five owned, three sleeved in reserved decks, so two copies are free.)*

- **Spit Flame** — {2}{R}. 4 damage to target creature, including your own Smaug (four Treasures). Whenever a Dragon you control enters, you may pay {R} to return it from the graveyard to your hand, so with 24 Dragons it is a recurring Treasure source and removal spell.
- **Stingerquill Charm** — {B}{R}. 3 damage to any target, first strike and deathtouch for a creature, or a hasty 2/2 Wizard Soldier. The deck's one gold instant.
- **Abrade** — {1}{R}. 3 damage to a creature, or destroy an artifact.
- **Chaos Warp** — {2}{R}. Shuffles away any permanent — the deck's answer to a resolved enchantment or a problem commander (tempo only, as they may recast).
- **Fiery Annihilation** — {2}{R}. 5 damage to a creature, exiling it if it would die. On Smaug it is **five Treasures for three mana**.
- **Go for the Throat** — {1}{B}. Cheap instant-speed kill, with the usual exception of artifact creatures.
- **Extended Absence** — {3}{B}. Exile target creature or planeswalker, deal 1 damage to each opponent and gain 1 life. The deck's hard answer to threats that damage and destroy effects can't handle.

- **Big Score** — {3}{R}. Discard a card, draw two, and make two Treasures.
- **Unexpected Windfall** — {2}{R}{R}. Discard a card, draw two, and make two Treasures, at instant speed so you can hold mana up through the pod's turns.
- **Deadly Dispute** — {1}{B}. Sacrifice an artifact or creature: draw two and make a Treasure. Sacrificing a spare Treasure makes it two cards for two mana, and at instant speed it can sacrifice a creature that a steal or removal spell is targeting. It does not stop an edict, which targets the player.

### Sorceries (3)
- **Blasphemous Act** — {8}{R}, {1} cheaper per creature on the battlefield (often {R} in a pod once seven or eight creatures are out). 13 damage to every creature: Smaug lives and banks thirteen Treasures. It also takes out your own Dragons, so cast it behind or after unloading your hand.
- **Feed the Swarm** — {1}{B}. Destroy target creature or **enchantment** an opponent controls; you lose life equal to its mana value. A third enchantment answer next to Withering Torment and Chaos Warp.
- **Exsanguinate** — {X}{B}{B}. Each opponent loses X and you gain that much. Treasures supply the black, and a hoard becomes a win with no combat at all.

## Key Synergies

- **Dragon cost reducers + Smaug**: Dragonspeaker Shaman ({2} less), Dragonlord's Servant, Nogi and Hazoret's Monument each knock mana off Smaug, who is red, black and a Dragon. With the first two he costs {2}{B}{R}; with all four he costs {B}{R}. They cut every other Dragon in the deck the same way, and Orb of Dragonkind pays for the rest.
- **Enter-triggers aimed at your own Smaug**: Scourge of Valkas and Dragon Tempest each deal damage equal to your Dragon count whenever a Dragon enters, and Warstorm Surge makes every creature that enters deal its power; Smaug is indestructible, so each trigger is a pile of Treasures. Smaug entering under Warstorm Surge hits himself for eight. Lathliss, Skyline Despot, Manaform Hellkite, Firdoch Core, The Elder Dragon War and Goldlust Triad's myriad copies all supply more entering Dragons.
- **Burn + Smaug**: Lightning Bolt, Stingerquill Charm, Spit Flame, Violent Echoes, Fiery Annihilation and Spicy Oatmeal Pizza put damage on Smaug on demand; Smaug the Magnificent, Drakuseth, Dragon Tempest, Warstorm Surge and The Elder Dragon War do it on attacks, entries and chapters, and Leyline Tyrant when it dies. Each is also removal or reach, so none is ever a dead card.
- **Dawnsire + Smaug**: Station it to 10 or more counters (tapping Smaug once gives 8), attack with anything, and its 100 damage lands on your indestructible commander for **100 Treasures** a combat. Exsanguinate or Shivan Devastator turns the hoard into the win. Stay at 19 counters or below so it isn't a creature, and Hoarding Dragon can tutor it.
- **Gratuitous Violence + creature-sourced damage**: every Dragon Tempest, Scourge of Valkas, Warstorm Surge, Drakuseth or Smaug the Magnificent trigger aimed at Smaug makes twice the Treasures, and the same triggers aimed at opponents become double reach.
- **Requiem Monolith + burn on Smaug**: activate it, then Bolt, Spit Flame or Pizza Smaug, and each point of damage is a Treasure *and* a card. Keep it away from Dawnsire and Blasphemous Act turns.
- **Parapet Thrasher + Smaug**: any Dragon that connects lets Thrasher deal 4 damage to **each other** opponent (8 across a three-opponent pod), destroy an artifact, or impulse-draw. Each mode works once per turn, so one connecting Dragon is enough.
- **Thundermane Dragon + a top-heavy creature suite**: 17 of your other creatures have power 4 or more, so Thundermane Dragon casts them off the top of the library with haste.
- **Treasures as card draw**: Deadly Dispute turns a spare Treasure into two cards and a fresh Treasure, Smaug, Wicked Worm draws whenever Treasure mana casts a spell, Requiem Monolith turns damage on Smaug into cards, and Big Score and Unexpected Windfall refill the hand while adding to the hoard. Breaching Dragonstorm is a free spell every time a Dragon brings it back.
- **Treasure hoard + Exsanguinate / Shivan Devastator**: when the hoard gets large, Exsanguinate is a table-wide drain and Shivan Devastator is a hasty finisher, both sized by however many Treasures you made.

## Short Mulligan and Play Notes

- **Early game (turns 1–4)**: Keep two- and three-landers with a rock or a cost reducer. Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Fire Diamond or Mind Stone, Sarkhan (a Treasure for revealing a Dragon), Dragonspeaker Shaman and Nogi are the plays that make a turn-four Dragon real. Ship one-landers — the curve is built around reaching five mana.
- **Mid game (turns 5–8)**: Land Smaug (or whichever Dragon the reducers made cheap) and attack. Each Dragon that enters with Dragon Tempest, Scourge of Valkas or Warstorm Surge out is a free Treasure ping at Smaug, and Parapet Thrasher turns every hit into reach across the pod. Play The Elder Dragon War from chapter I when the pod has tokens and small creatures, or read ahead to chapter III when your own early creatures matter more.
- **Late game**: Blasphemous Act is the reset; it also gives Smaug thirteen Treasures to rebuild with. Exsanguinate and Shivan Devastator convert a large hoard into a finish. Drakuseth, Skyline Despot and Tyrant's Familiar close games from the air.
- **Key interactions**:
  - Smaug's trigger is **noncombat** damage only; combat damage and blockers make no Treasures.
  - Swiftfoot Boots gives hexproof, which stops opponents' targeted removal but still lets your own burn and triggers target Smaug. It does not stop sweepers, edicts or -X/-X. Thunderbreak Regent punishes the targeting itself.
  - Warstorm Surge's trigger is the entering creature dealing damage, so a 5/5 Dragon aimed at Smaug is five Treasures; aim it at a creature or a face instead when you need removal or reach.
  - Dawnsire: keep it between 10 and 19 counters. Any attack triggers it, so Smaug can be the one tapped to station it on a turn something else attacks.
  - Requiem Monolith's draw is mandatory and counts combat damage too: never have it active on Smaug in a turn with Dawnsire, Blasphemous Act or a big trigger under Gratuitous Violence. One doubled Scourge of Valkas trigger with six Dragons out is 12 cards and 12 life.
  - Indestructible does not save Smaug from -X/-X, exile or sacrifice effects; Toxic Deluge-style spells still answer him. Haven of the Spirit Dragon is the recursion for the *other* Dragons.
  - Blasphemous Act and The Elder Dragon War's chapter I hit your own creatures as well. Plan around Atsushi, Immersturm Predator and Hoarding Dragon, which pay you back or survive, and hold Deadly Dispute to cash in a creature that is about to die.

## Why These Choices (Summary)

- **Tribal first, Treasure second**: with 24 Dragons, cost reducers and Dragon-only mana rocks do real work, and enter-triggers (Dragon Tempest, Scourge of Valkas, Warstorm Surge, Lathliss) turn every Dragon into value.
- **Ramp and fixing**: 9 mana rocks (Dragonstorm Globe and the Dragon-typed Firdoch Core among them), four cost reducers (Dragonlord's Servant, Dragonspeaker Shaman, Nogi, Hazoret's Monument) and a Treasure-making two-drop in Sarkhan sit on top of 34 lands. Red is the main colour (about 68 red pips to 13 black, commander included); black is limited to Smaug, Smaug, Wicked Worm, Immersturm Predator, Requiem Monolith and a few spells, and Treasures, Dragonstorm Globe and Haven of the Spirit Dragon help fix it.
- **Pod-ready interaction**: two sweepers that pay Smaug Treasures (Blasphemous Act and The Elder Dragon War), hard exile (Extended Absence), an edict (Break Under Pressure), graveyard hate (Soul-Guide Lantern), and removal that also hits artifacts and enchantments (Abrade, Chaos Warp, Withering Torment, Feed the Swarm, Steel Hellkite, Parapet Thrasher).
- **Group win conditions**: Parapet Thrasher, Drakuseth, Firespitter Whelp, Goldlust Triad's myriad, Exsanguinate and Dragonhawk all scale with the number of opponents, and the monarch engine of Skyline Despot keeps the cards coming.
- **Notable omissions**: planeswalkers (none in the collection fit Black/Red), counterspells, mass artifact/enchantment removal and a second hexproof piece — see below.

## Analysis

**Strengths**:
- A commander immune to damage-based wipes, and a deck that does not need him: almost any Dragon is a threat, so removal on Smaug costs the pod a card but not you the game.
- Heavy cost reduction and ramp make the 4–7 drops that dominate the list castable a turn or two early.
- Several independent win conditions (flying Dragons, spread damage, Exsanguinate off the hoard) plus a monarch engine for the long game.
- Resilient to sweepers: Atsushi, Immersturm Predator, Hoarding Dragon and Blasphemous Act's Treasures all pay out when the board is cleared.

**Weaknesses/Missing Staples**:
- **Mana demands**: RR is everywhere and RRR shows up in Scourge of Valkas, Drakuseth and Gratuitous Violence, with BB in Exsanguinate. Counting fetchlands, roughly 24 red and 17 black sources, plus Treasures, Firdoch Core and Dragonstorm Globe, are enough but not generous. Losing Multiverse Reforged's untapped duals hurt: five lands now always enter tapped (Path of Ancestry, Temple of Malice, Bloodfell Caves, Rakdos Guildgate, Stingerquill Annex).
- **Top-end weight**: three seven-drops (Tyrant's Familiar, Drakuseth, Skyline Despot) plus Blasphemous Act, and four six-drops. The reducers fix this only when you draw them.
- **Protection is thin**: Swiftfoot Boots and Thunderbreak Regent are all there is. Exile, edicts and -X/-X still answer Smaug. (Lightning Greaves is owned but was rejected: shroud would stop your own burn from targeting Smaug.)
- **Enchantment/artifact removal is spot-only**: Abrade, Chaos Warp, Withering Torment, Feed the Swarm and Parapet Thrasher answer one permanent at a time; Steel Hellkite is the only one that scales. Nevinyrral's Disk is the only mass answer in the pool, and it kills your Dragons too.
- **Graveyard hate and counters**: Soul-Guide Lantern is the only graveyard answer (Bojuka Bog is owned if the pod needs more), there are no counters, and there is little lifegain beyond Spicy Oatmeal Pizza and Exsanguinate.
