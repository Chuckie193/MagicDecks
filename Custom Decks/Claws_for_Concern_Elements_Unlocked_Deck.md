# Claws for Concern: Elements Unlocked — Gruul Werewolf Tribal Commander

Commander: Tovolar, Dire Overlord // Tovolar, the Midnight Scourge (2RG, Red/Green)

## Overview
- **Strategy**: The same Tovolar werewolf tribal aggro plan as [Claws for Concern](Claws_for_Concern_Deck.md), built on a card pool where **Dance of the Elements has been dismantled**. Cheap werewolves flood the board, Tovolar's upkeep trigger flips the whole team to night, Cult of the Waxing Moon mints a Wolf per flip, and Garruk's Uprising gives the pack trample while drawing a card for every 4-power creature that lands. Zopandrel and Unnatural Growth double the board at every combat.
- **Intended for**: Both — a low curve (average CMC ~3.13, 23 cards at 1–2 mana) and cheap targeted removal for 1v1, while the doubling pair, Endurance's flash blocker and Fury's free removal all scale into a pod.
- **Card pool**: **One reserved deck unlocked — Dance of the Elements.** Full Deployment and Squirreled Away remain assembled and off-limits. This is a *hypothetical* build: it is only legal to sleeve if you actually take Dance of the Elements apart.
- **This is a variant, not a replacement.** See "How This Differs from Claws for Concern" below.

## Lore

The treeline stops pretending to be trees. Something ancient exhales, and the fire that answers has a heartbeat. Tovolar doesn't command it — he simply howls first, and lets the woods agree. *Nature, red in tooth and kindling.*

## Decklist (100 cards)

### Commander (1)
- **Tovolar, Dire Overlord // Tovolar, the Midnight Scourge** — Draws a card whenever a Werewolf or Wolf you control deals combat damage to a player. At your upkeep with three or more Wolves/Werewolves it becomes night (flipping every daybound werewolf automatically) **and then** transforms any number of your old-border Human Werewolves. Night side is a 4/4 with `{X}{R}{G}: target Wolf or Werewolf gets +X/+0 and trample`.

### Creatures (42)
- **Ascendant Packleader** — A 1-drop Wolf that grows a +1/+1 counter every time you cast a 4-plus mana value spell; the deck has twenty of them.
- **Beorn the Fierce** — A 6/6 trampler that turns one creature into a Bear every combat, permanently, handing it a trample counter and Beorn's own "other Bears get +2/+2." At three Bears he draws **two cards a turn** — and Realmwalker is a changeling, therefore already a Bear, so that can be his *first* combat.
- **Breakneck Rider // Neck Breaker** — Old-border werewolf; night side gives attacking creatures +1/+0 and trample.
- **Burly Breaker // Dire-Strain Demolisher** — A 6/5 ward {1} that becomes an 8/7 with ward {3} at night. The biggest body, and the best Selvala target.
- **Child of the Pack // Savage Packmate** — Daybound werewolf that can pay {2}{R}{G} for a 2/2 Wolf; night side is a trampler that anthems the team +1/+0.
- **Cult of the Waxing Moon** — A 2/2 Wolf whenever a permanent you control transforms into a non-Human creature. Tovolar's upkeep flips *both* werewolf groups at once, so a developed board mints four or five Wolves off one trigger — and it repeats every time an opponent flips you back to day.
- **Daybreak Ranger // Nightfall Predator** — Old-border werewolf; pings fliers by day, repeatable fight machine by night.
- **Duskwatch Recruiter // Krallenhorde Howler** — Digs three deep for a creature by day; creature spells cost {1} less at night.
- **Endurance** *(new)* — `{1}{G}{G}` **flash** 3/4 **reach**, or **free** by exiling a green card from your hand. A surprise blocker that ambushes an attacker or eats a flier, plus graveyard hate on entry. It is not interaction in the counterspell sense — it answers nothing on the stack — but a free flash body in a deck that previously had none changes how you hold up mana.
- **Fangblade Brigand // Fangblade Eviscerator** — Daybound firebreathing/first-strike werewolf that can pump the whole team once it flips.
- **Fury** *(new)* — `{3}{R}{R}` double striker, or **free** by exiling a red card. On entry it deals **4 damage divided** among any number of creatures and planeswalkers — a one-sided mini-sweeper against tokens and mana dorks, or a clean kill on a blocker. The deck's most efficient piece of interaction. Note the evoke fodder is your own red werewolves, so "free" has a real cost.
- **Geier Reach Bandit // Vildin-Pack Alpha** — Hasty by day. Night side transforms Werewolves that enter, but only the **old-border** ones, since daybound permanents can't be transformed by other effects. It chains Cult triggers; it does **not** trigger Silverfur Partisan (a triggered ability is not a spell).
- **Greenwarden of Murasa** *(new)* — 5/4 that returns a card from your graveyard to your **hand** on entry **and again when it dies**. Two rebuys from one card, and to hand rather than the top of your library — the deck's real answer to a board wipe.
- **Hound Tamer // Untamed Pup** — Trampler with a {3}{G} counter sink; night side gives all Wolves and Werewolves trample.
- **Howlpack Piper // Wildsong Howler** — Uncounterable; puts a creature from your **hand** onto the battlefield and untaps if it's a Wolf or Werewolf. It finds nothing — it cheats mana.
- **Huntmaster of the Fells // Ravager of the Fells** — Wolf and 2 life on every flip to day, burn on every flip to night. Immerwolf shuts the flip-back half off permanently.
- **Ill-Tempered Loner // Howlpack Avenger** — Daybound damage-reflector; night side converts damage your permanents take into direct damage anywhere.
- **Immerwolf** — Anthem for Wolves/Werewolves (+1/+1, intimidate) that locks non-Human Werewolves from transforming back to day. Read the trade: it keeps the bigger stats but switches off Huntmaster's loop and caps Cult at one Wolf per werewolf.
- **Instigator Gang // Wildblood Pack** — +1/+0 to attackers by day, +3/+0 and trample by night.
- **Kessig Naturalist // Lord of the Ulvenwald** — Adds a mana when it attacks by day; a Wolf/Werewolf lord at night.
- **Kruin Outlaw // Terror of Kruin Pass** — First strike by day; double strike at night, and **Werewolves** gain menace (Wolf tokens do not).
- **Llanowar Elves** — Turn-one acceleration into a turn-two three-drop, and this deck has **twenty** three-drops.
- **Mayor of Avabruck // Howlpack Alpha** — Anthem for Humans by day (which includes Realmwalker), anthem plus an end-step Wolf by night.
- **Migloz, Maze Crusher** — Three-mana 4/4 with five oil counters spending on vigilance/menace, +2/+2, or artifact/enchantment removal.
- **Nightpack Ambusher** — Flash lord making a 2/2 Wolf at every end step you didn't cast a spell — also the only *free* route to night without Tovolar.
- **Outland Liberator // Frenzied Trapbreaker** — Cheap artifact/enchantment removal on a body.
- **Packsong Pup** — A two-mana Wolf that grows a +1/+1 counter at every combat for free with no mana investment, feeds Tovolar's threshold, collects every anthem, and turns on Wargling's ferocious and Werewolf Pack Leader's pack tactics.
- **Realmwalker** *(new)* — Changeling, so it *is* a Wolf and a Werewolf: it fills a body for Tovolar's threshold, takes Immerwolf's anthem, counts for Runebound Wolf, gets Full Moon's Rise, collects Mayor's Human anthem, and is a **Bear** for Beorn. On top of all that, name Werewolf and cast creatures straight off the top of your library. Best-in-slot for this shell.
- **Reckless Stormseeker // Storm-Charged Slasher** — +1/+0 and haste each combat by day, +2/+0 and trample by night.
- **Runebound Wolf** — `{3}{R}, {T}`: damage equal to your Wolf/Werewolf count **to an opponent**. Four mana a shot is steep, but it is the deck's only way to deal damage without attacking, and it's a two-mana Wolf besides.
- **Sage of Ancient Lore // Werewolf of Ancient Hunger** — Draws on entry and is as big as your hand; night side is a vigilant trampler as big as **the total cards in all players' hands**, which in a pod is enormous.
- **Scorned Villager // Moonscarred Werewolf** — Mana dork by day, two-mana vigilant dork by night.
- **Selvala, Heart of the Wilds** *(new)* — `{G}, {T}: Add X mana in any combination of colours, where X is the greatest power among creatures you control.` With Burly Breaker (6/5, 8/7 at night) or Tovolar's Huntmaster (6/6) that is six to eight mana off one green. She is the card that lets you cast Unnatural Growth and Greenwarden in the same turn. **Her draw trigger is symmetric** — an opponent playing a bigger creature than yours draws off her too, so she is a small liability against a pod with larger bodies.
- **Silverfur Partisan** — A 2/2 trampler (not a lord — no anthem clause) that mints a 2/2 Wolf whenever a Wolf/Werewolf you control becomes the target of an **instant or sorcery spell**: here that's four cards — Bite Down, Snakeskin Veil, Unnatural Moonrise and Wolf Strike.
- **Tovolar's Huntmaster // Tovolar's Packleader** — Two Wolves on entry; at night two more on every attack, and `{2}{G}{G}` makes another Wolf/Werewolf **fight** (removal, not a pump).
- **Village Messenger // Moonrise Intruder** — Hasty 1-drop that becomes a menacing 2/2 at night.
- **Volatile Arsonist // Dire-Strain Anarchist** — Menace/haste pinger hitting a creature, a player and a planeswalker on attack.
- **Wargling** — Two-mana 2/2 **Wolf** granting **all your creatures trample** whenever it attacks while you *control* a 4-power creature — that creature needn't attack. Now partly redundant with Garruk's Uprising, but it's still a cheap Wolf and the trample stacks in the turn Garruk's is answered.
- **Weaver of Blossoms // Blossom-Clad Werewolf** — Any-colour dork by day; a 3/4 that taps for two by night.
- **Werewolf Pack Leader** — Draws whenever you attack with 6-plus total power; can become a 5/3 non-Human trampler. Single-faced, so it never transforms.
- **Wolfkin Outcast // Wedding Crasher** — Costs {2} less with a Wolf/Werewolf out; night side draws whenever any Wolf or Werewolf you control dies.
- **Zopandrel, Hunger Dominus** — A 4/6 **reach** body that doubles the power and toughness of each creature you control, at the beginning of *every* combat phase in the turn cycle — opponents' included, so your board is doubled on defence too.

### Enchantments (5)
- **Full Moon's Rise** — **Werewolf** creatures get +1/+0 and trample, and it sacrifices to regenerate all **Werewolves**. It does nothing for your Wolf *tokens* or the **seven** non-Werewolf Wolves (Ascendant Packleader, Immerwolf, Nightpack Ambusher, Packsong Pup, Runebound Wolf, Silverfur Partisan, Wargling).
- **Garruk's Uprising** *(new)* — **The single best card this unlock provides.** Draws on entry if you have a 4-power creature, gives **every** creature you control trample permanently, and **draws a card whenever a creature with power 4 or greater enters**. This deck's night-side werewolves are 4/4 through 8/7, Tovolar's Huntmaster is a 6/6 that brings two more bodies, and Cult mints Wolves off every flip. It is a repeatable draw engine and a team anthem for three mana.
- **Howlpack Resurgence** — Flash anthem: Wolves *and* Werewolves get +1/+1 and trample — unlike Full Moon's Rise it covers the tokens.
- **Ranger Class** — Makes a Wolf on entry, a +1/+1 counter on an attacker at Level 2, then casts creatures off the top at Level 3.
- **Unnatural Growth** — `{1}{G}{G}{G}{G}`: doubles the power and toughness of each creature you control at the beginning of each combat. The enchantment half of the win condition — it survives the wraths that kill Zopandrel, and if both resolve the doublings stack.

### Artifacts & Mana (5)
- **Arcane Signet** — Untapped two-mana fixing.
- **Chromatic Lantern** *(new)* — **The mana fix this deck has always needed.** "Lands you control have `{T}`: Add one mana of any color" turns all 13 Mountains — and Kessig Wolf Run, and an unhelpful Exotic Orchard — into green sources, taking the deck from 21 green lands to **36**. It is what makes `{G}{G}{G}{G}` on Unnatural Growth genuinely castable. The risk to accept: the deck's colour fixing now leans on an artifact, so artifact removal reverts you to 21 sources.
- **Power Sneakers (Lightning Greaves)** — Haste and shroud for {2} with a **{0} equip**. Shroud stops your own targeting too — roughly thirteen of the deck's effects can't touch the equipped creature, and equip is sorcery-speed. Its job is carrying Tovolar safely to your next upkeep, not enabling him.
- **Sol Ring** — The best ramp piece in Commander.
- **The Celestus** — Ramps and fixes, and can push the day/night cycle — though `{3}` plus a tap is a four-mana turn producing no net mana, so it's a backup night-forcer.

### Instants (8)
- **Abrade** — 3 damage to a creature, or destroy an artifact.
- **Bite Down** — Fight spell using your creature's power; hits planeswalkers, and targets your own Wolf for a Silverfur token.
- **Broken Wings** — Destroys an artifact, an enchantment, **or a creature with flying**.
- **Chaos Warp** — Catch-all for any permanent.
- **Moonlight Hunt** — Every Wolf/Werewolf damages a single target. Its only target is a creature you *don't* control, so it does **not** trigger Silverfur Partisan.
- **Moonmist** — A fog for everything that isn't a Wolf or Werewolf, and it transforms all Humans. **Daybound permanents can't be transformed by other effects**, so it flips only the **11 old-border** werewolves, not the 14 daybound ones. Cast it as a fog first and a flip effect second.
- **Snakeskin Veil** — Protects a threat, leaves a permanent +1/+1 counter, and mints a Silverfur Wolf.
- **Wolf Strike** — Pumps (more at night) then fights — and targets your own Wolf for Silverfur.

### Sorceries (1)
- **Unnatural Moonrise** — Forces night and turns a creature into a trampling card-draw threat; target your own Wolf and it mints a Silverfur token. Flashback makes it a two-for-one, and it is the deck's only proactive night-forcer besides Tovolar.

### Planeswalkers (2)
- **Arlinn Kord // Arlinn, Embraced by the Moon** — Haste and pump or a 2/2 Wolf by day; anthem, 3 damage and a permanent haste-and-ping emblem by night.
- **Arlinn, the Pack's Hope // Arlinn, the Moon's Fury** — Day: +1 gives creatures flash and an extra +1/+1 counter as they enter; −3 makes two Wolves. Night: `+2: Add {R}{G}`, and `0:` turns her into a 5/5 **Werewolf** with trample, indestructible and haste — which counts toward Tovolar's threshold.

### Lands (36)
*Deliberately identical to Claws for Concern.* Every land Dance of the Elements unlocks is restricted, tapped, or both — Ancient Ziggurat has no `{C}` fallback at all and is a blank for this deck's 22 noncreature spells; Secluded Courtyard and Unclaimed Territory produce colourless or creature-type-restricted colour, so neither can help cast Unnatural Growth. Chromatic Lantern fixes the mana far better than any of them.

- **Bristling Backwoods** — Tapped Gruul dual that pings an opponent on entry. Still the weakest land here.
- **Command Tower** — Red or Green, no downside. *(Second copy.)*
- **Exotic Orchard** — Untapped, and its coloured mana is **unrestricted** — which is why it stays over any of the unlocked tribal lands. Can whiff against mono-blue or mono-white.
- **Forest** (x13) — Green basics.
- **Gongaga, Reactor Town** — Tapped Gruul dual.
- **Kessig Wolf Run** — Untapped finisher land; pump and trample scaling with mana.
- **Mountain** (x13) — Red basics.
- **Path of Ancestry** — Gruul dual with a scry on a shared-type creature. *(Second copy.)*
- **Raging Ravine** — Tapped dual that becomes a self-growing 3/3 — a threat that dodges sorcery-speed wraths. *(Second copy.)*
- **Rugged Highlands** — Tapped Gruul dual with 1 life.
- **Sheltered Thicket** — Tapped Gruul dual with cycling.
- **Temple of Abandon** — Tapped Gruul dual with a scry.

*10 nonbasics, 7 of which enter tapped. Command Tower, Exotic Orchard and Kessig Wolf Run are the untapped three — and Chromatic Lantern makes the distinction largely moot for colour.*

## Key Synergies

- **Garruk's Uprising + your 4-power creatures**: it draws whenever a creature with power 4 or greater **enters** — and the deck casts nine of them: Burly Breaker, Tovolar's Huntmaster, Beorn the Fierce, Zopandrel, Migloz, Nightpack Ambusher, Volatile Arsonist, Wolfkin Outcast and Greenwarden. **Important limit: a werewolf *transforming* is not entering**, so flipping Burly Breaker to an 8/7 draws you nothing — a transforming permanent never changes zones and never triggers enter-the-battlefield abilities. Wolf tokens are 2/2 and only cross the four-power line once two or more anthems are out. What Garruk's reliably gives you is permanent team-wide trample plus a card off most of your top end, which alongside Tovolar's combat-damage draw is two engines off one board.
- **Selvala + Burly Breaker / Tovolar's Huntmaster**: Selvala taps for X = greatest power among your creatures. A night-side Burly Breaker is 8/7, so that's eight mana off a single green — enough to cast Unnatural Growth and hold up Endurance in the same turn.
- **Realmwalker is a Wolf, a Werewolf, a Human and a Bear**: changeling means it fills Tovolar's threshold, takes Immerwolf's and Mayor's anthems, counts for Runebound Wolf and Full Moon's Rise, casts werewolves off the top, *and* turns Beorn's Bear count on a combat early.
- **Fury + evoke**: exiling a red card casts Fury for no mana, clearing two blockers or a key creature before you attack. **Note Fury has no flash** — unlike Endurance it is a sorcery-speed creature spell, so this is a main-phase play, not a combat trick. The cost is real in cards: that red card is usually a werewolf. It is still the deck's only removal that can cost nothing.
- **Zopandrel + Unnatural Growth**: both double at the beginning of each combat and the effects compound, so a 2/2 Wolf attacks as an 8/8, or **16/16** if it's already carrying Immerwolf and Nightpack Ambusher's anthems. They fail to different answers — creature removal versus enchantment removal — which is why both are here.
- **Doubling timing**: both trigger at the **beginning of combat**, *before* attackers are declared, and each locks in X as the creature's power when it resolves (CR 701.9b). Anything applied afterwards is added at face value — but Kessig Wolf Run and Tovolar's `{X}{R}{G}` are instant-speed, so use them **while the doubling trigger is still on the stack** and they get doubled too.
- **Immerwolf vs. Cult of the Waxing Moon**: two competing answers to being flipped back to day. Immerwolf **refuses** the flip; Cult **taxes** it. Immerwolf caps Cult at one trigger per werewolf and switches off Huntmaster's loop, so which one you're playing is the biggest piloting decision in the deck.

## How This Differs from Claws for Concern

**Seven cards change. Nothing else.** The land base, the werewolf core, both P/T doublers and the whole removal suite are untouched.

| Out | In | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Terrasymbiosis | **Garruk's Uprising** | Terrasymbiosis was standing in for exactly this card. Garruk's draws off 4-power creatures entering *and* gives the whole team trample — two roles for the same three mana. |
| Barkform Harvester | **Realmwalker** | Both are changelings, so tribal count is unchanged. Realmwalker adds cast-off-the-top card flow that Barkform's bottom-of-library ability never provided. |
| Hunter's Talent | **Selvala, Heart of the Wilds** | Garruk's Uprising blanks Hunter's Talent — Level 2's trample is now permanent and team-wide, and Level 3's 4-power draw is duplicated. Selvala turns a big werewolf into six-plus mana. |
| Warg Tactics | **Endurance** | A free flash 3/4 reach blocker with graveyard hate is worth more than a modal trick, and reach coverage stays level. |
| Wilderland Scrounger | **Fury** | Free removal in a deck whose interaction was all one-for-one and sorcery-speed. Costs a Wolf body. |
| Elvish Regrower | **Greenwarden of Murasa** | Same role, twice the triggers — entry *and* death, both returning to hand. |
| Mind Stone | **Chromatic Lantern** | Mind Stone was the only mana source producing neither red nor green. The Lantern takes green sources from 21 to 36. |

**What actually gets better**
- **Card advantage.** Garruk's Uprising is a repeatable draw engine on a board full of 4-power creatures; Realmwalker casts off the top; Greenwarden rebuys twice.
- **Mana.** Chromatic Lantern makes `{G}{G}{G}{G}` castable for the first time and turns 13 Mountains into green sources.
- **Interaction.** Fury is the deck's first genuinely efficient removal spell, and it can cost zero *mana* — though it always costs a card.
- **Trample.** Permanent and team-wide from Garruk's Uprising rather than conditional on Wargling attacking.

**What gets worse**
- **Tribal density drops 35 → 34** (Werewolves 26, Wolves 7, changeling 1). Wilderland Scrounger's Wolf body is the loss; the Barkform → Realmwalker swap is neutral.
- **Curve rises 3.05 → 3.13**, and three-drops go from 17 to 20.
- **Colour fixing now depends on an artifact.** Chromatic Lantern getting destroyed is a real setback in a way Mind Stone never was.

**What does not change at all** — and this is the important one. **No unlock in the collection contains a single night-forcer.** The deck's defining weakness, stated plainly in Claws for Concern, is that the night plan is almost entirely commander-dependent: without Tovolar you are a pile of below-rate day-side 2/2s and 3/3s. Dance of the Elements does nothing about that. **This deck is more powerful than Claws for Concern; it is not more robust in the way that actually defines it.**

## Short Mulligan and Play Notes

- **Mulligan to deploy and protect Tovolar, not to find him.** He's always in the command zone. You want the mana to cast him on turn 3–4, two or three cheap bodies for his threshold, and ideally Power Sneakers.
- **Early (1–3)**: Llanowar Elves or Sol Ring into a three-drop — the deck now has twenty. Garruk's Uprising on turn three is the best possible start after Tovolar himself.
- **Mid (4–6)**: Land Tovolar with three bodies already out. Selvala turns a big werewolf into a mana engine. Hold Endurance up rather than tapping out when you can.
- **Late (7+)**: Either doubler ends the game on a wide board. Greenwarden rebuys whatever was answered. Kessig Wolf Run closes what combat can't.
- **Day/night, stated correctly**: it becomes night when a player casts **zero** spells during their own turn; casting one changes nothing; two or more flips it back to day. **Tovolar's upkeep trigger is your real night button.**
- **Key interactions**: Daybound permanents can't be transformed by anything except the day/night cycle, so Moonmist and Vildin-Pack Alpha only flip the 11 old-border werewolves. Power Sneakers' shroud blocks your own effects — equip Tovolar to protect him, not to enable him. Fury's evoke cost is a red card from hand: check whether that werewolf is worth more than the removal before you exile it.

## Analysis

**Strengths**
- Two independent draw engines (Garruk's Uprising and Tovolar) plus Realmwalker, Duskwatch Recruiter, Werewolf Pack Leader and Beorn
- Two P/T doublers that fail to different answers, and stack multiplicatively
- Team trample is permanent and unconditional rather than sitting on an attacking 2/2
- Colour fixing genuinely solved for the first time — 36 effective green sources with the Lantern out
- Free spells: Endurance and Fury both cost zero when you need them to

**Weaknesses**
- **The night plan is still commander-dependent.** No unlock in the collection contains a night-forcer. This is unchanged and remains the deck's defining fragility.
- **Board wipes**: still none worth running — you are always the widest board.
- **Colour fixing leans on an artifact.** Losing Chromatic Lantern reverts `{G}{G}{G}{G}` to 21 sources.
- **Selvala's draw trigger is symmetric** and helps a pod opponent with bigger creatures.
- **Curve is up** to 3.13, with twenty three-drops competing for the same turn.
- Tribal density is down one body versus Claws for Concern.

**Power level**: **Bracket 3, low end.** Endurance, Fury, Selvala and Garruk's Uprising are genuine bracket-3 cards, but there are still no tutors, no fast mana beyond Sol Ring, no combo, and the clock is still "attack with wolves."

## Next Steps (Optional Suggestions)

- **This deck is only legal if Dance of the Elements is actually dismantled.** If it stays assembled, play Claws for Concern instead — this file is the argument for taking it apart, not a deck you can sleeve today.
- Cavalier of Thorns and Return of the Wildspeaker are both unlocked and both were *considered and rejected* — Cavalier because its recursion goes to the top of your library rather than hand, and Return of the Wildspeaker because "non-Human creatures" excludes 28 of this deck's 42 creatures until they flip at night.
- Kindred Summons (unlocked, `{5}{G}{G}` instant) puts X werewolves onto the battlefield at instant speed. Skipped as win-more with two doublers already, but it is the most powerful card left on the table.
- Bane of Progress is unlocked and should **not** be played — it would destroy your own Unnatural Growth, Garruk's Uprising, Ranger Class, Full Moon's Rise, Howlpack Resurgence, Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Chromatic Lantern and Power Sneakers.
- Cultivate and Kodama's Reach are both available and both were skipped — six accelerants plus 36 lands is already slightly over-built for a 3.13 curve.
