# Smaug the Impenetrable — Collection Review (for tomorrow)

Review of every Black, Red and colourless card in the collection against the current `Smaug_the_Impenetrable_Deck.md`. **The deck has not been changed.** Pool: *Avoid reserved decks* (Full Deployment, Claws for Concern, Table Manners, Dance of the Elements), subtracting copies, not names.

- **Cards reviewed**: 821 entries in the collection within Smaug's colour identity (Black/Red/colourless, 27 of them tokens and game accessories), plus the two basics. 80 are already in the deck, leaving 741 under review.
- **Tier A** (10) are the ones I would most like to test. **Tier B** (22) are worth a second look. **Tier C** (46) were considered individually and probably should stay out. **Reserved** cards are great but sleeved elsewhere. Everything else is bulk-reviewed and listed by reason at the end, so every card appears exactly once.
- Already agreed in the three-way review and **not yet applied**: Tweeze → Spit Flame, Chain Reaction → Extended Absence, Stingerquill Annex → Castle Locthwain, plus the wording fixes. Dawnsire was not in that list and is the main new question.

## Tier C — considered, probably not

- **Nogi, Draco-Zealot** ({1}{R}{R}) — Fifth Dragon cost reducer; its 5/5 mode needs three Dragons attacking. Redundant with Servant, Shaman, Horn and Monument.
- **Utvara Hellkite** ({6}{R}{R}) — Eight mana for a token every Dragon attack. Strong when it lands, but the top end is already four seven-drops.
- **Dragon Mage** ({5}{R}{R}) — Group wheel for politics; hands every opponent seven fresh cards.
- **Purifying Dragon** ({3}{R}{R}) — Pings creatures an opponent controls on attack only; it cannot hit your own Smaug.
- **Kargan Dragonrider** ({1}{R}) — 2/2 that flies with a Dragon in play. A weak two-drop here.
- **Skeleton Archer** ({3}{B}) — {3}{B} 3/3 that pings any target when it enters (1 Treasure on Smaug).
- **Fanatical Firebrand** ({R}) — Sacrifices for 1 damage to any target (1 Treasure). Too small.
- **Kindlespark Duo** ({2}{R}) — Pings one opponent each turn and untaps on noncreature spells; modest.
- **Koth, the Geomancer** ({2}{R}) — Landfall pings each opponent for 1, red mana off Mountains. Slow in a deck with few ramp lands.
- **Ornithopter of Paradise** ({2}) — {2} flying mana fixer; fine, but the deck already has plenty of mana.
- **Knuckles the Echidna** ({2}{R}{R}) — Double strike haste, Treasure on hits; a ground body in a deck of fliers.
- **Plargg and Nassari** ({3}{R}{R}) — Free spells at upkeep for the whole table; powerful but hands the pod free cards, and it is a ground 5/4.
- **Volcanic Torrent** ({4}{R}) — Cascade plus X damage to each opposing creature and planeswalker, X being spells cast this turn. Two spells in a turn is usually 2 damage.
- **Fulminous Forte** ({2}{R}) — Instant: 1 damage to each opposing creature or 5 damage to one. Both modes are weaker than what is already in.
- **Heated Argument** ({4}{R}) — {4}{R} for 6 damage plus 2 to the controller; too slow for what it does.
- **Conduct Electricity** ({4}{R}) — {4}{R} for 6 damage to a creature; slower than Violent Echoes.
- **Reckless Handling** ({1}{R}) — Tutors an artifact (Dawnsire) but discards randomly and costs a card. Only if Dawnsire goes in.
- **Pyromancer's Goggles** ({5}) — Copies red instants and sorceries; copying Blasphemous Act is huge but the deck has few red noncreature payoffs.
- **Lux Cannon** ({4}) — Repeatable destroy-anything but slow; Archive Arbiter and Extended Absence do the job faster.
- **Pinnacle Kill-Ship** ({7}) — {7} Spacecraft: 10 damage on entering, and a second Station card. Too expensive.
- **Meteor Golem** ({7}) — {7} destroy target nonland permanent. Too slow.
- **Skysovereign, Consul Flagship** ({5}) — Needs Crew 3; deals 3 to creatures only when it attacks.
- **Grave Titan** ({4}{B}{B}) — Excellent {4}{B}{B} card, but BB at six mana off roughly 18 black sources, and it is not a Dragon.
- **Archon of Cruelty** ({6}{B}{B}) — Eight-mana bomb in black; the deck has enough top-end.
- **Impact Tremors** ({1}{R}) — Pings the table whenever a creature enters; fine in a creature deck but low-impact here.
- **Count on Luck** ({R}{R}{R}) — RRR draw engine on turn three is awkward; Outpost Siege does the job.
- **Anchovy & Banana Pizza** ({2}{B}{B}) — {2}{B}{B} Food that destroys a creature when it enters and gains 3 life later. Fine, but BB.
- **Marchesa's Decree** ({3}{B}) — Monarch for {3}{B}; Skyline Despot already covers the monarch.
- **Gratuitous Violence** ({2}{R}{R}{R}) — {2}{R}{R}{R} doubles damage from creatures; the deck's damage mostly isn't creature-sourced.
- **Ajani's Anguish** ({X}{R}) — X damage to any target as a sorcery-speed mana sink; weaker than Shivan Devastator.
- **Hit the Mother Lode** ({4}{R}{R}{R}) — {4}{R}{R}{R} discover 10 plus Treasures; too expensive and RRR.
- **Mana Geyser** ({3}{R}{R}) — Only strong if opponents tap lands on their own turn.
- **Memorial to Folly** (—) — Tapped black land that can return a Dragon to hand; Haven of the Spirit Dragon already does it better.
- **Bojuka Bog** (—) — Graveyard hate on a land; worth swapping in only against a graveyard pod.
- **Rogue's Passage** (—) — Colourless utility land; Smaug already flies and the deck needs coloured sources.
- **Reflecting Pool** (—) — Conditional fixing that does little on its own early.
- **Bonders' Enclave** (—) — Colourless land that draws with a power-4 creature; the mana reviewer liked it, the rules reviewer and I did not.
- **Uncharted Haven** (—) — Tapped any-colour land. Ten copies owned, but slow.
- **Nevinyrral's Disk** ({4}) — Kills your own Dragons.
- **Lightning Greaves** ({2}) — Shroud would stop your own damage targeting Smaug. Swiftfoot Boots is correct.
- **Commander's Sphere** ({3}) — {3} rock; the deck has better.
- **Hedron Archive** ({4}) — {4} rock; slow.
- **Gilded Lotus** ({5}) — {5} rock; slow.
- **Fellwar Stone** ({2}) — Works, but the deck already has enough two-mana rocks.
- **Dragon Fodder** ({1}{R}) — Two 1/1 Goblins; no support for them.
- **Fuel the Flames** ({2}{R}) — 2 damage to every creature hits your Dragons too (it does give Smaug 2 Treasures).
