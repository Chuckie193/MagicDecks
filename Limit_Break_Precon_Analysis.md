# Limit Break: Omnislash — Naya Equipment Commander (Precon Upgrade)

Commander: Cloud, Ex-SOLDIER ({2}{R}{G}{W}, Red/Green/White)

## Overview

- **Strategy**: Cloud draws **a card for each equipped attacking creature**, so this deck wins by *spreading* Equipment across a wide board, not by stacking it on one creature. The upgrade imports the free-attach package the precon was missing — Hammer of Nazahn, Sigarda's Aid, Forge Anew, Bruenor Battlehammer, Brass Squire and Nahiri, the Lithomancer — so that every Equipment that hits the battlefield lands on a *different* body for zero mana. Six or seven equipped attackers is six or seven cards a turn. The secondary axis is the precon's own **power-7 check**: Cloud makes two Treasures at 7 power, Tifa untaps the team and grants an extra combat, Vincent hits every opponent, and Bugenhagen draws — all reachable in one step via Colossus Hammer or Wrecking Ball Arm.
- **Intended for**: Both. The curve is low (avg CMC 2.91, 29 cards at 1–2 mana) with eight pieces of cheap interaction for 1v1, while Barret Wallace, Moraug's extra combats and Bronze Guardian's board-wide ward scale into a pod.
- **Card pool**: **The entire Magic card pool** — this is a shopping-list upgrade, not a collection build. 33 cards swapped, ~**€114** for the 28 purchased non-basic cards (whole-deck non-basic total ~€173).
- **Base deck**: the Final Fantasy Commander precon **Limit Break**. Land count drops 37 → 36.

## Lore

The mercenary took the contract for the money. He always says that. But the buster sword was never the point — it was the *habit* of picking things up, carrying them, handing them to whoever needed them next. A whole squad armed out of one man's pockets.

*The reactor doesn't blow itself.*

## Decklist (100 cards)

### Commander (1)
- **Cloud, Ex-SOLDIER** — {2}{R}{G}{W} 4/4 with haste. Attaches an Equipment to itself on entry, and **whenever it attacks, draws a card for each equipped attacking creature you control** — then makes two Treasures if Cloud's power is 7 or greater. **The draw triggers on declare attackers and counts creatures, not damage**, so blockers and evasion are irrelevant to it; what matters is how many of your attackers are carrying something.

### Creatures (27)
- **Akiri, Fearless Voyager** *(new)* — Draws **one card per attack** (not per creature — she does not scale with Cloud), and `{W}` unattaches an Equipment to tap a creature and make it indestructible. The second ability is the real reason she's here: a one-mana answer to targeted removal and a combat trick.
- **Ardenn, Intrepid Archaeologist** *(new)* — At combat, attaches any number of your Auras and Equipment to **target permanent or player — a single target**. Ardenn *stacks*, it does not spread. It is the one-card route to power 7 (dump everything onto Cloud) and the rescue button when a carrier is about to die.
- **Auriok Steelshaper** *(new)* — **All equip costs you pay cost {1} less**, permanently, for two mana. It is also a Soldier, so SOLDIER Military Program counters it up.
- **Barret Wallace** — 4/4 reach that on attack deals damage equal to the number of equipped creatures you control **directly to the defending player**. Scales with the exact same number Cloud draws off, and ignores blockers entirely.
- **Barret, Avalanche Leader** — A 2/2 Rebel token **whenever an Equipment you control enters**, plus a free attach to a Rebel each combat. Bodies and attachments in one card.
- **Bastion Protector** — Cloud gets **+2/+2 and indestructible**. The +2 power is a third of the way to the 7-power check on its own.
- **Brass Squire** *(new)* — `{T}`: attach target Equipment to **target creature** — free, repeatable, and **not sorcery-restricted**, so it moves Equipment at instant speed on any turn. The genuine spread engine, and a 20th artifact for metalcraft.
- **Bronze Guardian** — Double strike, ward {2}, power equal to your artifact count (usually 16–19), and **every other artifact you control gains ward {2}** — a blanket tax on removal aimed at your Equipment suite. The most underrated card in the precon.
- **Bruenor Battlehammer** *(new)* — **First equip each turn costs {0}**, and every creature gets **+2/+0 per Equipment attached to it**. Four equipped attackers is +8 power spread across the board.
- **Bugenhagen, Wise Elder** — Reach, taps for any colour, and draws each upkeep you control a 7-power creature.
- **Cid, Freeflier Pilot** — Equipment spells cost {1} less, flies on your turn, and rebuys Equipment from the graveyard.
- **Hellkite Tyrant** — 6/5 flying trample; steals **all** artifacts from a player it connects with, which in an Equipment format converts opposing artifacts into more pieces for your spread plan. It also carries a genuine alt-win: **"at the beginning of your upkeep, if you control twenty or more artifacts, you win the game."** The deck runs **21 artifact cards**, and Cloud's Treasures, Professional Face-Breaker's Treasures and Bloodforged Battle-Axe's self-copying all add more — a real, non-combo line in a long game, though it needs 20 of them on the battlefield at once.
- **Leonin Shikari** *(new)* — **Equip at instant speed, on any turn.** Move Equipment after blockers are declared, or in response to removal.
- **Moraug, Fury of Akoum** *(new)* — 6/6 for {4}{R}{R}. Every creature gets **+1/+0 for each time it has attacked this turn**, and a land entering **during your main phase** gives an additional combat with everything untapped. **Note the restriction: it must be your main phase**, so your ordinary land drop and Cultivate/Nature's Lore/Three Visits trigger it, but Sword of the Animist's attack-triggered land does **not**.
- **Nahiri, Forged in Fury** *(new)* — Affinity for Equipment, so she routinely costs {R}{W}–{2}{R}{W}. Every equipped attacker exiles a card you may play this turn, **and Equipment cast this way is free**.
- **Professional Face-Breaker** — Menace; a Treasure whenever your creatures connect, and Treasures convert into impulse draw.
- **Professor Hojo** — The first activated ability each turn that targets your creature costs {2} less — that's an equip — **and you draw when your creatures are targeted by an activated ability**, once per turn. A two-mana equip discount that cantrips.
- **Puresteel Paladin** — Draws on every Equipment entering, and with three artifacts **all your Equipment have equip {0}**. This is the single best spread enabler in the deck; metalcraft is trivially on with 19 artifacts. **Still sorcery speed** unless Leonin Shikari or Forge Anew is out.
- **Red XIII, Proud Warrior** — Vigilance and trample for **all** your modified creatures, and returns an Equipment from the graveyard on entry.
- **Reyav, Master Smith** *(new)* — Two mana: **every equipped creature that attacks gains double strike.** Doubles the damage of the exact board Cloud is drawing off, and doubles Bloodforged Battle-Axe's copy trigger.
- **Sephiroth, Fallen Hero** — 7/5 that on attack sets **every modified creature you control to base 7/5** — a mass power-7 switch that turns on Cloud's Treasures, Tifa, Vincent and Bugenhagen at once. Recurs itself from the graveyard for {3} and a modified creature.
- **Sram, Senior Edificer** *(new)* — Draws on **every** Equipment spell cast. With 16 Equipment it is a two-mana engine.
- **Stoneforge Mystic** *(new)* — Tutors any Equipment, then cheats it onto the battlefield for {1}{W} — the way you deploy Kaldra Compleat on turn three.
- **Stonehewer Giant** *(new)* — `{1}{W}`, `{T}`: search out an Equipment, put it onto the battlefield **and attach it**. **The ability has no sorcery restriction — it works at instant speed**, and because it puts the Equipment *onto the battlefield*, it re-triggers Hammer of Nazahn and Sigarda's Aid for a **second** free attach on a different creature. The best mana sink in the deck.
- **Tifa, Martial Artist** — Melee, and whenever your creatures with **power 7 or greater** connect, untap everything and take an **additional combat phase**. The payoff the power-7 theme is built around.
- **Vincent, Vengeful Atoner** — Menace, grows on every connection, and at power 7 mirrors its damage onto every other opponent.
- **Yuffie, Materia Hunter** — Ninjutsu; steals a noncreature artifact and attaches an Equipment to herself on entry.

### Enchantments (4)
- **Forge Anew** *(new)* — Returns an Equipment from the graveyard on entry, lets you **equip at instant speed during your turn**, and makes the **first equip each turn free**. Three relevant abilities in one three-mana card. *(Instant-speed clause is "during your turn" only — no end-step tricks on an opponent's turn.)*
- **Garruk's Uprising** *(new)* — **Trample for every creature you control**, which is what stops a 17/17 Colossus Hammer carrier being chump-blocked. The draw clause is better than it looks: **ten creatures here have printed power 4 or greater** (Cloud, Barret Wallace, Barret Avalanche Leader, Bruenor, Hellkite Tyrant, Moraug, Nahiri Forged in Fury, Sephiroth, Stonehewer Giant, Tifa), plus Bronze Guardian, whose power is your artifact count. **Living-weapon Germs do not count** — they enter as 0/0 and the Equipment attaches afterwards.
- **SOLDIER Military Program** — With a commander out, **both** modes each combat: a 1/1 Soldier token *and* a +1/+1 counter on each of up to two Soldiers. Cloud is a Soldier, so this is **+1/+1 on Cloud every turn** — 4/4 to 7/7 in three turns with no Equipment at all, and those counters are "modifications" for Red XIII and Sephiroth. The tokens are **summoning-sick**, so they are next-turn attackers and free Skullclamp fodder, not same-turn bodies.
- **Sigarda's Aid** *(new)* — Equipment gains **flash**, and every Equipment attaches **free on entry**. Flash plus free attach means you deploy at end of turn and untap into a full attack.

- **Conqueror's Flail** — +3/+3 here, and **opponents can't cast spells during your turn**. Protects the whole alpha strike from instant-speed answers.
- **Embercleave** *(new)* — Flash, costs {1} less per attacking creature (routinely 2–3 mana), attaches free, grants **double strike and trample**. **Flashing it in after blockers is too late for Cloud's Treasure check**, which resolves during declare attackers.
- **Hammer of Nazahn** *(new)* — **Every Equipment you control attaches free as it enters**, and the equipped creature is **indestructible**. The backbone of the spread plan and the replacement for Darksteel Plate.
- **Hero's Heirloom** — +2/+1, and trample plus haste on legendary creatures.
- **Kaldra Compleat** *(new)* — Living weapon: brings its own 5/5 **first strike, trample, indestructible, haste** Germ that is itself an equipped attacker, and exiles any creature it damages. Seven mana is fine because Stoneforge Mystic, Stonehewer Giant and Nahiri's `−2` all cheat it in.
- **Mask of Memory** — Draw two, discard one, on every connection.
- **Maul of the Skyclaves** *(new)* — Attaches free on entry for +2/+2, **flying and first strike** — real evasion. **Equip is {2}{W}{W}**, so without Puresteel metalcraft the ETB attach is effectively one-shot.

## Key Synergies

- **Hammer of Nazahn / Sigarda's Aid + any Equipment = a free attach on a different creature.** This is the engine. The precon's problem was that every Equipment cost mana *twice* — once to cast, once to equip — so it could only ever arm one or two creatures a turn, and Cloud's per-creature draw stayed at one or two. With the free-attach package, each Equipment that resolves arms a **new** body for zero mana.
- **Reyav, Master Smith + Bloodforged Battle-Axe.** Double strike means two "deals combat damage to a player" triggers, so **two token Axes per combat** — and each Axe *entering* re-triggers Hammer of Nazahn and Sigarda's Aid for another free attach. The board arms itself.
- **Puresteel Paladin + three artifacts = equip {0} on everything.** The cheapest possible way to redistribute the whole suite before an attack. Sorcery speed unless Leonin Shikari or Forge Anew is out.
- **Colossus Hammer or Wrecking Ball Arm = instant power 7.** One card turns on Cloud's two Treasures, Tifa's untap-and-extra-combat, Vincent's multiplayer damage and Bugenhagen's draw simultaneously. Sephiroth does it for the **whole modified board** at once.
- **Nahiri, the Lithomancer's `+2` is a new equipped attacker every single turn** — token and attachment in one activation, i.e. +1 card off Cloud each turn, compounding.
- **Moraug + a held land drop.** Play your land in the **precombat** main phase and you get an extra combat before your normal one, with everything untapped and +1/+0 per prior attack. Note there is **no main phase between those combats**, so re-equipping in that window needs Leonin Shikari or Forge Anew.
- **Bronze Guardian wards the whole suite.** Every other artifact you control gets ward {2}, which is a blanket tax on the targeted removal that would otherwise pick your Equipment apart one at a time.

## Short Mulligan and Play Notes

- **Keep hands with a free-attach effect or an equip-cost reducer.** Hammer of Nazahn, Sigarda's Aid, Puresteel Paladin, Bruenor, Forge Anew, Auriok Steelshaper, Brass Squire. Without one, you are playing the stock precon and paying for every Equipment twice.
- **Early (1–3)**: Sol Ring or a two-mana rock into Sram/Puresteel/Sigarda's Aid. Steelshaper's Gift on turn one finds whatever the hand is missing.
- **Mid (4–6)**: Deploy Cloud, then **spread before you attack, not after**. The draw counts equipped attackers at declare attackers.
- **Late (7+)**: Sephiroth's attack trigger or a Colossus Hammer sets up the power-7 turn; Tifa converts it into a second combat.
- **Cloud's ETB targets the Equipment, not the creature** — and it can pull a piece off a creature that already has one, lowering your count. Attach something **unattached**.
- **The Treasure check resolves during declare attackers.** Anything that raises Cloud's power after blockers — flashing in Embercleave, a late equip — is too late for the Treasures, though still fine for damage.
- **Living weapon ordering matters.** Kaldra Compleat or Nettlecyst entering triggers living weapon *and* Hammer of Nazahn *and* Sigarda's Aid off the same event. If an attach resolves before living weapon, the Equipment snaps onto the fresh 0/0 Germ; and **moving Kaldra or Nettlecyst off its Germ later kills the Germ**. Order deliberately, or decline the optional attach.
- **Layers on the power-7 checks**: Wrecking Ball Arm (base 7/7) and Sephiroth's trigger (base 7/5) are both layer 7b, so the **later timestamp wins**. Colossus Hammer's +10/+10 is layer 7c and applies **after** either, so Wrecking Ball Arm plus Colossus Hammer is 17/17 in any order.
- **Hold your land drop for the precombat main phase once Moraug is out** — that is what buys the extra combat.
- **Living-weapon Germs don't draw off Garruk's Uprising.** The 0/0 token is created *first* and the Equipment attaches after, so it never enters with power 4.
- **Skullclamp is an engine here, not filler.** Nahiri's `+2` and SOLDIER Military Program each make a 1/1 every turn; clamping one kills it and draws two.
- **Five pain sources, effectively no lifegain.** Battlefield Forge, Brushland, Karplusan Forest, Spire of Industry and Talisman of Conviction all charge life, and Sacred Foundry costs 2 on entry. In a long pod game that is 8–12 life with nothing gaining it back.
- **Colossus Hammer removes flying.** Never on Hellkite Tyrant or Cid.

## Analysis

**Strengths**
- **The free-attach package is the whole upgrade.** Eight effects attach Equipment for zero mana (Hammer of Nazahn, Sigarda's Aid, Cloud, Embercleave, Nettlecyst, Stonehewer Giant, Barret Avalanche Leader, Nahiri's `+2`), plus three free-equip effects and instant-speed equip from Leonin Shikari, Forge Anew and Brass Squire. Cloud's draw goes from one or two cards a turn to five or more.
- Four tutors (Stoneforge Mystic, Steelshaper's Gift, Stonehewer Giant, Nahiri's `−2`) make a seven-mana Kaldra Compleat a turn-three play.
- Eleven draw sources, most of them attached to bodies you were casting anyway.
- Bronze Guardian's board-wide ward {2} plus Hammer of Nazahn's indestructible makes the Equipment suite genuinely hard to dismantle piecemeal.
- Mana rebalanced to actual pip demand rather than the precon's even split.

**Weaknesses**
- **Zero sweepers.** Austere Command, Vanquish the Horde and Ultimate Magic: Meteor were all cut as anti-synergy and nothing replaced them. Cloud's Limit Break on Omnislash is a real asymmetric answer to a board that has just attacked, but **a wide board that resolves and holds still beats this deck**. That is a conscious trade, not an oversight.
- **Edicts and exile are uncovered.** Hexproof, ward and indestructible do nothing against a sacrifice effect or a Farewell. Clever Concealment's phase-out is the only genuine answer to an untargeted wrath.
- **The deck is white-primary off a three-colour base.** Five `{W}{W}` costs against ~21 white sources is adequate rather than comfortable, and Maul of the Skyclaves' `{2}{W}{W}` equip is realistically a one-shot without metalcraft.
- **Thin evasion.** Flying is Hellkite Tyrant, Cid (on your turn only) and whatever carries Maul of the Skyclaves; everything else relies on trample from Garruk's Uprising, Red XIII, Kaldra and Embercleave. Against a wide board of small blockers that is usually enough; against a single large flier it is not.
- **It is a creature-and-artifact deck with no counterspells and no graveyard hate.** An instant-speed combo cannot be interacted with.

**Power level**: **Bracket 3, upper end.** It deploys a threatening board by turn four and draws four to six cards a turn once assembled, but it runs no fast mana beyond Sol Ring, no tutors outside the Equipment package, and no infinite combo.

## Next Steps (Optional Suggestions)

- **Considered and rejected**: *Guardian Project* (€16) — 18 legends means it draws far less than it looks like it does. *Balan, Wandering Knight* and *Armory Automaton* — both attach everything to themselves, which is the opposite of what Cloud wants. *Breath of Fury* (€9) — that is the Bracket 4+ line. *Ancient Tomb* (€105), *Esper Sentinel* (€40), *Smothering Tithe* (€37), *The Great Henge* (€50) — wrong price-to-gain at this power level.
- **Sword of Vengeance** (€0.42) — +2/+0, first strike, vigilance, trample **and haste**, which would turn Nahiri's and the Program's fresh tokens into same-turn attackers. It was proposed as the cut for Hellkite Tyrant; **Hellkite stayed** for its 20-artifact alt-win and its steal clause. Note the haste gap is real: **Hero's Heirloom grants trample and haste to *legendary* creatures only**, so the Kor Soldier and Soldier tokens, both Germs, Brass Squire, Auriok Steelshaper, Puresteel Paladin, Stoneforge Mystic, Bronze Guardian and Leonin Shikari get nothing from it. Slayers' Stronghold covers exactly one creature per turn. Sword of Vengeance is the cleanest fix if fresh tokens sitting idle bothers you.
- **If you lift the budget**: *Halvar, God of Battle* (€20) gives **every** equipped creature double strike and moves a piece each combat — the strongest card not in this list. *Shadowspear* (€25) adds trample, lifelink and hexproof-stripping. *Umezawa's Jitte* (€7) if your meta is creature-dense.
- **A fourth mana rock** (Mind Stone ~€0.30, Fellwar Stone ~€1) is defensible over **Summoning Materia**, the softest of the 16 Equipment.
- **Open the Armory** (€2.31) is a fifth Equipment tutor if you want more consistency.
