# Archetype: Tokens

Go-wide decks that flood the board with creature tokens and convert them into damage, cards, or sacrifice fodder. Strongest in White and Green; Red supplies the damage payoffs. Price legend: see [`../README`](../README.md). Figures without ✓ are **estimates**.

## Also Consider

- **Sacrifice outlets** turn tokens into value — see [`Aristocrats-Sacrifice.md`](Aristocrats-Sacrifice.md).
- **Aura Shards** (White-Green) makes every token entering destroy an artifact or enchantment.
- **Mentor of the Meek** (White) draws off small tokens for 1 mana each.
