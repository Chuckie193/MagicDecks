# Archetype: +1/+1 Counters

Decks that accumulate +1/+1 counters on creatures and multiply them. Primarily Green and White, with Blue for proliferate. Price legend: see [`../README`](../README.md). Figures without ✓ are **estimates**.

> Not what you were after? For counter**spells**, see [`Counterspells-Control.md`](Counterspells-Control.md).
