# Archetype: Lifegain

Gaining life as a resource, then converting it into cards, counters, or damage. White-Black is the core; Green adds bulk. Price legend: see [`../README`](../README.md). Figures without ✓ are **estimates**.

> **Build note:** lifegain alone doesn't win games — the payoffs below are what matter. A deck that gains 60 life and does nothing with it just loses slowly.
