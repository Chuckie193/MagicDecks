# Archetype: Aristocrats & Sacrifice

Decks that treat their own creatures as resources — sacrifice them for value and drain opponents through death triggers. Black-Red and White-Black are the natural homes. Price legend: see [`../README`](../README.md). Figures without ✓ are **estimates**.
