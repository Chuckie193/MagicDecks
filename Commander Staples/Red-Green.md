# Red-Green Staples

Gold (multicolor) staples and mana fixing specific to Red-Green decks. Doesn't repeat mono-Red or mono-Green staples — see [`General Staples`](General%20Staples.md) and consider a dedicated mono-color list if that's wanted later.

## Value & Payoffs
- **Xenagos, God of Revels** — doubles a creature's power and grants trample/haste on attack; premier combat payoff.
- **Ruric Thar, the Unbowed** — huge trampler that punishes opponents for casting noncreature spells.
- **Kessig Wolf Run** — colorless land closely associated with RG; pumps a creature and grants trample as an activated ability.

## Removal & Interaction
- **Gruul Charm** — modal instant: fight, sacrifice a permanent to cast an instant/sorcery for free, or a Naturalize-style effect.

## Mana Fixing
- Gruul Signet / Gruul Locket / Gruul Cluestone
- Stomping Ground, Rootbound Crag, Karplusan Forest, Gruul Guildgate
