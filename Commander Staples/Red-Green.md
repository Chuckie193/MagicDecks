## Card Advantage

Red-Green's weakest axis, and the one that most often decides whether a Gruul deck runs out of gas. There is very little in *gold* — the real answers are mono-green, so read [`Mono-Green`](Mono-Green.md) alongside this file: **Ohran Frostfang**, **Guardian Project**, **Beast Whisperer**, **Garruk's Uprising** and **Return of the Wildspeaker** all draw off things a creature deck is doing anyway.

| Card | Mana Cost | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Escape to the Wilds | 3 generic, Red, Green | €0.15 ✓ 💚 | Exile the top five and play them this turn — five cards deep for five mana, provided you can deploy them. |
| Bloodbraid Elf | 2 generic, Red, Green | €0.15 ✓ 💚 | Cascade: a hasty 3/2 plus a free spell costing three or less. |
| Rhythm of the Wild | 1 generic, Red, Green | €3.06 ✓ 💛 | Not draw, but it protects the gas you have: creature spells can't be countered, and each nontoken creature gets riot (haste, or a +1/+1 counter). |
