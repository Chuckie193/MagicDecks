# General Staples

Broadly efficient Commander staples that slot into almost any deck. Most are colorless and go in literally anything; a few are extremely efficient mono-colored auto-includes that are worth knowing regardless of which deck you're building, as long as the color is available.

See the folder [`README`](README.md) for caveats — this is a reference list, not a check against what's owned.

## Ramp & Mana Fixing
- **Sol Ring** — 1 mana, taps for 2 colorless. The single most efficient ramp piece in the format.
- **Arcane Signet** — 1 mana rock that taps for any color in your commander's color identity.
- **Fellwar Stone** — 2 mana rock that taps for any color an opponent's land can produce.
- **Thought Vessel** — 2 mana rock, also removes your maximum hand size.
- **Solemn Simulacrum** — fetches a basic land on ETB, draws a card on death.

## Universal Lands
- **Command Tower** — taps for any color in your commander's color identity.
- **Fabled Passage** — fetches a basic (untapped if you control 4+ lands), thins the deck.
- **Terramorphic Expanse** — basic fetch land, fixes colors.
- **Buried Ruin** — recurs any colorless artifact from the graveyard to hand.

## Protection
- **Lightning Greaves** — free equip; grants haste and shroud.
- **Swiftfoot Boots** — free equip; grants haste and hexproof.

## Efficient Mono-Colored Removal & Interaction
- **Swords to Plowshares** (W) — 1 mana, exiles any creature.
- **Abrade** (R) — 2 mana modal removal: 3 damage to a creature, or destroy an artifact.
- **Blasphemous Act** (R) — cheap board wipe, often castable for 1 mana.
- **Chaos Warp** (R) — universal removal; hits any permanent, including lands and planeswalkers.
- **Counterspell** (U) — the classic 2-mana hard counter.
- **Swan Song** (U) — 1-mana counter for instants/sorceries/enchantments (and a few other things), with a minor downside.
