# White-Red Staples

Gold (multicolor) staples and mana fixing specific to White-Red decks. Doesn't repeat mono-White or mono-Red staples — see [`General Staples`](General%20Staples.md) and consider a dedicated mono-color list if that's wanted later.

## Removal & Interaction
- **Wear // Tear** — split card: destroy an artifact, or destroy an enchantment, either half castable alone.
- **Boros Charm** — modal instant: 4 damage to a player, indestructible for your team, or double strike.

## Value & Payoffs
- **Assemble the Legion** — snowballing token engine that wins games left unchecked.
- **Aurelia, the Warleader** — extra combat + first strike/haste, powerful combat payoff.
- **Combat Celebrant** — extra combat on attack, aggressive value.
- **Sunforger** — colorless equipment built for WR decks; tutors and flashes back cheap instants when it connects.

## Mana Fixing
- Boros Signet / Boros Locket / Boros Cluestone
- Sacred Foundry, Clifftop Retreat, Battlefield Forge, Boros Garrison, Boros Guildgate
