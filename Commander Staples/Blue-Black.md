# Blue-Black Staples

Gold (multicolor) staples and mana fixing specific to Blue-Black decks. Doesn't repeat mono-Blue or mono-Black staples — see [`General Staples`](General%20Staples.md) and consider a dedicated mono-color list if that's wanted later.

## Value & Card Advantage
- **Baleful Strix** — 1-mana flying deathtouch blocker that draws a card on ETB; one of the most efficient value creatures in the format.
- **Notion Thief** — turns opponents' card-draw effects into your own draws; brutal alongside wheel effects.
- **Thief of Sanity** — steals and exiles cards from an opponent's library on combat damage, castable from exile.

## Removal & Interaction
- **Dimir Charm** — modal instant: bounce a creature, destroy an artifact, or make a player discard.

## Mana Fixing
- Dimir Signet / Dimir Locket / Dimir Cluestone
- Watery Grave, Sunken Hollow, Underground River, Dimir Guildgate
