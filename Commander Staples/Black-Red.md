# Black-Red Staples

Gold (multicolor) staples and mana fixing specific to Black-Red decks. Doesn't repeat mono-Black or mono-Red staples — see [`General Staples`](General%20Staples.md) and consider a dedicated mono-color list if that's wanted later.

## Removal & Interaction
- **Terminate** — 2-mana instant, destroys any creature. One of the most efficient removal spells in the format.
- **Bedevil** — destroys a creature, artifact, or planeswalker.
- **Angrath's Rampage** — modal removal: destroy a creature, a planeswalker, or exile an artifact.
- **Kolaghan's Command** — modal value spell: choose 2 of deal 2 damage, destroy an artifact, return a creature from graveyard to hand, or force a discard.

## Value & Payoffs
- **Rakdos, Lord of Riots** — reduces the cost of your creatures based on damage dealt this turn; aggressive ramp payoff.

## Mana Fixing
- Rakdos Signet / Rakdos Locket / Rakdos Cluestone
- Blood Crypt, Dragonskull Summit, Bloodfell Caves, Rakdos Guildgate
