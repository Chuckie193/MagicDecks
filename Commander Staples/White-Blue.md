# White-Blue Staples

Gold (multicolor) staples and mana fixing specific to White-Blue decks. Doesn't repeat mono-White or mono-Blue staples — see [`General Staples`](General%20Staples.md) and consider a dedicated mono-color list if that's wanted later.

## Removal & Control
- **Supreme Verdict** — 4-mana board wipe that can't be countered.
- **Austere Command** — modal 6-mana wrath; choose 2 of 4 modes (creatures, small creatures, enchantments, artifacts).
- **Detention Sphere** — exiles a nonland permanent and anything sharing its name.
- **Render Silent** — counters every spell a chosen opponent controls and silences them for the turn.
- **Dovin's Veto** — uncounterable counterspell or removal, split card.
- **Fractured Identity** — exile a permanent, then get a copy of it yourself.

## Card Advantage & Value
- **Absorb** — counter a spell and draw a card.
- **Sphinx's Revelation** — X-spell that draws cards and gains that much life.
- **Grand Abolisher** — protects your combo/setup turns from interaction.
- **Approach of the Second Sun** — alternate win condition, doubles as card draw the first time it's cast.

## Mana Fixing
- Azorius Signet / Azorius Locket / Azorius Cluestone
- Hallowed Fountain, Prairie Stream, Tranquil Cove, Azorius Guildgate
