# White-Black Staples

Gold (multicolor) staples and mana fixing specific to White-Black decks. Doesn't repeat mono-White or mono-Black staples — see [`General Staples`](General%20Staples.md) and consider a dedicated mono-color list if that's wanted later.

## Removal
- **Vindicate** — 4 mana, destroys any permanent.
- **Anguished Unmaking** — exiles any nonland permanent, minor life-loss downside.
- **Utter End** — instant-speed exile removal for any nonland permanent.
- **Vona's Hunger** — sacrifices creatures to destroy a permanent per creature sacrificed.

## Card Advantage & Recursion
- **Debtors' Knell** — reanimates a creature from any graveyard at the start of each of your end steps.
- **Athreos, Shroud-Veiled** — recurring death-trigger engine / pseudo-removal via devotion.
- **Debt to the Deathless** — X-spell finisher that drains life equal to X plus creature count.

## Mana Fixing
- Orzhov Signet / Orzhov Locket / Orzhov Cluestone
- Godless Shrine, Isolated Chapel, Concealed Courtyard, Caves of Koilos, Orzhov Guildgate
