# Blue-Green Staples

Gold (multicolor) staples and mana fixing specific to Blue-Green decks. Doesn't repeat mono-Blue or mono-Green staples — see [`General Staples`](General%20Staples.md) and consider a dedicated mono-color list if that's wanted later.

## Value & Engines
- **Rashmi, Eternities Crafter** — draws or ramps every time you cast a spell of higher mana value than your last.
- **Edric, Spymaster of Trest** — draws a card for every unblocked attacker across the table, huge card advantage.
- **Kinnan, Bonder's Prodigy** — doubles mana-dork output and cheats big creatures/artifacts into play.

## Removal & Interaction
- **Trygon Predator** — grows and can be sacrificed to destroy an artifact or enchantment; strong artifact/enchantment hate.

## Mana Fixing
- Simic Signet / Simic Locket / Simic Cluestone
- Breeding Pool, Hinterland Harbor, Yavimaya Coast, Simic Guildgate
