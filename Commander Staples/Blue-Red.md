# Blue-Red Staples

Gold (multicolor) staples and mana fixing specific to Blue-Red decks. Doesn't repeat mono-Blue or mono-Red staples — see [`General Staples`](General%20Staples.md) and consider a dedicated mono-color list if that's wanted later.

## Value & Engines
- **Niv-Mizzet, Parun** — draws a card and pings for every noncreature spell cast; iconic spellslinger payoff.
- **Jhoira, Weatherlight Captain** — draws a card whenever a historic (legendary/artifact/Saga) spell with cost 3+ is cast.
- **Reckless Fireweaver** — cheap spellslinger payoff, pings on every noncreature spell.

## Removal & Interaction
- **Izzet Charm** — modal instant: counter an activated ability, deal 2 damage to a creature, or draw a card and discard a card.

## Mana Fixing
- Izzet Signet / Izzet Locket / Izzet Cluestone
- Steam Vents, Sulfur Falls, Spirebluff Canal, Izzet Guildgate
