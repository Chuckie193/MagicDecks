# White-Green Staples

Gold (multicolor) staples and mana fixing specific to White-Green decks. Doesn't repeat mono-White or mono-Green staples — see [`General Staples`](General%20Staples.md) and consider a dedicated mono-color list if that's wanted later.

## Value & Engines
- **Trostani, Selesnya's Voice** — lifegain and token-doubling payoff.
- **Karametra, God of Harvests** — ramps off creature landfall, huge devotion body.
- **Aura Shards** — punishes opponents' artifacts/enchantments while your creatures fly around eating them.
- **Wilt-Leaf Liege** — anthem plus protection from black and red, efficient body.

## Removal & Interaction
- **Selesnya Charm** — modal instant: fight, exile a graveyard, or make a 3/3 token.

## Mana Fixing
- Selesnya Signet / Selesnya Locket / Selesnya Cluestone
- Temple Garden, Canopy Vista, Brushland, Selesnya Guildgate
